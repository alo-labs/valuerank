# Codex allowance tracking and crowdsourced alternatives

**Research date:** 2026-10-05 (Australia/Sydney). **Cutoff for empirical reports:** published 2026-09-05 or later.

## Finding

I found no numeric Codex allowance series on the [TiboTattle homepage](https://tibotattle.com/) in the Codex built-in `web__run` page capture opened on October 5, 2026. The page showed “Latest community evidence — Checking daily activity,” then “Checking allowance estimates” and “Checking for published allowance estimates…” in the “Pro 20x-equivalent allowance” panel. The separate daily-activity panel also said “Checking daily series” and “Checking for published daily activity…”. No numeric community allowance series or community sample count was visible. The homepage has a separate interactive demo and labels its figures illustrative, not live account data; those figures are excluded. An earlier unverified availability label has been removed. The observed checking message is a loading state, not proof that no estimate exists. I therefore make no Codex allowance amount, percentile, plan comparison, or other empirical numerical claim. I do not substitute values from Reddit, repository pages, announcements, search snippets, product demonstrations, or any other source. See [the capture-specific citations](citations.md#homepage-observation) for the exact page sections and lines.

### Data provenance and limits

The homepage presents the public chart as a community estimate of API-price value for a Pro 20x-equivalent week. TiboTattle's [documentation](https://tibotattle.com/docs) and [privacy overview](https://tibotattle.com/privacy) say eligible contributions from signed-in or accountless installations may enter the public sample. Current disclosures say fresh Electron installs enable sharing automatically, with a persistent opt-out; earlier native Mac releases retain a review-and-approve flow. The privacy page describes a contribution source as an installation/account track, not a unique person or verified provider account; independently created installations can overlap, and an eligible daily estimate may be based on a single source. The publisher says contributions exclude prompts and responses. These are publisher descriptions, not an independent audit of submissions or representativeness. The homepage remains the sole eligible source for any numeric Codex crowd allowance value in this report.

The [TiboTattle documentation](https://tibotattle.com/docs) says the public chart has aggregate, by-plan, and by-model views, and that API-price equivalents are estimates, not the underlying ChatGPT subscription's actual value or bill. Any future reading should preserve the displayed sample and definitions and should not be described as an official allowance or a population-representative measure.

### Signal classes

- **Provider/account signal:** OpenAI’s current [Codex plan help page](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11369540-using-codex-with-your-chatgpt-plan) points users to their own Codex usage dashboard or `/status` for their limits and reset information. It says usage limits vary by plan and does not publish one universal empirical allowance figure. This is policy/navigation context, not a community measurement.
- **Crowdsourced aggregate:** TiboTattle’s homepage is the only allowed source for any Codex aggregate allowance values in this work. No usable series or sample information was available at retrieval.
- **Local token/cost estimate:** ccusage, AIUsage, SessionWatcher, OpenUsage, and CodexBar describe local parsing or pricing for at least some outputs. Those numbers measure observed logs and/or apply chosen public API prices; they are not interchangeable with an account quota or actual subscription value.
