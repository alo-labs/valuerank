## Cycle 1 — Skeptic review

**Steelman.** The homepage is the user's mandated sole source for Codex crowd numbers. A timestamped capture with no numeric result is a valid negative retrieval result when carefully bounded to the page state.

**Contradiction hunt.** Earlier package language risked implying that a loading state meant no estimate existed. It also contained outdated workflow wording about general SB access. The current routed session successfully read the skill and phase contracts using repo=silver-bullet; the automated /sb:deep-research command was not invoked.

**Missing stakeholder.** TiboTattle users may interpret the chart label as an actual plan quota, and people with different plan tiers may be obscured by an aggregate.

**Failure mode.** A future rendering or delayed script load could make the current observation stale or incomplete.

**Severity and loopback.** High. Revised the conclusion to “no numeric value visible in this capture,” excluded the demo, and recorded the exact workflow invocation boundary. Loopback: refine report and manifest.

## Cycle 2 — Methodology and measurement review

**Steelman.** The report distinguishes community aggregation from personal account signals and local token/cost processing, and publisher privacy documents expose sample provenance limits.

**Contradiction hunt.** The phrase “Pro 20x-equivalent” could be misread as the respondent's actual plan tier. Discovery dates could be mistaken for empirical allowance dates. No user-reported numeric allowance row is actually present.

**Missing stakeholder.** A subscription-value analyst needs reproducible units, aggregation period, source-tier, and sample count before comparing plans.

**Failure mode.** Mixing API-price estimates, local logs, provider quotas, or demo figures would create a false comparable allowance number.

**Severity and loopback.** High. Added the exact plan-tier rule, clarified that the chart label is a comparison basis, kept the one-month cutoff explicit, and recorded zero eligible user datapoints. Loopback: refine scope, triangulation, and report.

## Cycle 3 — Red-team scope and attribution review

**Steelman.** A broad app-discovery appendix is useful if each capability is attributed to the publisher and the set is labeled as bounded rather than exhaustive.

**Contradiction hunt.** Loading/placeholder pages and search artifacts cannot support absence claims. First-party marketing pages may omit competitors or limitations; store privacy disclosures may be publisher-provided and unverified.

**Missing stakeholder.** Researchers needing all platform-specific or emerging trackers may not be represented by this discovery set.

**Failure mode.** Treating a reviewed-source search as an exhaustive market census, or a lack of described aggregation as proof that no product has one.

**Severity and loopback.** Medium. Marked the product list as bounded discovery, limited “no public aggregate found” language to reviewed sources, classified placeholders as coverage-only, and attributed product claims. Added a crosswalk from the preserved legacy evidence IDs to the hashed SB ledger IDs. Loopback: refine report and validation wording.

## Stop decision

Three cycles completed. No unresolved high-severity defect remains in the bounded conclusion. Remaining medium limitations are documented: the homepage can change, publisher claims are not independently tested, and product discovery is not exhaustive. Final report retains a retrieval-time statement, not a timeless absence claim.
