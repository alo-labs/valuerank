# Scope

## Research question

What Codex allowance observations did TiboTattle publish on its homepage during 2026-09-05 through 2026-10-05, and what other apps publish or track comparable usage information?

## In scope

- A retrieval-time check of the TiboTattle homepage for numerical Codex crowdsourced allowance information.
- Discovery of other apps that monitor Codex or adjacent AI-provider plan usage, with the publisher-described data source and measurement type.
- Source and methodology limits that affect interpretation of any community aggregate.
- Strict separation of empirical crowd observations, provider/account signals, local token or cost estimates, and illustrative demonstrations.

## Out of scope

- Estimating Codex allowances from Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, provider claims, app demos, local logs, or search snippets.
- Treating OpenAI's account-specific dashboard guidance as a community or population measurement.
- A full app-store or worldwide product census.
- Collecting private account data or inferring a user's plan from usage.

## Assumptions and decision rules

- Only numerical values displayed on the canonical TiboTattle homepage are admissible for Codex crowd allowance claims.
- Include empirical allowance reports only if dated 2026-09-05 or later.
- For every user-reported datapoint, preserve the exact plan tier; if the report omits it, record “not stated.” No user-reported allowance datapoints were included in this package.
- “Pro 20x-equivalent week” is the homepage chart's comparison basis, not an individual reporter's subscription tier.
- A page's loading/checking state supports only an observation about that retrieval; it cannot establish that a value was never published.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Report the homepage state without interpreting loading text as absence of all historical data.
2. Include no Codex numeric crowd value from any source other than the TiboTattle homepage.
3. Preserve all included evidence with stable IDs and claim links.
4. Identify comparable apps while labeling the review as bounded discovery.
5. State empirical date cutoff and exact tier rule.
6. Complete three critique cycles with explicit loopbacks and validation evidence.
