## Cross-source assessment

TiboTattle's documentation and privacy material supplies the publisher's definitions and sample caveats, but not a second independent numerical measurement. It says the chart is an API-price-equivalent estimate, and discloses installation/account-level contribution provenance, potential overlap across separate installs, and the possibility of a single-source daily estimate. These caveats prevent interpreting a displayed aggregate as representative of unique people or as an official plan allowance. The statements remain publisher disclosures, not independent audits.

The reviewed tracker sources converge on a useful distinction: account-specific live/endpoint reads and local session-log cost summaries are different products from a public crowd aggregate. This is a triangulated classification of published methods, not an independent product test. OpenAI's help page directs each user to account-specific limits and reset details; that guidance is not a population report.

## Contradictions and confidence

- No cross-source contradiction changes the homepage-only numeric rule.
- Search pages and the developer's Reddit post are discovery leads only; their allowance or usage quantities are excluded.
- Several CUStats pages exposed loading placeholders or headings without answer text, so those pages are coverage records rather than support.
- Confidence is high in the captured homepage state and in the separation of measurement classes; moderate in the bounded list of adjacent tools; low for claims about market completeness or representativeness.

## Source quality notes

Publisher pages are primary for what a product says it does, but have commercial and single-perspective risk. OpenAI help is primary for OpenAI's own account-navigation guidance. Search-result pages are portals and not evidence. The Reddit product-discovery post is secondary/community context and does not establish allowance facts.
