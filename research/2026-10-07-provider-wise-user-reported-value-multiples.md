## Calculation and evidence notes

- SemiAnalysis's Oct 6 charts already express API-equivalent capacity **per month**. Each new multiple is the chart's monthly dollar amount divided by the matched monthly subscription fee; no additional `52 ÷ 12` scaling is applied. This source is expressly selected by the user for Anthropic and Codex, superseding the earlier rows from individual Claude reports and Sep 28 TiboTattle aggregates. [SemiAnalysis source](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/anthropic-subscriptions-offer-5x)
- The SemiAnalysis estimates combine controlled account measurements with an agentic workload mix: 0.4% fresh input, 96.6% cached input, 2.6% cache writes, and 0.3% output (99.9% in total because of rounding). Token-rate estimates use meter intervals with approximately ±5% uncertainty. The public article and chart images support the quoted figures, but no public complete token ledger enables independent reproduction; the paid administrative dashboard was not accessed. The published monthly projections do not establish each subscriber's actual consumption or task output. [SemiAnalysis methodology](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/anthropic-subscriptions-offer-5x)
- Preserve the exact evaluated variants: Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Astra. Opus 5.5 estimates are not transferred to Opus 5; GPT-6.1 Sol estimates are not transferred to GPT-6 Sol. Fable's 50% weekly allocation leaves another 50% of the pool; its quoted capacity is not added to the full-pool Opus projection. [SemiAnalysis charts](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/anthropic-subscriptions-offer-5x)
- The Z.AI author's text says the graphic was generated from their usage and that summary statistics were based on billing-statement exports analyzed with Hermes/ChatGPT. The author reports 637.8M tokens, 44.5K plan credits, 9,047 calls, a 90.7% input-cache-hit rate, and about $102 of API usage. The graphic shows $102.17 and Aug 29–Sep 29. This remains a user-reported estimate, not an independently audited bill. Mixed GLM 5.3/Flash use makes the result plan-level only.
- The Kimi source reports K3-256, $7.20 at API prices, and 20% of a weekly meter in a five-hour session. Dividing `$7.20 ÷ 0.20` gives the user's approximately `$36/week`; annualizing and dividing by the user's `$39/month` gives approximately `4.0×`. The source's separate approximately 2× extrapolation for standard K3 is not assigned to the ranked `kimi-k3` model.
- OpenAI rows use SemiAnalysis's current post-Sep 29 schedule. Eligible legacy Pro $200 subscriptions retain prior capacity until Oct 29, so the current schedule does not describe those temporarily grandfathered accounts. The article's older $5,904/month GPT-6 Sol and $5,734/month GPT-6 Astra Pro $200 figures describe the pre-cut schedule and are historical, not the current rows' numerator. The Sep 28 TiboTattle aggregates are also historical evidence retained in the earlier research trail. [SemiAnalysis transition analysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/anthropic-subscriptions-offer-5x)
- The SuperGrok Heavy account experiment reports $124.85507688 of API-equivalent use across 17 runs. Its calibration uses $76.09728984 of deduplicated receipts while the meter rose from 56% to 75% between Sep 23 and Sep 25; the resulting approximately $400.5/week is a projection. The source labels confidence low: shared product pools, possible unlogged activity, interrupted runs, and calibration assumptions limit inference. The $300 fee is owner-reported. The primary page was inspected; its linked underlying audit files have not been independently reproduced. SuperGrok Plus remains excluded because the user's displayed session cost has not been established as API-list-price equivalent.
- Current-window Google/Gemini and Alibaba/Qwen community reports reviewed in the Ultradeep package do not provide qualifying personal subscription usage priced at API rates and matched to a tier. Meter-only reports, API-only workloads, unattributed token counts, and provider claims remain excluded. Search coverage is bounded; no zero-value finding is implied.
- Mistral is omitted because it is not featured in ValueRank. The previously removed 120× entry remains excluded.
