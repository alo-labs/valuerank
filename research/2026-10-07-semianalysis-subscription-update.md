## Measurement and inference boundaries

- SemiAnalysis reports repeated controlled requests, token-type isolation, provider-reported token counts and meter steps. It drops incomplete endpoint steps and reports rate estimates after its stated ±5% target is reached. This is a source-reported method, not independently rerun here.
- The source's agentic blend is 0.4% fresh input, 96.6% cached input, 2.6% cache writes and 0.3% output. Displayed rounded shares sum to 99.9%. Capacity depends on model, plan, workload and token pricing; these are not universal cash savings or observed full-month spending.
- Current Codex figures reflect the September 29 allocation change. The source says eligible pre-cut Pro 200 accounts keep older limits until October 29. Prior Pro 200 chart values ($5,734 Astra and $5,904 GPT-6 Sol) are historical, and the old Sol comparison changes model to GPT-6.1 Sol as well as allocation/pricing. Do not apply the full dollar decline solely to tokens or transfer current Sol data to GPT-6 Sol.
- Exact account experiment timestamps and public raw request ledgers are not supplied in the visible article. October 6 is the publication date; September supplies the workload blend. No precise experiment interval is invented.
- The headline's roughly fivefold Anthropic-versus-OpenAI advantage is a cross-provider comparison, not a 5× subscription/API value multiple. Each plan's actual multiple above comes from its monthly capacity divided by its fee.
- All source amounts are rounded chart labels. Additional decimals from division are arithmetic precision, not extra measurement precision.
