# DR-SCOPE — Scope

**Question.** Which other websites rank or compare AI models on *value* (quality against cost, including subscription-plan cost), as ValueRank does, and how do they differ from ValueRank in method, evidence, cost treatment and audience?

**Mode.** `deep` (research_type `default`): a market landscape that informs product direction, with conflicting vendor claims likely; not a tool-selection decision, so no need-profile interview or solution matrix is required.

**Date basis.** Host clock 2026-10-17T17:15Z (2026-10-18 local). Recency window for "current" claims: the last 18 months.

## What ValueRank does (the comparison baseline)

- Ranks 16 frontier models (from a 28-model roster reconciled against Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index versus total-cost frontier) on 16 dimensions: measured benchmark quality (AA components, DeepSWE v1.1, Bug Hunt Bench, LiveBench Instruction Following, Terminal-Bench 4.0, speed) plus a cost dimension.
- Two cost bases: API cost (AA total evaluation cost in USD) and Plan cost (that cost ÷ the highest eligible subscription Value Multiple ÷ AA Intelligence Index, i.e. USD per Index point).
- Exact-variant evidence policy: nothing transferred between model versions, efforts or harnesses; missing values stay missing and priorities renormalise; every value carries a source, date and evidence grade (measured, claim, estimate, assumption).
- Published as a static site with shareable URL state, model pages, plan evidence, changelog and downloads; refreshed per release from pinned snapshots.

## In scope

1. Public websites, leaderboards or indices that rank or compare LLMs/AI models and expose a **cost or price dimension** alongside quality (price-performance, cost per task, intelligence per dollar, value, efficiency frontiers).
2. Sites that compare **subscription plans** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor, etc.) by allowance, usage limits or value, including community trackers and analyst measurements.
3. Benchmark aggregators and independent evaluators whose published views include cost (even if value is not the primary framing), because they are ValueRank's nearest substitutes and its data sources.
4. For each: what it ranks, method and transparency, cost treatment (API, task-level, plan), evidence/provenance practice, refresh cadence, audience, business model, and the gap or overlap with ValueRank.

## Out of scope

- Pure capability leaderboards with no cost signal (noted only as context).
- Hardware/GPU cost benchmarks, inference-provider throughput comparisons, and enterprise TCO consultancies.
- Model routers and gateways as products (their public rankings pages are in scope only if they rank models on value).
- Paid analyst reports whose content cannot be read.

## Assumptions to validate

- A1: No other public site combines benchmark quality with subscription-plan cost at the model level.
- A2: Several sites offer API price-performance views (cost per task or intelligence per dollar) that overlap with ValueRank's API basis.
- A3: Plan-allowance comparisons exist mostly as blog posts, forum threads and trackers rather than maintained rankings.
- A4: Provenance practices (dated, exact-variant, graded evidence) are rare among comparison sites.

## Success criteria

- At least 25 graded sources across site pages, methodology pages, independent analyses and community discussions; at least 3 source types.
- Every core claim about a site's method or cost treatment is supported by that site's own page (primary) and, where it matters, one independent source.
- A landscape table of named sites with their value framing, cost treatment, evidence practice and overlap with ValueRank, plus a decision record on positioning gaps.
