Bug Hunt's leaf mixes a mean-based numerator with a max-based, cross-harness denominator, and diverges from the source's own scoring semantics. The numerator is the mean over the published runs for the exact model/effort (O163/E206), while the denominator P_max + U_max is the maximum of planted and unplanted counts taken separately over every run on the live board, any model or harness (O170). Three consequences: (a) models with many published runs have their numerators regressed toward their mean while models with one lucky run keep it — a systematic anti-correlation between run count and z; (b) P_max and U_max typically come from two different runs/harnesses, so the denominator is a composite nobody attained, and the draft's own fixture (57 and 65.3) shows the unplanted component already dominates; (c) the board's publisher states the score counts planted bugs only and that unplanted fixes "stay separate" — ValueRank's 1:1 sum of the two, at 8–12% of quality, is a rescoring of the source, not a reading of it. Why it hurts: the leaf pretends to the same 0–100 raw-magnitude status as FrontierCode or Terminal-Bench while its scale is neither frozen (like every other leaf's anchors) nor drawn from one population. The live/frozen question was owner-decided twice (O173, O190) — the new argument here is the mean/max asymmetry and the planted/unplanted 1:1 weighting against source semantics, not live-vs-frozen. Concrete change: normalise the numerator by a consistent statistic computed identically for every row (e.g., denominator = the largest per-run planted+unplanted total, not the sum of two separate maxima), and split the leaf's published form into planted-share (x/105, a frozen, source-true scale) plus an unplanted bonus term with an explicit frozen weight; publish the z under both normalisations as the registered stress.

Plan anchor extremes can be set by plan rows of one configuration/provider, so the frozen Plan exchange rate encodes a plan-tier price ladder rather than configuration diversity. Plan anchors are the measured plan rows of the E160 reference configurations (O157): c_L = cheapest measured plan row ÷ 2, c_H = dearest × 2. Since measured routes today exist only for Anthropic and OpenAI plans, the anchor range spans from a cheap tier (e.g., Claude Pro) to a premium tier (e.g., ChatGPT Pro 500) — plausibly rows of one or two configurations. The resulting exchange rate ("a cost doubling is worth 5.31 points" on the max-effort Plan basis) is then calibrated mostly to how one provider prices its tier ladder, not to the value differences among frontier configurations, and O48's one-row-per-plan rule multiplies this: 30 plan rows for 9 configurations (74 for 20 in AI Coding Daily) means the gate population, anchors and scenario shares all weight providers by how many plans they sell (the draft acknowledges this weighting only for the identification yield's configuration-balanced companion). Why it hurts: a headline "Plan value" score whose cost dimension is stretched across subscription tiers answers a different question than "which model is the best value on a plan," and tier-ladder shifts (OpenAI's 2026-10-29 doubling of Pro-tier limits, for instance) move the scale for everyone. Concrete change: add an anchor-panel diversity rule — at freeze, the two reference extremes must come from distinct configurations (distinct AA families), else publish a "single-family anchor" flag; and publish, beside the official Plan exchange rate, a configuration-balanced sensitivity (one representative plan row per configuration, reusing E245's representative-row notion, which extends the already-adopted E164/E249 calibration diagnostics rather than adding a new ranking).

2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised:

(Highest) Evidence-concentration metric for the cost/Plan leg. Mirror the quality side's source-share publication: per ranking, publish planEvidenceSources = rows and displayed-cost share by upstream observer class (controlled measurement — named observer / concordant reports / each owner-assumption kind / API fallback), plus a configuration-balanced variant, plus a warning flag at >50% single-observer share of measured rows.
