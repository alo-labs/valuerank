1. Findings by severity

Critical

None. The lifecycle, valuation gate, interval solver and label machinery are internally consistent with the owner constraints, and every failure mode I traced ends in a disclosed flag, an unresolved status or a held release rather than a published wrong claim.

High

H1 — Stale Plan evidence stays in the official ordinal ranking for up to ~120 days with unchanged numeric weight. E212's lifecycle keeps the score and route comparison "unchanged" through Possibly superseded (>30 d), Expired in grace (90–120 d), and only demotes past grace; the Known limitations section itself counts 21 of 30 and 60 of 74 plan rows resting on flagged or expired routes, plus 3 and 12 in acknowledged fault windows. The Plan ranking's objective is to tell a buyer which plan is better value now; one ordinal list that silently orders mostly-stale routes beside current ones (flags print, but the rank arithmetic treats them equally) can invert a real answer when only one of two close competitors' routes was refreshed. This does not repeat any rejected claim: I am not asking to remove rows, change scores, loosen O11/O13/O127, or exclude flagged routes. Recommendation: add a third published presentation, computed entirely from data the lifecycle already emits — the "current-evidence Plan view": restrict the ordinal comparison to rows whose selected route is in the Current stage, list the remaining rows separately with their stage, and print the Kendall τ_b between the two orders. Zero new evidence collection; it reuses E212's stage field. If the two orders agree, that is itself a published robustness result; if they diverge, readers see which headline positions are freshness-dependent.

H2 — (Owner decision challenge: O54 makes the max-effort ranking lead.) Per the draft's own Known limitations figures, the leading ranking covers 9 of 37 cohort configurations (24%), AI Coding Daily covers 20, and 12 configurations are ranked in neither. What is new relative to the ledger: O54 was recorded when coverage figures were different in character (pre-O132/O145, 27-then-37-row cohort); the v5 migration shrank the max-effort per-model benchmark coverage while the cohort grew, so the headline now rests on the smaller-coverage ranking by a wider margin than when the decision was made. Recommendation: keep O54's designated lead, but require the headline block to print the leading ranking's cohort coverage beside the τ_b companion (O172's common-leaf answer), and auto-promote the co-lead when one ranking's coverage exceeds the other's by a fixed margin (e.g. 2×). This is a presentation rule over existing outputs, not a rescoring; it keeps headline claims covering what most of the cohort's figures support.

Medium

M3 — "Dependence" (part 15) specifies positive/negative dependence stress branches without defining their joint laws. Where paired task/run observations are not public, the draft says "independent / positive / negative dependence stress branches are published" but never says what "positive" means operationally — comonotonic coupling of the marginals, a Gaussian copula at a fixed ρ, or Fréchet bounds — and each reviewer or build maintainer could legitimately choose differently, producing different stress numbers from the "same" public evidence. Recommendation: pin the convention in the spec: positive branch = comonotonic (sort both marginal quantile functions to the same uniforms), negative branch = countermonotonic, both well-defined for any marginals and reproducible from public data alone; state that these bracket any copula (they are the Fréchet–Hoeffding extremes), which makes the three named branches provably conservative. One sentence plus two fixtures suffices.

M4 — The E335/C_W price fixture overclaims. Part 15's release test says "doubling the price of a category present only in the measured consumption halves the Plan cost". Halving requires p·x to double, which holds only if that category is the sole priced category in x (or all other x categories have zero demand or zero price); as worded, an implementation that is wrong for multi-category consumption would still pass the fixture. Recommendation: reword to "doubling the price of the sole priced category in the measured consumption halves the Plan cost", and add a two-category companion fixture with a non-trivial demand split (which exercises the E335 ratio rather than degenerating to 1×/0.5×).

Low

L5 — O56's convergence criterion counts rounds as clean at medium-and-above only, and the ledger shows low findings are "considered" but carry no register. Over 67+ rounds this risks low-severity findings being re-raised repeatedly or silently dropped. Recommendation: a one-line low-findings log per release (finding id, disposition: accepted / deferred / declined-with-reason), so clean rounds stop re-litigating and the panel's history stays auditable; this is cheap because the review ledger already exists.

L6 — The E265 figure lint defines "today"-speech and number-lint rules by natural-language heuristics ("a sentence that speaks of today… in any sentence not dated before the release day"), which is brittle to wording drift and will let hand-typed figures through whenever phrasing is novel (the draft itself concedes "a stale figure found after this is a bug in the check"). Recommendation: require an explicit machine-readable marker (e.g. {#fig:ok hand …} exemption tag) on any sentence the author intends to exempt, inverting the default from "lint guesses" to "author opts out", at zero cost to the reader.
