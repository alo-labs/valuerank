## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

None.

**High**

- **The pooled cell value can fall outside every admitted report's interval (O115/O176).** The concordance band is \[largest lower ÷ 1.20, 1.20 × smallest upper\], and each report's midpoint is clipped to it before the geometric mean. But O86's 1.20 factor is an *admission* tolerance (two reports may disagree by 20% and still coexist); reusing it as the *estimation* clip lets the published value sit up to 20% beyond the tightest bound any observer reported. Example: observer A gives a point reading 10.0, observer B gives \[10.5, 12.0\]; LL = 10.5 ≤ 1.20 × SU = 12.0 passes, the band is \[8.75, 12\], midpoints 10.0 and 11.25 are unclipped, and the pooled value ≈ 10.61 — above A's exact reading and outside the intersection of the intervals (which is empty). That value becomes the route's multiple, hence Plan cost, value score and rank. The stated intent ("a wide report cannot pull the pool beyond what O86 admitted") conflates admission tolerance with estimation support. No report routes are admitted at this release, so the effect is latent — but the rule governs the first cells that pass. **Change:** clip the geometric mean to \[LL, SU\] when LL ≤ SU (guaranteeing the value lies inside every admitted report's interval); when the bounds cross, keep the geometric mean but publish a `crossedBounds` flag and each tightest bound; in both cases flag `valueOutsideIntervals` whenever the unclipped mean exits the intersection.

**Medium**

- **E246's effort binding is not versioned, so it can silently rebind.** A SemiAnalysis figure naming a model without an effort binds to "the configuration AA plots under the bare model name." If AA re-plots the bare name at a different effort on a later capture, every plan row served by that figure changes configuration with no change-detection event — E18/O77 detect limits, prices and credits, not AA plot semantics. This can move measured multiples between configurations of one model, hurting exact-variant traceability (the fixed exact-variant evidence policy). **Change:** store `boundConfiguration` (model, effort, AA capture ID) on the route at admission; each release, compare against the current bare-name plot and flag `effortBindingChanged`, treating a rebind like a donor change under E144's editorial-event rules.
- **E270's build stop contradicts the observer-pooling machinery of O189/O192 and lacks merge semantics.** Part 7 records both that an observer's several values pool into one report with tightest readings (O192, E350) and that "E270 stops the build on a second record from one observer in one partition." An observer who posts a follow-up measurement (a second record, e.g. a follow-up Reddit thread) in an unchanged partition therefore blocks the release, even though the machinery to pool readings exists — routine evidence growth becomes a blocker, and the draft never states what a merged record's date (E194) or partition membership would be. **Change:** auto-merge a second record into the observer's `readings` list under O192's tightest-readings rule, E194 date = the latest reading's window end, reserving the build stop for identity conflicts (same link, inconsistent values).
- **The O46 MiMo ceiling's net direction is indeterminate and the point sits at a rate corner, not just the use corner.** The ceiling is flagged "the optimistic end on use" (full quota consumption), but its rate choice — "for Pro its cache-hit rate and the lower of its two published rates" — is the *minimum* of the recorded per-token rates, which is conservative and assumes effectively 100% cache-hit traffic; the two named directions offset by an unpublished amount. O125 fixed the point, so this is not a challenge to the point's value but to its labelling and missing bounds. **Change:** publish a four-corner bracket \[credits × r_min ÷ fee, credits × r_max ÷ fee\] over {day, night} × {cache-hit, standard} rates (all on Xiaomi's public price list, reusing the O144 bracket machinery), plus a mix-weighted sensitivity using the registry's shared token mix already maintained for the Claude brackets.
- **Reduced search reach is recorded but not discounted in the conclusions that rest on it.** The search log states reddit.com (via archive mirrors only), X, Zhihu, LINUX DO search, GitHub search and YouTube were unreachable from the build environment, yet the MiMo research's "found no end-user measurement" (which, with O62, keeps the O46 ceilings in place) and E278's coverage test treat a mirror-only search as full coverage. Absence of evidence from unreachable live sources is being used as evidence of absence. **Change:** add a `reach` object per research record (sources attempted / reached / via mirror), publish per-cell `searchReach: reduced` where the three-stage search was degraded, and treat reduced-reach no-findings as provisional for O62/O46-retention decisions (e.g. ceilings retained but flagged until a full-reach search).
- **The weekly→monthly conversion at 52/12 presumes the label "weekly" equals the meter's actual period.** The eligibility profile converts a weekly allowance at 4.333 weeks, which the draft itself calls an annualised display convention. Two things are new here: (i) providers' labelled windows have demonstrably not matched the meter's real period — a telemetry study of Claude Max found the "weekly" limit resetting on a 72-hour cycle during mid-2026, roughly 10 resets a month, not 4.33; (ii) even on a true weekly meter, reset timing is account-assigned and fixed (Claude) or rolling from first use (ChatGPT/Codex 5-hour windows), so calendar months contain either 4 or 5 full weekly windows depending on phase. **Change:** derive the conversion factor from the quota schema's recorded reset semantics (period as documented or measured), never from the plan's label, and publish a \[4, 5\]-window calendar bracket beside the point for any weekly-metered admitted cell. [gist.github](https://gist.github.com/monperrus/3ac4b303a84946bbeaf2b1123ee99491)

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from public, routinely refreshable data:

1. **Intersection-clip for pooled cell values (fixes the High finding).** Value = clip(GM(clipped midpoints), \[LL, SU\]) when LL ≤ SU; when crossed, publish the GM with `crossedBounds` and the tightest bounds. Adds one field to `planAllowanceCells` and one flag; no new evidence.
2. **Reset-period-derived conversions.** Conversion factor = 30.44 ÷ recorded actual reset period (from the quota schema's semantics fields), with a calendar-based \[4, 5\]-window bracket for true weekly meters. This closes the >2× error channel seen when a "weekly" label conceals a shorter cycle. [gist.github](https://gist.github.com/monperrus/3ac4b303a84946bbeaf2b1123ee99491)
3. **O46 four-corner bracket** over day/night × cache-hit/standard rates plus a mix-weighted sensitivity from the registry's existing shared token mix, so readers can see the ceiling's net direction rather than two offsetting one-sided flags.
4. **E246 binding versioning:** `boundConfiguration` + capture ID on the route, compared each release, with `effortBindingChanged` routed through the E144 editorial-event path.
5. **E270 merge rule:** auto-merge second observer records into `readings`, O192 tightest readings, E194 date = latest window end; build stop only for same-link value conflicts.
6. **Search-reach accounting:** `reach` field per research record and per-cell reduced-reach flags feeding O62/E278 conclusions.
7. **Register OpenAI's paid resets as a public consumption-dependent payment rule.** OpenAI documents purchasable instant/banked resets that immediately restore both 5-hour and weekly usage and move the weekly reset date  — exactly E80's "publicly evidenced consumption-dependent payment rule" case. Recording it in the quota schema lets the capacity program bound what full utilisation plus purchased resets delivers, instead of leaving ChatGPT plans on the "no additional purchases" default. [help.openai](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001507-paid-weekly-work-and-codex-rate-limit-resets)
8. **Window-length-weighted geometric mean as a published sensitivity** (weights = measured window length in days from the already-recorded window field), keeping the equal-observer GM as the official figure — it costs nothing new to collect and shows how sensitive a cell is to short anecdotes.
