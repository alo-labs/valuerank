## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

None.

**High**

None.

**Medium**

1. **Promotional tariffs get no standard-tariff bound, and the S2 branch's exactness gate is stricter than the evidence requires.** E141 keeps a promotion with an unknown end in the primary ranking, flagged "promotional tariff", with a standard-tariff sensitivity branch "only where the standard tariff is publicly documented **and the consumption ledger supports repricing**". Two harms to the objective: (a) a label earned at a promotional cost is durable only until the promotion ends, and a promotion with an unknown end has no `scheduled_refresh_at`, so it persists on page-diff detection and the monthly cadence — a buyer acting on the label up to a release later may face the standard price; (b) there is no hull, whereas O94/O98 hull AA pricing omissions and E196/O196 widen hulls for adverse changes on flagged Plan routes, so the promotional case is the one repricing risk the machinery does not bound. The new argument against the current gate: E185's ratio bound C′ ∈ [C·min r, C·max r] (and the Plan-side [P·min r ÷ max r, P·max r ÷ min r]) needs only the two public price vectors, not per-category consumption — so the branch's second condition excludes exactly the cases where a bound is still computable. Recommended change: for any row flagged "promotional tariff" whose standard tariff is publicly documented, compute the E185-style standard-tariff bound from the two price records and (i) publish it beside any label the row earns at the promotional price, (ii) admit it as the row's blocker hull under the E276 rule that shared factors sit only on blockers. Where the promotion is a uniform multiplier this collapses to the exact common-factor k case E170 outcome 2 already defines.

2. **The configurations ValueRank cannot answer for are counted but not named.** The Known limitations section states the max-effort ranking ranks 9 of the 37 cohort configurations, the AI Coding Daily ranking 20, and 12 are in neither. The shares are published (`rankingCoverage`, O166) and O143 names unevaluated cheaper *siblings* in headline answers, but nothing in these parts names the 12 cohort configurations that have no value answer in any ranking, or why (which missing leaf, which absent independent SWE coverage). Why it hurts: a public ranking whose objective is to rank frontier models silently omits a third of its cohort from every output; a reader cannot distinguish "not frontier" from "not answered", which is the trust question the O43 listed-families disclosure already answers for the frontier claim. Recommended change: publish a named "not answered" table per release — configuration, family, and the specific blocking gap (missing leaf, no independent SWE benchmark coverage, listed-not-ranked reason) — generated from data the build already holds (the leaf matrix and the listing reasons behind O36/O38/O135). This is fully implementable and refreshable.

**Low**

1. **Capacity certification can be withheld purely by grid resolution.** E39/E56 certify a point capacity only when the feasible lower bound and relaxed upper bound coincide, on a default 15-minute grid; a bound divergence caused only by grid granularity forces the interval [lower, upper] into Θ as a structural bound on M_W even when the public quota semantics are fully known. This is conservative, not wrong, but it converts a numerical artifact into a permanent identification failure on the Plan basis. Recommended change: when 0 < upper − lower < a stated tolerance (say 2% of lower), automatically re-solve on a halved grid (bounded iterations, e.g. down to 5 minutes) and publish both resolutions with `grid_resolution`; certification still requires coincidence. This separates "semantics genuinely unresolved" from "grid too coarse" in `optimality_scope`.

2. **Calendar-day ages leave up to ~24 hours of slack at every threshold (E314).** Ages are whole UTC calendar days with strict thresholds, so an observation taken up to about a day past a boundary still counts in the lower stage. The capture instants for the artifacts this governs are recorded (they are in the evidence bundle), so the slack is avoidable. Recommended change (amends the E212/E222 convention): compute ages from instants wherever both endpoints are instants, and keep calendar-day counting only for date-only stamps (e.g. Bug Hunt report dates, where no instant exists). One line of fixture updates.

3. **Search-reach limits are disclosed but not linked to the compensating intake path, and mirror staleness is indistinguishable from absence of evidence.** The limitations record that the staged search could not reach Reddit directly, X (HTTP 402) or YouTube, and read LINUX DO only through a mirror; "no concordant pair" outcomes for those providers partly reflect where the search could look. This stays within owner decision O197 (automated reach), so I am not challenging it — but two cheap improvements exist: (a) the limitation text and the registry search log should cross-reference the O124 form-submission channel (hashed evidence artifacts), which is the existing mechanism by which reports from unreachable platforms can still enter; (b) each mirror read should record the mirror's own last-update or crawl date, so a stale mirror is distinguishable from a genuine absence of reports. Both are implementable from public data.

4. **The E62 matched-panel statistics cannot localize disagreement.** The two τ_b statistics (quality agreement; quality-substitution value agreement) run over the independent leaves as a set — with only two or three independent leaves (Vals boards + LiveBench Overall, or Bug Hunt + AI Coding Daily), a single disagreeing leaf dominates the statistic and the reader cannot tell which. Recommended change: publish a per-leaf Spearman or τ_b of each independent leaf against the observed composite on the matched panel, beside the existing two statistics. Descriptive only, same panel, no new evidence requirements.

5. **The bridge's fallback bucket is mislabelled.** A component public evidence cannot separate is labelled "benchmark-record update — decomposition unavailable", which asserts a cause class (benchmark-record update) in the very case where the cause is unknown. Recommended change: rename to "unattributed (decomposition unavailable)" so sequential-bridge output cannot be read as an attribution.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritized, all implementable from routinely refreshable public data:

1. **Standard-tariff bounds for promotional rows (from Medium 1).** For each row flagged "promotional tariff" with a documented standard tariff: collect the standard and applied price vectors from the existing E141 price records; compute r over priced categories/bands; publish the API bound C′ ∈ [C·min r, C·max r] and Plan bound P′ ∈ [P·min r ÷ max r, P·max r ÷ min r] beside the row's earned labels, and apply the bound as the row's blocker hull only (E276-consistent: the row still earns its own label at the published promotional record; it cannot deny another row a label on the strength of a price that may not persist). Uniform-multiplier promotions use E170 outcome 2's exact k.

2. **Named "not answered" table (from Medium 2).** Per release, per ranking and overall: configuration name, family, AA plot status (frontier / near / retained under O145 / listed), the specific missing leaf or coverage gap, and the listing decision that governs it (O36, O38, O41, O135). Place it beside `rankingCoverage` in scores.json and reference it from the headline answers, extending O143's sibling disclosure to configurations with no ranked sibling at all.

3. **Grid-refinement pass for capacity certification (from Low 1).** Trigger: certified bounds diverge by less than 2% of the feasible lower bound. Action: halve the grid, re-solve both bounds, publish both resolutions and the grid-induced gap as a named component of `optimality_scope`. Certification still requires exact coincidence of the refined bounds; no loosening of E115/E56.

4. **Instant-based ages (from Low 2).** Age = (V − capture/measurement instant) in exact hours wherever both endpoints are instants; calendar-day counting retained only where a source publishes dates without instants. Thresholds unchanged (30/90/120 days); fixture set extended to sub-day boundaries.

5. **Per-leaf matched-panel decomposition and search-log cross-references (from Lows 3–4).** Add per-leaf rank correlations to the E62 output; add the O124 submission-channel pointer and mirror last-update dates to the registry search log.
