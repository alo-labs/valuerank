### Low
- **Near-anchor unclipped rows in E242 transfer tolerance (Part 1)**: E242 defines a symmetric tolerance `2^(t/d)` for unclipped rows and a directional tolerance for clipped rows, noting that neither exists when `2^(t/d) > c_H / c_L`. However, an *unclipped* row (`c_L < C < c_H`) that sits close enough to an anchor that `C · 2^(t/d) > c_H` or `C / 2^(t/d) < c_L` also hits the clip before moving its value by `t` in the direction of the nearer anchor. Why it hurts the objective: minor mathematical edge case where the symmetric tolerance is unreachable in one direction. Concrete change: note in E242 that for unclipped rows within `2^(t/d)` of an anchor, the tolerance applies only in the direction away from that anchor (and is unreachable toward it).
- **Missing upper cap on signed scale correction in E234 / E316 (Part 2)**: E316 specifies that a third-party gap fill adjusted by the cross-source mean offset `d` becomes `z - d` "floored at zero, so a negative `d` raises it". When `d < 0` (as Cognition currently is at `-1.5` against AA) and `z` is near 100, `z - d` can exceed 100. Why it hurts the objective: minor domain violation on a `[0, 100]` percentage leaf. Concrete change: change "floored at zero" to "clipped to `[0, 100]`".
- **Multi-fallback-model aggregation in O158 (Part 3)**: O158 states that a row's fallback attempts are summed over evaluations "with the fallback models named" (plural), then re-priced "from the fallback model's list price to the row's own at the lower and the higher of the input and output price ratios". If a row ever triggers more than one distinct fallback model across evaluations, the text does not state whether the ratio pair is taken per evaluation (attempt-weighted) or as the min/max across all named fallback models. Concrete change: add one clause specifying per-evaluation attempt-weighted repricing (or min/max across the named fallback models) when multiple fallback models appear on one row.
