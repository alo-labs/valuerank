## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

- **Owner decision challenge — the current Plan ranking has no Plan-specific cost evidence.** Part 17 says 0 of 30 max-effort Plan rows and 0 of 74 AI Coding Daily Plan rows have an authorised or admitted route, so every row falls back to API cost. The new argument against O13/O72/O48 is not merely that fallback rows are mixed with measured rows; coverage is categorically zero, so the “Plan” ordering cannot measure subscription fees, quotas or route availability at all. API costs remain useful diagnostics, but calling the result an official Plan ranking changes the estimand while preserving the appearance of a Plan comparison. **Change:** when measured/admitted Plan-route coverage is exactly zero, publish `no measured Plan ranking this release`, retain the API-cost rows only under a separately titled fallback diagnostic, and propagate a `PLAN_NO_MEASURED_ROUTE` status through scores and the summary. If continuity absolutely requires numeric rows, place a top-level `0/N measured-routes; ordering is API fallback, not Plan value` banner wherever the table appears.

- **Owner decision challenge — the AI Coding Daily value score is currently cost/workload-misaligned.** Part 17 says that ranking presently has no coding-agent cost unit after the FrontierCode stand-in retired, while its quality composite specifically rewards coding-agent outcomes. This is stronger than the already settled “pending component” concern: fulfilling a coding-quality workload at a cost inferred from general SDLC work is not an incomplete estimate of the same workload but an estimate of a different cost generator. The O182 per-attempted-task check can reveal an implausible number, but a passing ratio does not make the basket coding-workload evidence. **Change:** until an exact-variant coding-agent cost component is admitted over the identical ranked subset, display AI Coding Daily quality rank beside generic-SDLC cost as two diagnostics rather than combining them into a value score. Alternatively, restore a narrowly defined temporary coding unit from public exact Terminal-Bench attempted-task cost or FrontierCode chart cost, version it separately, publish its task scaling, and distinguish it from a complete basket. This challenges O116/O137 and O182/O183 on the ground that workload orientation, not merely coverage, is missing.

**High**

- **Owner decision challenge — the lead ranking is too narrow to carry the public objective without stronger naming.** Part 17 gives a 37-configuration cohort, 9 max-effort rows, 20 AI Coding Daily rows and 12 configurations in neither; those figures imply 25 ranked in at least one and an overlap of only 4, for which the inter-ranking agreement check is undefined. A 9-row specialist workload can be valuable, but making it the lead “frontier value” output risks misleading users about absent frontier choices. The new argument is the combination of low lead coverage and demonstrably insufficient common-panel evidence. **Change:** add a mechanical lead-eligibility display rule—for example, lead status requires at least two-thirds cohort coverage using the existing O57 denominator convention, otherwise the headline reads `specialist workload: 9/37 cohort rows covered`. Keep the computation unchanged, place the common-leaf comparison beside it, and never aggregate the two scores across rankings.

- **Owner decision challenge — labels remain affirmative model claims under unresolved harness confounding.** Parts 14, 15 and 17 describe vendor/harness-dependent results, a transferred tbench.ai spread, acknowledged pair- and direction-specific assumptions, and the possibility that the stress is unevaluable. Yet labels remain cross-harness under O162, and the S2 harness stress is not part of Θ or the authorising label branch. The new point is that an affirmative confirmed or scenario-supported cheapest label can survive when the sole empirical harness-bound construction is vacuous or not benchmark-specific. **Change:** define `HarnessComparable(i,j)` as true only when corresponding quality leaves use the same documented executable or a direct public cross-harness bound covers the compared rows; require it for label candidates and potential blockers, otherwise publish the rank and frequency without a unique-cheapest label. Also title unmatched-harness outputs `model+harness stack value`, not model value, until the owner answers the estimand question below.

**Medium**

- **The 15-minute scheduling grid can under-certify continuous window feasibility.** A feasible gridded schedule correctly gives a conservative lower bound, but a relaxation still constrained to gridded session starts is not automatically an upper bound on continuous-time session schedules. Discrete-time scheduling formulations are approximations of continuous-time problems, with approximation error and model size as standard limitations. Consequently, equal gridded lower and upper bounds should not by themselves prove a point capacity under session-anchored or other continuous semantics. **Change:** model established session starts with continuous event variables, or refine 15-, 5- and 1-minute grids and certify convergence with a valid continuous relaxation; carry \([L_{\rm feasible},U_{\rm continuous}]\) into M_W until the certified relative gap is below a pre-registered threshold. [lin.engin.umich](https://lin.engin.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/551/2021/10/Mixed-Integer-Linear-Programming-in-Process-Scheduling.pdf)

- **Scenario reproducibility is recorded but not cryptographically enforced.** The draft records Python’s version because Python explicitly guarantees only `random()` across versions; other `random` algorithms and seeding functions may change. Recording a version change as a draw-order change helps auditability, but it does not prevent an accidental regeneration under an untested patch release from silently changing scenario frequencies that feed labels. **Change:** freeze the released scenario artifacts and store their SHA-256, container or interpreter digest, seed, draw order and first 32 raw `random()` outputs; make reproduction fail unless all match. Prefer project-owned Beta and normal transforms driven solely by raw MT outputs. [docs.python](https://docs.python.org/3.12/library/random.html)

**Low**

- **“Most advanced contributor’s stage” is ambiguous.** In the Plan lifecycle, the phrase could mean the freshest report, the furthest lifecycle state, or the most conservative state. Implementations could therefore let one current report mask a stale partner, or vice versa. **Change:** specify `stage(partition) = maximum(stage_i)` under the explicit order Current < Possibly superseded < Expired < Demoted, and publish every contributor’s own age and stage beside the partition result.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

1. **P0 — Add a claims gate before every headline.** Add `claimability = {full, specialist, fallback-diagnostic}` to the decision-target signature. Require measured Plan-route coverage greater than zero for a Plan claim, workload-aligned cost evidence for a value claim, and disclosed cohort coverage for a lead ranking claim. This preserves ranked rows while preventing the output grammar from overstating what is measured.

2. **P0 — Use a route-coverage state rather than implicit fallback semantics.** For each ranking \(r\), publish
   \[
   \rho^{\rm Plan}_r=\frac{\#\{i:\text{official Plan-specific route admitted}\}}{\#\{\text{Plan rows}\}}.
   \]
   If \(\rho^{\rm Plan}_r=0\), set the Plan value field to `∅` and display API fallback cost separately. If \(0<\rho^{\rm Plan}_r<1\), show measured-only and mixed-route panels consistently rather than allowing route availability to masquerade as value.

3. **P0 — Align the AI Coding Daily basket with its target workload.** Define a temporary coding unit from one public exact-source cost—for example \(C^{\rm TB}_{i}=c^{\rm attempt}_{i}\times N_W\), where \(N_W\) is a versioned benchmark-task count—and use it only on the identical exact-variant subset. Publish the unit’s public source, attempted-task definition, denominator, exclusions and workload hash. If its coverage falls below the owner’s two-thirds standard, split quality and generic cost instead of producing a composite.

4. **P1 — Formalise lead coverage.** Print
   \[
   \rho_r=\frac{|R_r|}{|C_t|},\qquad
   \rho_{\rm union}=\frac{|R_{\rm max}\cup R_{\rm AICD}|}{|C_t|},\qquad
   |R_{\rm max}\cap R_{\rm AICD}|
   \]
   beside both names. Treat low overlap as an explanation limit, not as evidence of disagreement, and automatically retitle any lead ranking below the selected coverage floor as a specialist workload.

5. **P1 — Make harness comparability part of candidate eligibility.** Store `harness_id`, `harness_version` and `comparison_scope` on each quality record. A unique label requires all potentially qualifying blockers to be either same-harness or covered by a direct paired bound; a broader model+harness ranking can remain separately scored and clearly titled. This does not impute quality across variants or efforts.

6. **P1 — Certify continuous-time capacity, not grid-specific optimality.** Separate constraints into public-quantified exact events and approximate session events. Compute a feasible lower-bound schedule, a mathematically valid continuous upper relaxation, and a grid-refinement certificate. Publish `capacity_error = (U-L)/L`; keep M_W set-valued until that error meets the registered tolerance.

7. **P2 — Publish a capability-domain coverage matrix.** For every quality domain \(d\), calculate
   \[
   W_d=\sum_{\ell\in d}w_\ell
   \]
   after normalisation and display its covered and unintended-zero shares. This would make the current absence of an instruction-following leaf explicit in the quality vector itself rather than only in the limitations narrative.

8. **P2 — Freeze scenario generation as a release artifact.** Use a small golden covariance plus scenario-hash test in CI: regenerate every stream, compare raw draws byte-for-byte, and fail on any interpreter, container, transform or artifact-hash mismatch. This is stronger and cheaper than relying on prose that records Python’s version.
