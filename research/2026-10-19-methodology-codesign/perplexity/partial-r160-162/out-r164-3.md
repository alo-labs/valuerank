**Medium**

1. **Change-detection evidence has no stated independence test.** The most consequential flag of this release — the undocumented Claude shared-meter change "from 2026-10-17" — rests on "per-token measurements by two Max users in claude-code issue 102667 and a Reddit report" . E18's two-report rule is invoked (the 2026-10-13 one-user report is rejected), and the reports are called "independent", but E223's machinery (distinct observers, no shared upstream source, build-checked, with the same-thread proviso) is defined only for pooled report admission; nothing in parts 5–6 states it applies to change-detection reports . Two users posting in one GitHub issue can share an upstream source (one reading derived from the other, or both from a shared dashboard), and Anthropic's own position (support-bot denial, second-hand A/B report) leaves the change contested . Why it hurts: this flag cascades to every Claude plan route and to the transfers and O88 routes resting on them, types "unclear", and puts each flagged route's interval output over an open-ended hull — a large cost-side distortion resting on evidence whose independence is asserted, not tested. Concrete change: state explicitly that change-detection reports must pass the E223 independence test (no shared upstream source), record the upstream-source test for the issue-102667 pair in the registry, and add a build check that fails on an untested change record carrying two same-thread observers.

2. **The paywalled selected analysis is auditable for tampering but not for accuracy.** E238 pins the SemiAnalysis selection "by URL and by the figures transcribed at selection" and discloses that "the article itself is not captured in the evidence bundle, so no content hash is published"; O130's per-release re-read is performed by the same party that made the transcription . The public can verify the transcription's *stability* (it is hashed in `plan_cost_routes.json`) but not its *fidelity* to the source. Why it hurts: the entire measured Plan basis for the two leading providers rests on one paywalled article, and the objective is a public ranking refreshable from public data — a systematic transcription error would be undetectable by any outside reader. Concrete change: for each transcribed figure, publish its locator in the article (figure/table/row label, e.g. "figure table, row 'Max 20x, Opus 5.5'") alongside the route record, so any subscriber can spot-check; archive a licensed excerpt if SemiAnalysis permits. This costs nothing routine and closes the audit gap O130 only partially covers.

3. **Long-context tier optimism is disclosed in prose only.** The OpenCode Go routes for Claude Haiku 5.5 (long-context tier "prices every token category five times higher") and GPT-6 Luna (1.5–2x) have multiples "taken at the short-context tier", so "a long request divides those two routes' multiples by up to that factor and their Plan rows are optimistic for long requests by that much" . Why it hurts: a Plan row can overstate value by up to 5x with no numeric, machine-readable output a reader, build check, or downstream sensitivity can consume — unlike the E144 price-ratio brackets, this exposure exists only as a sentence. Concrete change: publish, per affected route, a tier-adjusted multiple as a labelled sensitivity (M_short and M_long = M_short ÷ tier factor, using the published per-category tier prices), in the same style as the E144/E146 brackets.

4. **Owner decision challenge (O161/O164/O167): the cost of the report-window cut is unmeasured.** Because each cell's admission window ends at its latest staged search while the monthly clock allows that search to be up to 31 days old, up to four weeks of reports dated inside the release envelope are excluded as "dated after the cell's latest staged search"; today's effect is nil (the all-provider search of 2026-11-05 coincides with the cutoff) . I am not re-challenging the cadence (O164 is settled). What is new: the size of the excluded set is never counted, so the tradeoff is assumed rather than observed, and the weekly freshness check already scans providers without recording whether it incidentally noticed end-user reports. Concrete change: publish, per release, the count (and plan cells) of reports excluded solely under the O161 cut, and let the freshness-check log record — without admitting — end-user reports it incidentally encounters. No staged search is added, no admission rule changes, and the O164 cadence is untouched.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from routinely refreshable public data:

1. **E223-for-change-detection check (addresses Medium 1).** Extend the build's independence gate: every change record citing two or more reports stores `upstreamTested: true/false` with the evidence IDs; the build fails on an untested record whose reports share a thread or venue without a derivation ruling. Data source: the existing E18 registry records; no new capture burden.
2. **Figure-locator provenance (Medium 2).** Add `sourceLocator` (article figure/table/row) to each transcribed SemiAnalysis route figure in `plan_cost_routes.json`; the O130 re-read then compares locator-tagged figures, and the locator is published in the route-alternative table. Cost: one-time annotation, refreshed only on re-selection.
3. **Tier-adjusted multiple sensitivity (Medium 3).** For each OpenCode Go route with a long-context tier, publish M_short and M_long = M_short ÷ f, with f = the published per-category long-context price ratio (5 for Haiku 5.5, 1.5–2 bracketed for GPT-6 Luna), as a labelled sensitivity beside the row — formula, factor source (the archived plan page), and both displayed Plan value scores shown together.
4. **Excluded-report counter (Medium 4).** One number per release in `planAllowanceCells[]`: reports dated within [V − 30 d, V] but excluded solely by the O161 cut, with the cell's `searchedThrough` already published. Zero build risk, direct evidence for future O164 reviews.
5. **Flag-prevalence summary (Low 4).** A one-line table in scores.json: per route-level flag (change-flagged, reset-spanning, mix-unmatched, not-archived, workload-unmatched), the count and share of Plan rows carrying it.
6. **Schema rename of the `singleProvider` mark (Low 2).** `narrowScope: true` with the old field kept as a deprecated alias for one release, since the search log is build-checked and versioned.
