Midpoint convention is internally inconsistent (O115). A passing cell's value is the geometric mean of report midpoints, but the midpoint of a range is presumably arithmetic ((a+b)/2). For a ratio-scaled quantity, the natural midpoint is √(a·b); arithmetic midpoints bias wide, coarse meter readings upward, and the two conventions disagree most exactly where O86 tolerates the coarsest readings. Concrete change: define midpoint = √(lower × upper) in the E270 eligibility profile, or state the arithmetic convention explicitly in the registry so the choice is at least pinned.

2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from refreshable public data:

Per-model speed-mode multiplier registry with drift stop and sensitivity band (fixes both Medium items on O76). Fields: model, mode, multiplier, source URL, check date; recompute each release; ±20% one-row bracket on converted routes reusing the O144/O146 publication channel (planRouteBrackets).

Reach matrix and coverage-limited flag (fixes the High item): a searchReach table (stage × platform × reachable), rendered beside planAllowanceCells; E278's stop rule amended to distinguish unreachable venues; archive-mirror procedure generalized beyond Reddit.

Crossing-aware pooling semantics: O189 exclusion for crossed per-observer tight bounds; intersection-first clipping for the O115 pool with the band as fallback, plus a flag when the pooled value lies outside all observed ranges.

Tasks-per-billing-period companion diagnostic (Owner decision challenge, O3-compatible). Within a single plan, cross-model differences in M reduce to list-price-per-token differences — the draft itself states the mechanism ("a dearer target's tokens are worth more API dollars", part 8) — so the full-utilisation headline rewards expensive tokens rather than delivered work, a distinction practitioners already ask benchmarks to make. Without touching the official score: publish, per plan row, estimated tasks per billing period = (M × fee) ÷ the row's SDLC-basket per-task cost, labelled "not a score, not a claim". All inputs already exist in the build (M, fee, per-task basket costs).

Night-rate bracket for O46 ceilings: the draft prices MiMo ceilings at day rates and notes the 0.8× night rate "would raise it by up to 25% and is not counted". Publish [day-rate ceiling, night-rate ceiling] as a bracket sensitivity (same O144 machinery) so the optimistic-end labelling is quantified rather than only asserted.

Geometric midpoint convention for range midpoints in the O71 profile (√(lower × upper)), or an explicit pinned definition of "midpoint" in the registry.
