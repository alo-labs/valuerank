## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

None.

**High**

- **Provider-claimed leaves can set bar levels (E233 × E192/O34).** Part 13 defines the bar population as "the ranking's ranked rows that are not provisional and have a displayed quality … a listed row never sets a bar." E233 blocker rows (a row with a provider-claimed leaf, e.g. MiMo-V2.6-Pro under O37's third fallback, where O36 explicitly admits "the provider's result" as a last-resort leaf source) are ranked, non-provisional and quality-complete, so they sit in the bar population and can set the headline bar H and both quartile bars. This hurts the objective because the cheapest-above-bar labels — the ranking's headline claims — are then *certified against a threshold partly defined by marketing-grade evidence*, even though E233 itself judges that same evidence too weak to earn a label. The ladder is an order statistic, so one inflated provider claim shifts every bar by roughly one position's worth of quality (material at n ≈ 9–20). The population rule already excludes cost-side unverified rows (E222 provisional); the quality-side analogue is missing. Concrete change: amend the bar-population predicate to `ranked ∧ ¬provisional ∧ ¬has_provider_claimed_leaf`, as a versioned bar-policy change with the E197/O120 dual run; minimally, publish a `verified_bar_ladder` sensitivity beside E243's `costed_bar_ladder`. This amends the population under O34's ladder, so it needs owner sign-off — **Owner decision challenge** (new argument: E233's own evidential logic, previously applied only to label eligibility, has not been extended to bar-setting).

**Medium**

- **Label denial on benign plan changes exceeds the recorded owner decision (E92/E228 vs O196).** Part 14's blocker predicate flags a Plan row whose route had "a detected limit, price or credit change … dated on or after the earliest day its measurement can have been taken," with no direction qualifier; part 13 then has "a benign change leaves the point record," i.e. benign-change rows are blockers that compete at their published point but "never earn a label." The ledger's O196 ties "no label" explicitly to *adverse or unclear* changes ("an adverse or unclear change … widens its blocker hull … ; no label"); the benign case is not covered. This hurts the objective in substance, not just authority: a benign change (quota increase, price cut) makes the published record *conservative* for the row, so denying its label is over-conservative and can systematically strip the only legitimate cheapest-above-bar answer on the Plan basis whenever a provider improves a plan. Concrete change: either record an owner decision that any detected change suspends label eligibility until a concordant post-change report pair (O78) — labelled "label suspended: change pending re-measurement" — or scope the E228 clause of blocker(i) to `direction(change) ∈ {adverse, unclear}` so benign-change rows keep eligibility at their published record. **Owner decision challenge** (the draft extends O196 beyond its recorded text).

**Low**

- **O196 hull product is not scoped by direction.** Part 13: "the blocker then competes over [C, C divided by the product of the stated factors], or [C, ∞) when any such change states none." "Any such change" is parsable as adverse/unclear, but "the stated factors" is not explicitly restricted to factors ≤ 1; a stated benign factor above 1 mixed into the product would shrink the upper endpoint below C, contradicting the same sentence's "the upper end is at least C." Concrete change: write the hull as \([C,\; C / \prod_{k \in \text{adverse, unclear}} f_k]\) with each \(f_k \le 1\), state that benign factors never enter, and add a fixture (benign 1.2 + adverse 0.5 → hull [C, 2C]).
- **Noise-margin companion population is unpinned.** Part 13's O44 companion takes "the median, over the ranking's configurations (one value each, the same on both bases), of the analytic quality SD." "The ranking's configurations" is not a defined set (ranked rows? cohort? bar population? blockers included?), unlike every other population in these parts (E180, E203's panel, E341). Since the margin feeds the published bar answer, the population should be named in the bar policy object — recommend "median over the bar population" for consistency with the level's provenance.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from the existing build's public data:

1. **Verified-leaf bar population (fixes the High finding).** Bar population = ranked ∧ ¬provisional ∧ no provider-claimed leaf; ship as bar policy v-next with the E197 transition and an O120-style dual run. Publish `verified_bar_ladder` (ladder excluding provider-claimed rows) beside `costed_bar_ladder` so the effect is visible even if the owner keeps E233 rows in the primary population.
2. **Direction-aware label suspension (fixes the Medium finding).** Split the E228 blocker predicate into: adverse/unclear change → blocker over the O196 hull (as today); benign-only change → row stays in R_W at its published record, flagged "benign change pending re-measurement," label-eligible, with O78's fresh-report-pair rule as the unblocking path. If the owner prefers strict staleness denial, record it as an explicit decision so the ledger and draft agree.
3. **Hull formula precision (Low).** Rewrite the O196 hull with an explicit product over adverse/unclear factors and the ≥ C guarantee as an invariant, plus the fixture above.
4. **Companion SD population (Low).** Add `margin_population` to the bar policy object (recommend the bar population) and print it in `scores.json` beside `displayedCheapest.<basis>.bars[].margin`; also cross-reference in the summary that the O44 companion (median-based, one value per ranking) and the O171 pointwise screen (per-row σ_i with frozen σ_H) are different quantities, so readers do not read the companion as the screen's pass rule.
