Part 10: "if no row or more than one row matches, the row with the latest test date among the matching rows, or among all rows when none matches". M3 — The GDPval drift diagnostic measures consecutive-capture movement, but the estimand risk under O70 is cumulative refit drift. Part 10's diagnostics.gdpvalMovement compares each page's displayed Elo against "the capture it replaced". Because AA refits the Crowd-BT model as models join (Elo fitted by MLE over all pairwise comparisons ) while its Index integrates the rating frozen at a configuration's addition, the divergence between ValueRank's leaf and AA's own Index input accumulates across many captures; consecutive moves can each be ~0 while cumulative drift is material. "0 of 27 pages moved between two captures" is near-evidence-free about the estimand question. Why it hurts the objective: the headline quality composite could drift for reasons unrelated to any configuration's performance, and the published diagnostic would report zero movement throughout. Recommended change: store each configuration's first captured Elo in the provenance registry and publish cumulative drift (and drift since the last anchor freeze) beside the consecutive-capture move; flag rows whose cumulative drift exceeds the O44 noise-margin equivalent. This does not challenge O70; it measures O70's cost. Owner decision challenge, in part: if cumulative drift turns out to be systematic and large, that would be new evidence for revisiting O70 — but the recommendation stands under O70.
