GPT lower-of transfers are exempt from the O144/O146 bracket machinery on a justification that does not establish r = 1. The draft brackets Claude cross-model transfers by price ratio and same-model cross-effort routes by Index price ratios, then states ChatGPT transfers "are not bracketed, because Codex usage is priced per model at API token usage (OpenAI's Codex rate card...)". Per-model pricing is not itself a reason to skip a bracket: the bracket is needed precisely when the meter-to-API-dollar conversion differs between donor and target. OpenAI's Codex rate card prices credits per 1M input/cached/output tokens at model-specific rates that differ by roughly an order of magnitude across models on the same plan (e.g., GPT-5.6 Luna 25 vs GPT-5.6 Sol 125 vs GPT-6 Astra 250 credits per 1M input tokens), so unless credits are priced at exact API-dollar parity for every donor/target pair, r ≠ 1 and the transfer uncertainty on ChatGPT plans — the largest consumer segment of the Plan ranking — goes undisclosed while Claude's is bracketed. This asymmetric uncertainty disclosure weakens the objective's defensibility. Concrete change: compute the per-category ratios r_k from the already-recorded Codex rate card and API list prices exactly as O144 does for Claude and publish them in planRouteBrackets; or, if the registry's rates are at exact credit-to-API-dollar parity, add a build check asserting r_k = 1 within tolerance and print "r = 1 (rate-card parity)" so the exemption rests on a verified fact rather than an assertion.

The diagnostic exists to isolate how assumption-derived official anchors reorder measured-only rows "through the exchange rate alone", but the padded span inflates ln(c_H/c_L) by ln 4 relative to official-style anchors built from the same rows, systematically shrinking cost's influence in the measured-only rescoring. Owner decision challenge (O82): the blanket bar on 5-hour figures discards a set-identification path the draft's own machinery supports. O82 says a 5-hour figure "stays in its own unit and never converts to a billing period, so it never admits a route". But part 7's quota schema records window semantics (session-anchored / fixed reset / sliding), and E61's taxonomy admits set-identified intervals: with documented reset semantics, a monthly fee and a per-window figure w bound the billing-period multiple in [w, ⌈billing period ÷ window⌉ × w] — a finite interval, not an extrapolation. The real-world mechanics bear this out: the 5-hour window starts at first use and is shared across surfaces, with a separate weekly limit also in force. There is also a coherence tension with O3's full-utilisation stance: at full utilisation a user saturates every window, making windows-per-month × w the natural full-utilisation reading rather than an assumption. Today this rule discards the only three concordant end-user reports found for a cohort model (GPT-6.1 Terra on Plus). Concrete change, without touching the official ranking: (a) publish 5-hour cells in planAllowanceCells as flagged one-sided diagnostics with their recorded window semantics, so concordant measured evidence is visible rather than invisible; (b) if the owner accepts the interval reading, admit a flagged set-identified route [w, N·w] into the measured-only view only, where N is computed from the documented semantics. All inputs are public provider documentation refreshable each release.
