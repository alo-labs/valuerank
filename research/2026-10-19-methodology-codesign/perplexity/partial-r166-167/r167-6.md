## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

**High**

**Medium**

1. **The S1T kernel treats Terminal-Bench as a binomial leaf, but AA documents its score as a mean over three attempts per task.** Part 11 draws binomial leaves from a Jeffreys Beta posterior with "k = n × score ÷ 100" and lists Terminal-Bench at n = 66 among them, explicitly naming only AutomationBench-AA, FrontierCode and AI Coding Daily as leaves "whose per-task score lies in  but is not binary." However, AA's own coding-agent methodology states that "each benchmark score is the mean score over three attempts per task", and E68 ties the leaf to "AA's Index Terminal-Bench 4.0 score," so each task contributes a value in {0, 1/3, 2/3, 1}, not a Bernoulli trial. The Jeffreys Beta(66) kernel therefore overstates task-sampling dispersion by up to √3 (exactly √3 if attempts were independent, shrinking toward 1× as within-task attempt correlation rises). This matters because the kernel feeds the O42 noise bands, the O44/O128 noise margin and the separability statements — published user-facing uncertainty figures for a leaf carrying 11 points (max-effort) and 17 points (AI Coding Daily) — and because the implied `parameter_derivation` under E25 ("66 binary task outcomes") contradicts the source's documented scoring protocol. The direction is conservative (wider noise, fewer separable ranks, no label at risk), which is why this is Medium, not High. Recommended change: record the attempt structure in the kernel object and publish an attempt-level alternative (see §2.1); note that Terminal-Bench 4.0 being 66 tasks is correct.[0][1][2][3][4][5]

**Low**

1. **Arithmetic slip in the Jeffreys-variance parenthetical.** Part 11 says the Beta draw's SD is smaller than the plug-in SD "by a factor √(n/(n + 2)) (1.5% at 66 tasks, 0.2% at 1,000)." At n = 66 the figure 1.5% is right (√(66/68) ≈ 0.9852), but at n = 1,000 the SD reduction is ≈ 0.1% (√(1000/1002) ≈ 0.99900); 0.2% is the *variance* reduction. No output changes — E353's rule takes the larger SD as the analytic summary, so the slip is purely in the published text — but it sits in a document whose figures are claimed build-checked (E265), and this one is a hand-computed aside. Recommended change: correct to "0.1% at 1,000" or restate as "variance smaller by 0.2%, SD by 0.1%."
