## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

None.

**High**

1. **The ChatGPT transfer-bracket exemption rests on a meter characterization the draft's own registry contradicts.** Part 8 brackets every Claude transfer (O144) but states "ChatGPT transfers are not bracketed, because Codex usage is priced per model at API token usage." Two problems. First, O126's registry (quoted in the same part) records that "today every ChatGPT and Claude plan meters tokens" — and OpenAI's own documentation indeed meters Work/Codex usage as credits per 1M input, cached-input and output tokens at per-model rates, i.e., a token/credit meter with model-specific rates, not a model-invariant dollar meter. On a token meter with per-model rates, a multiple measured on one GPT model transfers to another only under the same equal-attainable-allowance conditional the Claude bracket exists to expose, so the price-ratio bracket should apply to the GPT lower-of transfer too. Second, the exemption's premise (per-model API pricing makes the multiple transfer exactly) is contradicted by the draft's own data: GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra have *different* SemiAnalysis figures per plan, which is precisely why O45's lower-of rule exists — model-invariance visibly fails. Why it hurts the objective: "every other GPT row" on ChatGPT plans rides the lower-of transfer — the largest assumption family in the official Plan ranking — with zero uncertainty disclosure, while the comparable Claude family gets mix-level and per-category brackets; a reader cannot see how much of a GPT plan row's value rests on the price-ratio conditional. Recommended change: key bracket eligibility to the O126 meter-denomination record and the quota schema's burn coefficients, not to provider family; for token/credit meters with per-model API-parity rates, bracket GPT cross-model transfers with r = target's list price per token ÷ donor's, over the registry's shared token mix, exactly as O144 does for Claude. [help.openai](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11481834-chatgpt-rate-card-business-enterpriseedu-credit-based-pricing)

**Medium**

1. **Reasoning tokens are missing from the per-category bracket.** O144's `categoryMultipleRange` spans "input, cache-read, cache-write and output" — four categories — while the Value Multiple explicitly records "reasoning" as a token category and E131's canonical ontology is five coordinates (uncached input, cache read, cache write, reasoning, answer-only output), with the draft itself verifying that AA's output = answer + reasoning (37,901,142 = 13,409,872 + 24,491,270). A transfer between models whose reasoning-to-output price ratio or reasoning share departs from the mix can move the effective multiple through a channel the bracket does not expose — the exact failure mode the bracket was built to prevent ("a category whose ratio departs from the mix's … is not hidden"). Recommended change: extend `categoryMultipleRange` to the full E131 ontology (add reasoning; keep cache-write inclusion as-is) and state explicitly whether the shared token mix used for the headline r includes reasoning tokens.

2. **The cell-level admission-window rule cannot be applied from the draft alone.** Part 7 states the general window (2026-10-02 to 2026-11-01, at the release cutoff), then carves out cells "a later supplementary search covered, whose admission windows end at that search, or at the release cutoff when the search is later" (O161, O185), then the blanket rule that a report dated after the release cutoff is outside every window, with E194 elsewhere dating windows to the search cutoff. The operative rule for any given cell is only recoverable as an unstated precedence — roughly windowEnd(cell) = min(V, latest staged search covering the cell) — which is exactly the kind of ambiguity that produces divergent re-implementations of a public methodology. Recommended change: state one formula for windowEnd(cell) and windowStart(cell) in the draft, and publish the per-cell window bounds in `planAllowanceCells` alongside the cell's status, range and value so the build-check is mechanical.

3. **Unreachable search platforms are logged but never surfaced per cell.** The search log records that reddit.com (via archive mirrors only), X, Zhihu, LINUX DO, GitHub search and YouTube pages were unreachable from the build environment. The coverage gate (E278's build-stop on an uncovered cohort model × plan cell) and O71's ≥2-report rule depend on the staged searches actually reaching the venues where users publish plan measurements — X and YouTube for OpenAI/Anthropic allowances, Zhihu for Kimi's China-region tiers. A cell whose three-stage search could not reach those platforms is not covered in the same sense as one that could, yet both pass the same gate. Recommended change: record per-cell platform reach in the search log's structured output and flag cells with partial platform reach in `planAllowanceCells`, so the coverage claim is auditable rather than global.

**Low**

1. **Speed-mode multipliers are hard-coded without registry provenance.** O76 converts user reports at "OpenAI Fast 2.5×, Ultrafast 8×" — real, checkable OpenAI service modes  — but unlike the Codex rate card and meter denominations (E176, E261, O126, all registered with check dates), the multipliers have no registered source URL, capture date, or stated conversion direction (multiply vs divide). A silent multiplier change would silently rescale converted reports. Recommended change: register both multipliers in `evidencePolicy` with source, check date and direction, and stamp each converted report with the multiplier applied. [help.openai](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11481834-chatgpt-rate-card-business-enterpriseedu-credit-based-pricing)

2. **The geometric-mean pooling estimator is unjustified and mildly biased low.** A passing cell's value is the geometric mean of clipped midpoints (O115/E350). Under the O86/O192 band the maximum ratio of two clipped midpoints is 1.44, so GM vs arithmetic mean differs by at most ~1.7% — immaterial today, but the choice is never defended. Recommended change: either justify it (scale symmetry with the log-cost score) or publish an AM-vs-GM comparison as a diagnostic in `planAllowanceCells`.

3. **Owner decision challenge (O82): discarded 5-hour evidence is invisible to readers.** Three concordant GPT-6.1 Terra reports on ChatGPT Plus are discarded solely because they are five-hour figures — sound under the no-extrapolation rule, and consistent with how these plans actually meter (5-hour windows with weekly caps on Plus; Pro tiers without 5-hour limits). But the concordance was earned and the draft records it nowhere reader-visible. Recommended change, implementable without touching the numerator rule: publish in-window 5-hour figures as a labelled diagnostic (the O41 pattern — "not ranked, not comparable"), so a flagship model's Plus utilisation signal is not lost to the audience while staying out of scores and routes. [support.claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11647753-how-do-usage-and-length-limits-work)

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritized, all implementable from routinely refreshable public data:

1. **Meter-keyed bracket rule (fixes High 1).** Replace provider-family bracket eligibility with a rule driven by the O126 registry: for each plan, read `meterDenominations` plus the quota schema's pool scope and burn coefficients. Token/credit meters with per-model rates → cross-model and cross-effort brackets with r = p_target·μ ÷ p_donor·μ over the shared mix (O144/O146 unchanged); API-dollar-denominated meters → r = 1, no bracket, with the meter record cited as the reason. This also makes the December 2026 announced ChatGPT change (shared token allowance across ChatGPT and Codex) self-handling: when the meter changes, the bracket treatment follows automatically under O77.
2. **Full-ontology category bracket (fixes Medium 1).** `categoryMultipleRange` = [M × min_k min(1, r_k), M × max_k max(1, r_k)] over k ∈ {uncached input, cache read, cache write, reasoning, output} from the E131 ontology, with the shared mix's reasoning share published alongside `planRouteBrackets`.
3. **Publish per-cell window bounds (fixes Medium 2).** Add `windowStart`, `windowEnd`, `windowEndBasis` (release cutoff / latest staged search / supplementary search) to each `planAllowanceCells` entry; one formula in the text.
4. **Per-cell platform-reach disclosure (fixes Medium 3).** A `platformReach` field per search stage per cell, derived from the existing search log; cells with partial reach flagged. Zero new research cost — it is bookkeeping over what O75's three stages already log.
5. **Register the speed-mode multipliers (fixes Low 1).** `evidencePolicy.speedModeMultipliers` with `{value, source, checkedOn, direction}`, stamped on each converted report.
6. **5-hour diagnostic display (the O82 challenge).** A `diagnosticFiveHourFigures` list in scores.json, labelled "not ranked, not comparable, 5-hour window," refreshed with the same report pipeline.
