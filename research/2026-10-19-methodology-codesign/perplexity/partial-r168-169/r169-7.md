E204's left-of-line tolerance is denominated in rendered pixels. The build gate checks that no plotted point lies left of the rendered path's first vertex "by chart x-coordinate, tolerance 0.5 px". Why it hurts: the cohort C_t = P_t ∪ T_t is the foundation of the whole ranking, and a plotted point cheaper than every rendered-frontier vertex has no F(c) and is silently not near-frontier; E204 is the only guard, and its threshold's meaning in dollars depends on the capture's chart width, resolution and axis scale (log or linear), none of which the rule records. If AA re-renders the same data at a different size, the same capture can pass or fail across releases, and a point within tolerance left of the vertex drops from the cohort without the owner decision E204 exists to force. Recommended change: calibrate the axis from the capture's own tick marks (pixels per x-unit, log/linear flag recorded in the capture metadata) and express the tolerance as a cost-relative threshold — e.g. flag when a plotted point's x-cost is more than 0.5% below the first vertex's cost — keeping the pixel value only as a fallback with the calibration printed.

A provider-claimed row can set a bar level. E92/E156 let a ranked, non-provisional row with a provider-claimed leaf (E233) count in the bar population, and O34's ladder freezes displayed qualities from that population, so a self-reported score can set the headline bar H that the "cheapest above bar" answer is keyed to. Why it hurts: the claiming row earns no label, but the published cheapest-above-bar answer would then be keyed to a threshold of unverified provenance — exactly the marketing-evidence leakage the Value Multiple and leaf rules elsewhere exclude. Today's named setters (GPT-6 Luna, Claude Sonnet 5.5 (high), GPT-6.1 Sol (high)) are not provider-claimed, so this is latent, but nothing in the draft blocks or flags it. Recommended change: add a build-checked setterHasProviderClaim field to the E156 policy object's bar record, and publish a bar_ladder_no_provider_claims sensitivity beside E219/E243, recomputed on the population minus provider-claimed rows. This escalates to High the moment a provider-claimed row sets a bar.

The O171 pointwise noise screen fixes correlation 0, inconsistent with O42's published grid. The screen Δ_i = (Q̄_i − H)/√(σ_i² + σ_H²) at correlation 0 is printed beside each displayed-record answer in the leading rankings' headlines, making it the most prominent noise statement in the product — while O42's noise bands publish a 0/0.3/0.6 correlation grid and E337 disclaims only simultaneous coverage, not correlation sensitivity. Why it hurts: with positively correlated quality noise between a candidate and the bar setter (shared task pools across benchmarks), ρ = 0 understates separation, so answers read "rounding-stable only" or "contested" more often than the rest of the noise machinery implies, and a reader cannot see how sensitive the verdict is to the independence assumption. Recommended change: print Δ at ρ ∈ {0, 0.3, 0.6} with the declared verdict at ρ = 0, mirroring O42; both remain diagnostics and change no label.

2. Methodology design recommendations
