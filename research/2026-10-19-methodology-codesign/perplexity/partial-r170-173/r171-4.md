1. Findings by severity

Critical

None.

High

H1 — No joint (donor-group) uncertainty propagation for shared-donor transfer families. Every Claude transfer rests on one Opus 5.5 donor measurement, and every non-Sol/Astra GPT row rests on the lower-of rule over the same two SemiAnalysis figures; an error in a single donor moves all its dependants together. The O144/O146 brackets are per-row, leave-one-out rescorings on price ratios with "every other row at its displayed value" — they cannot show the rank effect of the donor multiple itself being wrong (e.g., sitting at the edge of its O115 concordance band). E167 handles donor removal by making dependants unevaluable (which says nothing about ranks), and E274 marks interval labels unresolved when rows share an uncertain donor but quantifies nothing. This hurts the objective because the official Plan ranking (O3/O4) has its largest assumption block — all Claude rows, most GPT rows — with no published bound on how ranks move if the one underlying measurement is off, which is exactly the question a sceptical reader of a public ranking will ask. Concrete change: publish a donor-group joint sensitivity — for each donor route with a measured range (reports: the O115 clip band [largest lower ÷ 1.20, 1.20 × smallest upper]; a SemiAnalysis donor: its published figure range), rescore all dependant rows simultaneously at donorM × band_low and donorM × band_high, and publish per-group multiple/value/rank ranges in scores.json beside planRouteBrackets. Purely diagnostic, computable from data already in the registry, refreshed with the same releases.

Medium

M1 — O76 speed-mode multipliers are unregistered and uncensused. The conversion multipliers (OpenAI Fast 2.5×, Ultrafast 8×) are provider-published figures that directly scale the numerator of an admitted, measured route — yet, unlike O46 ceilings and O198 limits (both with capturedOn, recompute checks and renewal clocks), part 7 gives them no source record, capture date, or change-detection path, and part 8's assumption enumeration names only transfers, O46 and O198, omitting O76 entirely. So the E242 census of rows "resting on an owner assumption, by kind" may not count them, and a speed-converted report can feed the O157 measured-only Plan anchors — meaning a provider-published multiplier can influence anchor setting while the row presents as measured. Concrete change: register the multipliers in evidencePolicy (source page, capture date, recompute check, E18-style change detection, build stop on change), add a speedModeConverted kind to the E242 census, and flag affected rows in planRows. Owner decision challenge (the stronger option): exclude speed-converted figures from O157 anchor eligibility — "measured rows only" was not written with provider-multiplier-scaled numerators in mind, and this is the one place the fixed no-provider-numerator rule leaks into official evidence unlabelled as an assumption.

M2 — The no-bracket rule for ChatGPT transfers is under-justified as stated. Part 8's stated reason — "Codex usage is priced per model at API token usage" — does not by itself neutralise the price-ratio effect; per-model pricing is exactly the condition under which a token-metered allowance is worth different API dollars per model, which is the very rationale O144 applies to Claude. The decisive fact is the allowance's denomination (a shared credit/dollar cap, which transfers dollar-value-neutrally, vs a token cap, which does not), not the meter's unit. Public evidence cuts both ways and must be pinned: usage is measured in tokens and shared across ChatGPT and Codex, and Work/Codex usage is priced per model in credits per 1M tokens by input/cached/output category — the same structure as Claude's. (Side check: the announced-change paragraph is accurate — OpenAI's help center confirms the December shared ChatGPT/Codex allowance.) Concrete change: add a limitDenomination field per plan to evidencePolicy.meterDenominations; if the ChatGPT allowance is a credit cap, state that mechanism explicitly with the rate-card citation (then no bracket is correct and the draft should say so); if it is a token cap, apply the O144 bracket to GPT model-to-model transfers using the per-model per-category rates from the rate card — fully public and routinely refreshable.

M3 — Third-party plan cells have no coverage gate. E278's build stop fires only on cohort model × first-party plan cells, while O195 brings third-party plans into scope. A cohort model sold on a third-party plan therefore has no equivalent gate: an unsearched third-party cell silently degrades to API fallback, with no flag distinguishing "searched, nothing found" from "never searched" — undermining the claim that staged research coverage is complete (the very thing E278 exists to guarantee for first-party cells). Concrete change: maintain a roster of third-party plans that list cohort models, built from those providers' published plan pages and refreshed each release under the same ≤7-day capture rule, and extend the E278 stop to cohort model × rostered third-party plan cells; publish the roster as a bundle artifact like the Bug Hunt roster.

Low

L1 — E164's diagnostic anchor widening may not match the official rule. The measured-only calibration uses c_L = cheapest/2, c_H = dearest×2; if the official Plan anchors do not widen identically, the diagnostic conflates the evidence-composition effect it is meant to isolate with a ln 4 shift in the exchange rate (which systematically shrinks the cost term's weight in the diagnostic). Both anchor objects and exchange rates are published, so this is comparability, not correctness. Concrete change: publish a matched-widening variant of the calibration, or state in the draft that the official anchor rule widens identically.

L2 — Unstated-effort figures take the exact-effort precedence tier. E246 binds an unstated-effort SemiAnalysis figure to the AA-plotted max configuration and flags it, but the grade key then treats it as an exact-effort measurement ranked above O88 routes — a tier stronger than "source effort not reported" establishes. Value is unaffected (O87 effort-invariance), so this is Low: give unstated-effort measurements their own tier (or O88-class standing) in the identification-class key so precedence never outruns evidence.

2. Methodology design recommendations

Donor-group joint sensitivity (H1), highest priority. For each donor measurement d with range [d_L, d_U] (O115 clip band for report-based donors; published range otherwise), rescore the set R_d of all dependant rows jointly at M_r × d_L/d and M_r × d_U/d for every r ∈ R_d; publish group id, member rows, the moved multiple/value range, and each member's rank range in scores.json (planDonorGroupBrackets). Fixtures: donor with two dependants that swap ranks with a non-donor row; group move that changes the cheapest-above-bar answer.

Registry record and census kind for speed-mode multipliers (M1). Fields: source URL, capture date, provider-stated multiplier, recompute check on every build, stop on a difference; E242 kind speedModeConverted; row-level flag in planRows.

limitDenomination field deciding bracket applicability (M2). Values: tokens / credits / unresolved; brackets apply to model-to-model transfers only where tokens, with the documented exemption (rate-card citation) otherwise; build-checked against the rate card each release.

Third-party coverage roster (M3). A bundle artifact listing third-party plans offering cohort models, with the E278 stop extended to those cells and an unsearched flag distinguishing never-searched from searched-empty cells.

Matched-widening calibration variant (L1). One extra run of E164 with the official widening rule applied to the measured-only anchors, published beside the existing one.
