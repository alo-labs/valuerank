**Critical**

**High**

- **H1 — The label gate rests on one partially circular like-for-like statistic (Owner decision challenge to O182/O183/O187).** The gate that decides whether "cheapest above bar" labels are issued reads τ_b between basket cost and Terminal-Bench 4.0 cost per attempted task — but Terminal-Bench cost is itself a basket component, so the draft itself concedes "a pass shows agreement with that component." Meanwhile the execution-relevant reading (cost per solved task) reads τ_b 0.35 on 20 configurations in the AI Coding Daily ranking, below the pre-registered 0.5 threshold, and labels still stand there with a warning riding on them. Why it hurts the objective: the headline deliverable can be issued while the basket's cost ordering disagrees with outcome-adjusted cost for most of the panel, and the informative divergence is demoted to a warning beside every label. Concrete change: (a) extend the like-for-like check to every basket component where per-attempted-task cost and resolution are both public — the Coding Agent Index publishes a pooled per-task-attempt average cost under O27 and a resolution score, so a second like-for-like check is computable today from already-captured data; (b) make the gate read the minimum τ_b over available components and withhold when any component's resampling interval lies entirely below 0.5. The leave-one-component-out τ_b attribution already computed for the diagnostic makes the marginal implementation cost near zero.
- **H2 — The registered leave-AA-out branch is inoperative, leaving the dominant structural risk unstressed (challenge to E146's 40% coverage rule, framed as an addition).** With AA running 66% (max-effort) and 78% (AI Coding Daily) of nominal quality weight, the joint-removal branch is "not identifiable" in both rankings, so common-harness bias shared across all AA leaves (same run protocol, same capture pipeline) is exposed only by per-leaf removal and E62's matched-panel statistics, neither of which perturbs the AA leaves jointly. Why it hurts the objective: a public value ranking whose quality vector is two-thirds to three-quarters one lab's measurements has no operative answer to "what if AA's harness is systematically off by a couple of points?". Concrete change: register a **cross-source common-shift stress** — perturb every AA-run leaf by z_ib → z_ib + δ with δ ∈ {−Δ, 0, +Δ}, where Δ is anchored to the observed AA-vs-independent disagreement already captured (E234: FrontierCode AA vs Cognition, τ_b 0.73 on 10, mean +2.5), reported as rank shifts and τ_b on the common panel (E65), with a sensitivity grid Δ ∈  points. This respects the exact-variant evidence policy (it is a labelled stress, never a replacement or transfer) and uses only already-captured public data. **Medium**

- **M1 — AI Coding Daily's noise kernel is the max-variance binomial reading (n = 6) while the per-project capture that would replace it is deferred.** The draft's own figures show the wide/narrow choice moves that ranking's median threshold from 4.48 to 3.12 API (~30% of the threshold), so the published "0 of 19 adjacent ranks separable" statement is kernel-dominated. The wide reading is a legitimate bound (max variance of a mean of six bounded scores), but 10-point graded projects are not Bernoulli, so it is almost certainly loose. Concrete change: promote the per-project points capture to a tracked work item with a build-printed "kernel: wide (per-project capture pending)" flag on that ranking's noise outputs, since the replacement rule (sample SD ÷ √6) is already specified.
- **M2 — S1T's primary kernel carries a score-dependent mean shift (Owner decision challenge to O142/O154).** The Jeffreys posterior mean (k + ½)/(n + 1) shrinks every leaf toward 50% — the draft's own formula 100·((k+½)/(n+1) − k/n) gives shifts of roughly 1.4–2.9 points at n = 6 (AI Coding Daily) and ~0.6 points at n = 40 (CritPt v2). Because the shift's sign depends on the score, it compresses quality gaps in a rank-order-distorting way, not merely as dispersion. The recentred alternative (O154) exists but is secondary, and its disclosed defect (non-mean-preserving after clipping near bounds, E303) matters only near 0/100. Concrete change: promote the recentred kernel to primary when its realised clip rate is below a stated bound (e.g. < 1% of draws at a bound), keeping the raw Jeffreys draw as the alternative, and publish per leaf the pairwise win-frequency difference between the two kernels beside the noise bands.
- **M3 — GDPval-AA rows are drawn independently although a Crowd-BT MLE fit induces rating covariance through shared matchups.** The draft discloses the approximation but does nothing to bound it. Bradley–Terry/Crowd-BT strengths are jointly estimated from all pairwise comparisons, so two models' rating errors are correlated through shared opponents and independent draws can over- or under-state the dispersion of rating differences — exactly the quantity close-model separability reads. Concrete change: mirror the Bug Hunt treatment (E309): restate the affected separability figures and noise bands under a pairwise correlation grid (e.g. −0.3, 0, +0.3) as a specification stress, the house pattern already used for the planted/unplanted correlation. This needs no new evidence and fits the existing S2-stress taxonomy.

**Low**

- **L1 — E353's "analytic SD" is not the SD of the distribution actually drawn.** The draw uses the Beta posterior (variance p̃(1−p̃)/(n+2)) while the published analytic SD is the larger plug-in value; the draft's √(n/(n+2)) factor and the 1.5%/0.1% figures check out, but the label invites misreading. Rename it a "conservative summary SD" in the kernel object.
- **L2 — E72's trigger wording is ambiguous against E77.** "A frequency whose repeated-look-adjusted interval … crosses a decision threshold (90%)" should be restated as E77's trichotomy (met at lower bound ≥ 0.90; not met at upper bound < 0.90; else continue) so an implementer cannot read "crosses" as "moves past."
- **L3 — The workload cross-check does not specify the treatment of ∞ d_ib rows.** d_ib = cost per attempted task ÷ resolved fraction is ∞ when resolution is zero; whether ∞ rows enter the paired panel and tie with each other in τ_b is unstated. Specify it.
- **L4 — LiveBench 2.0's wide reading lacks a published narrow companion.** The kernel uses category counts 4 (Reasoning) and 3 (Data Analysis) as clustered units — a defensible choice, but the suite-level task counts on mirror boards are far larger, and unlike AI Coding Daily no narrow reading is published beside the LiveBench-derived separability figures. Publish both, and pin the per-suite counts in the registry so the reading is auditable.

**Verification notes.** The statistical machinery in these parts is correct as written: the Jeffreys posterior Beta(k + ½, n − k + ½) with mean (k + ½)/(n + 1) and variance p̃(1 − p̃)/(n + 2) is the standard conjugate result; the Clopper–Pearson closed forms at k = n and k = 0 ((δ/2)^(1/n) and 1 − (δ/2)^(1/n)) match the textbook endpoints; tbench.ai's whiskers do span a 95% confidence interval, as the draft quotes; and AA's own methodology page confirms GDPval-AA ratings are fit by Crowd-BT MLE with sandwich-estimator intervals, with per-row half-widths around ±17 to ±26 on top rows reported by secondary boards  — consistent with the draft's ±26 wide-reading sensitivity. LiveBench 2.0's Overall is indeed a weighted mean of the seven suites (Long Agentic ×2, Coding and Design ×1.5, rest ×1), which supports the draft's weighted-variance-bound approach, and the suite task counts the draft uses for Long Agentic (18), Coding (29), Browser (25), Research (22) and Design (25) match published mirrors  — only Reasoning and Data Analysis are re-read as clustered parts. ## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from public data refreshable routinely:

1. **Cross-source common-shift stress (new S2 branch, priority 1).** For every AA-run leaf: z_ib → z_ib + δ, δ ∈ {−Δ, 0, +Δ}, Δ anchored to E234's measured AA-vs-Cognition mean delta (+2.5) with a grid over; report composite shifts, rank changes and τ_b on the E65 common panel per ranking; label "specification stress, not evidence." Data: already-captured E234/E287 cross-checks. This converts the inoperative leave-AA-out registration into a live sensitivity to single-lab systematic bias. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binomial_proportion_confidence_interval)
2. **Multi-component like-for-like gate (priority 1).** Compute the E41-style cross-check for every basket unit with published per-attempted-task cost and resolution (Coding Agent Index under O27 is available now); gate = minimum τ_b over components, withhold when any component's interval is entirely below 0.5; publish per-component τ_b and the leave-one-component-out attribution beside it. Fixes H1's circularity without new data collection.
3. **AI Coding Daily per-project capture (priority 2).** Capture the six graded projects' points per row; replace the n = 6 max-variance binomial with sample SD ÷ √6 (rule already drafted); until capture, print the wide/narrow threshold pair beside every separability figure in that ranking's outputs, not only the median threshold, and flag the kernel as pending-capture.
4. **Recentred-kernel primacy rule (priority 2).** Primary = O154's recentred draw when realised clip rate < 1%; else the O142 Beta draw; publish the per-leaf mean shift, clip rate and pairwise win-frequency delta between kernels in `scenarios.samplingMeanShift`. Reduces the systematic score-dependent compression in S1T's primary output.
5. **GDPval-AA pairwise correlation grid (priority 3).** Add `diagnostics.rankSeparability.api.gdpvalPairwiseRho` with the separability statement, noise bands and noise margin restated at ρ ∈ {−0.3, 0, +0.3} between each close pair's rating draws, mirroring E309's Bug Hunt covariance stress. If AA ever documents pairwise intervals, upgrade to source-derived.
6. **Small specification fixes (priority 3).** Rename E353's summary SD; align E72's trigger wording with E77's trichotomy; specify ∞-row tie treatment in the workload cross-check; publish LiveBench narrow readings beside the wide ones.
