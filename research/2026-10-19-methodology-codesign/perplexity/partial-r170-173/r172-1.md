The E328 algorithm keys membership to the band's first row, so the draft itself concedes "adjacent rows in different bands may also be inseparable", and lists configurations whose own plan rows span more than the threshold. A subset row "joins no band and starts none", has no scenario shares (E266), and its screen neighbours are "display neighbours, not adjacent ranks" — yet it renders as a numbered rank in one list with main-workload rows. O184 (owner decision) requires only that a subset row is ranked within its own workload; the mixed single-table display is a presentation choice, not an owner decision. Recommend a two-panel default (main workload, then subset workload) with the mixed view secondary.

Ordinal primacy foregrounds a distinction that is, by the draft's own figure, within noise for every adjacent pair in that ranking, while the noise-honest grouping (bands, O42/E328) already exists.
