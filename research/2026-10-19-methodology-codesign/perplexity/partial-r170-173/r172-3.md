This hurts the objective because ValueRank's Plan ranking is presented as a headline basis; its current information content is materially different from its steady-state content, and that difference is discoverable only by reconstructing the flag state across every route. Recommended change: publish a per-release Plan-basis status block (share of Plan rows by E139 capacity status; share by flag state clean/superseded/blocking; whether any row can earn an interval label; the remediation trigger per route family — e.g., "an O78 concordant report pair post-dating 2026-10-11 unflags GPT routes") composed from fields the build already has.

Medium

O86's overlap test admits arbitrarily imprecise range-valued reports. Concordance for range reports is max_k lower_k ≤ 1.20 × min_k upper_k; two reports and pass (8 ≤ 12), and the O115 clip then pulls both midpoints (5.5, 10) into the band [6.67, 12], producing a pooled geometric mean (~8.1) that is an artifact of the band edge, not of the reports. This is not in the rejected-claims list (O115 clipping and the O86 overlap rule are settled; a contributor-precision requirement is a new argument). The harm: a route can be admitted to the official Plan ranking on "concordance" of reports that carry an order of magnitude of individual uncertainty, and the displayed Plan score will look exactly like one built on tight measurements. Recommended change: a range report counts toward concordance only if its width ratio (upper/lower) is at most ~1.5; wider range reports count as lower-bound diagnostics under E270. Publish, beside every pooled multiple, the clipped band [largest lower ÷ 1.2, 1.2 × smallest upper] as the M envelope so band-dominated pools are visible.

2. Methodology design recommendations

In priority order:

Release-level Plan-basis status block (addresses High 1). Computed entirely from existing scores.json/registry fields: distribution over the five E139 capacity statuses; distribution over flag states (clean, possibly superseded, blocking-period entered, open-ended hull under O196); a boolean "interval labels deliverable this release" with the blocking routes named; and per route family the remediation trigger (for the GPT family, a concordant report pair post-dating 2026-10-11 under O78; for Claude, one post-dating the unclear shared-meter change, or an owner re-selection of SemiAnalysis evidence measured after it). Zero new data collection; pure aggregation of data the build already gates on.

Precision-gated concordance with a published pooled envelope (addresses Medium 2). Formal rule: a range-valued report r with upper(r)/lower(r) ≤ θ (recommended θ = 1.5) may be a concordance contributor; otherwise it is admitted only as a lower bound under E270. For every passing partition, publish next to the pooled geometric mean the O115 clip band as an interval envelope; the ratio pooled-mean-to-band-width is a one-line diagnostic of whether the pooled value is data-driven or band-dominated.

Denomination-typed third-party-limit computation (addresses Medium 3). Extend the route record's O126 denomination field to govern the multiple: USD-denominated limits use M = limit ÷ fee verbatim; usage- or price-tier-dependent redemption uses E335's repricing form, with each adjustment (off-peak, long-context tier) a named, versioned factor rather than a prose caveat. Record Go's sub-period caps as evidenced constraints in the capacity solver's input so the E70 binding-constraint publication can state whether the monthly limit is attainable and at what burn profile.

Composited Plan-row label (addresses Medium 4). One machine-generated label string per Plan row: {capacity status} × {change-flag state} × {identification class}. This costs a formatting rule over three existing fields and makes every ranked Plan row self-describing without touching ranking semantics.

Submission arithmetic gate (addresses Low 5). At intake, join each form report to the price records in force at its observation date; pass only reports whose implied multiple equals their stored figure within reporting precision. Rejects cheap forgeries of the arithmetic kind and all stale-price submissions.
