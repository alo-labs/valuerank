## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

None.

**High**

1. **E88 dominance pre-filter: two undefined readings break its own guarantee.** Part 5 states the filter removes route b when a has "an evidence grade at least b's and identified multiples (point or set) with L_a > U_b — a is then cheaper on W in every admissible state," and that "L_a > U_b is a sufficient condition under any joint constraints." Two terms are unspecified. (a) *Grade*: comparator key 2 contains the O78/O92/O93 demotions (a controlled measurement flagged by a post-dated change, or inside an acknowledged fault window, ranks *below* a concordant report pair). The filter's "evidence grade at least b's" does not say whether it uses this amended ordering or the raw controlled-measurement-first ordering; in the raw reading, a flagged measurement eliminates the report pair the owner deliberately ranked above it. (b) *Bounds*: for routes carrying O94 ([C, factor × C]), O98 ([C, ∞)) or O196 widened hulls, the "identified multiple" and the admissible box differ; if L_a/U_b are read from the bare identified range, "cheaper in every admissible state" is false — an adversely-flagged route's true multiple can sit far below its bare range. This is not contrived: at this release every Claude SemiAnalysis route is flagged by an *unclear* shared-meter change with an open-ended hull, exactly the configuration where a future concordant report pair (the entire purpose of the O71 searches) could be filtered out by a stale flagged measurement. This distorts Plan cost inputs and therefore Plan value scores, the official Plan ranking's core. **Recommendation:** amend E88 to fire only when (i) a and b share configuration, plan, workload, valuation basis and allocation regime; (ii) L_a and U_b are read from each row's full admissible box including all widenings; (iii) the grade comparison uses the amended key-2 total order, with the owner explicitly choosing whether a demoted measurement may filter the pair that outranks it (recommended: it may not). Add fixtures: a flagged measurement  vs a clean report pair at 10 → pair survives; an O196 open-ended hull never fires. What is new versus E280 (which only restricted the filter to one allocation regime) is precisely these two readings. [leanopstech](https://leanopstech.com/blog/opencode-go-limits/)

**Medium**

2. **Context-tier optimism on OpenCode routes is disclosed in prose only, with no flag or stress output.** Part 6 states the multiple for OpenCode Go routes is "taken at the short-context tier," that Claude Haiku 5.5's long-context tier "prices every token category five times higher" and GPT-6 Luna's "between one and a half and two times," so "their Plan rows are optimistic for long requests by that much." The mechanism is real: Claude Haiku 5.5 is priced by prompt length, with requests over 100,000 tokens paying higher prices. Since Plan cost divides by the multiple, a ÷5 error on the multiple is a ×5 understatement of Plan cost for long-request workloads — material to cheapest-above-bar claims — yet unlike MiMo's O46 ceilings, which O125 requires to be flagged "as the optimistic end on use," this optimism has no machine-readable flag, registry field or published stress figure. **Recommendation:** add a `contextTier` registry field (threshold tokens, tier factor, price basis), flag the affected routes `shortContextValued`, publish a tier-stress multiple (M ÷ tier factor) and the tier-adjusted displayed Plan value score in the route-alternative table, and publish per-ranking tier exposure (share of basket demand above the threshold, computable from captured task context lengths). All implementable from the already-archived price pages. [platform.claude](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)

3. **Owner decision challenge (O199): listing only OpenCode Go is not unconditionally per-dollar dominant.** The draft lists only the single-member Go plan ($10) with no stated rationale. OpenCode's published tier table shows Go Plus at $40 with limits "2x to 4x Go" (never better per dollar) but Go Teams at $20 per member with limits "$5 a month higher than Go on every model". Teams therefore yields a higher multiple (limit ÷ fee) than Go for any model whose Go monthly limit is under $5 — for those models, listing only Go can misstate the Plan cost and any cheapest-above-bar claim that row supports. What is new: the ledger records O199 as a bare decision; the Teams arithmetic from the published tier table is a concrete conditional dominance violation, not a preference objection. **Recommendation:** per cohort model, compute limit ÷ fee for every published OpenCode tier from the archived page, list the argmax tier (or publish all tiers in the route-alternative table), and add a build check that fails if the listed tier is not the argmax. If O199 rests on a purchase-policy assumption rather than dominance, state it. [leanopstech](https://leanopstech.com/blog/opencode-go-limits/)

4. **O124's anti-coordination rule is satisfiable by seeding the staged search itself.** O124 requires a passing cell to include at least one report "found by the staged O71 search rather than submitted." But the staged search reads exactly the public venues an adversary can seed (Reddit, Hacker News, LINUX DO, and X posts read through Yahoo! JAPAN's real-time search, which does surface X posts without login ); one planted public post plus one coordinated form submission satisfies both O124 and E223's distinct-observer test, and would create *measured* evidence that outranks every assumption-graded route. What is new versus O124: the "found by staged search" channel is itself gameable, which O124's found-vs-submitted distinction does not address. **Recommendation:** record per counting report its venue, discovery channel (stage 1/2/3 or form), observer identity and an account-history class (established vs first seen within the admission window); flag a passing cell `lowProvenance` when all counting reports share one venue or all observers first appear inside the window, and publish the per-cell provenance summary. Fully implementable from public data. [search.yahoo.co](https://search.yahoo.co.jp/realtime)

**Low**

5. **The selected SemiAnalysis analysis has no auditability commitment.** Part 5 notes the paywalled article "is not captured in the evidence bundle, so no content hash is published," so a reader cannot verify the transcription or the O130 re-reads against the source. **Recommendation:** at each O130 re-read, publish a SHA-256 of the fetched figure-table bytes (a hash commitment, not the content) and retain the bytes for dispute resolution, mirroring O165's report-archive practice without republishing paywalled content.

6. **Search-cutoff definition wording.** Part 6 first defines the search cutoff as the latest logged search "not marked `singleProvider`" in terms of the routine three-stage search, then satisfies the release-time clock with "the latest all-provider search of either kind," which includes freshness checks. Whether a freshness check moves the search cutoff is inferable but not stated. **Recommendation:** one clarifying sentence: freshness checks are all-provider, carry no mark, and do move the search cutoff, while never moving any cell's report window.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritized:

1. **E88 amendment (from finding 1).** Filter fires iff: same (configuration, plan, workload, valuation basis, allocation regime) ∧ both multiples identified ∧ L_a > U_b where L/U are full admissible-box bounds (O94 [C, f·C], O98 [C, ∞), O196 [C, C/factor] included) ∧ grade_a ≥ grade_b under the amended key-2 total order. Publish each removal's box bounds and the amended-grade comparison, with the three fixtures above. This closes the only path by which the comparator can select a route that is not certifiably cheaper.

2. **Tier-stress output (from finding 2).** Registry: `contextTier: {thresholdTokens, longTierFactor, priceBasis}`; route flag `shortContextValued`; published sensitivity M_tier = M ÷ longTierFactor with the tier-adjusted displayed Plan value score in the E171 route-alternative table; per-ranking `tierExposure` = share of the basket's demand-region passes above the threshold. Sources: provider price pages already archived under O198 (e.g., Haiku 5.5's prompt-length pricing ). [platform.claude](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)

3. **OpenCode best-tier selection (from finding 3).** Per cohort model, compute limit ÷ fee across every published tier from the archived plan page (Go, Go Plus, Go Teams ); list the argmax tier per model; build check fails when the listed tier is not argmax, making O199 self-verifying against future OpenCode repricings rather than a frozen choice. [leanopstech](https://leanopstech.com/blog/opencode-go-limits/)

4. **Provenance hardening (from finding 4).** Extend `planAllowanceCells[].reports[]` with venue, channel, observer identity and account-history class; build-level `lowProvenance` flag on single-venue or all-new-observer passing cells; provenance summary published per cell. This adds Sybil resistance on top of O124/E223/O165 without changing any admission rule.

5. **Re-read hash commitment (from finding 5).** `sourceEvidence.semianalysis.rereads[].contentSha256` recorded at every O130 re-read; bytes held by the owner, hash published.
