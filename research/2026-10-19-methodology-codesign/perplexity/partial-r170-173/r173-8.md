## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

None.

**High**

None.

**Medium**

1. **The cache-rate assumption in AA's cost is registered as provenance but never quantified as a sensitivity.** Part 17's registry records that "AA values cached input at live measurements of the model's typical cache-hit rate, not the run's one-off rate" and that the measurement window is not published, and E176 makes a cache-rate update a swing-bridge cause — but nowhere in these parts is there a numeric stress of C_W against that assumption. This matters materially: cache reads are priced at 0.1× base input on most models (0.025×–0.05× on some), so a shift in the assumed hit rate moves input-dominated cost by large factors. Cost carries 25% of the headline value score (O2), so a reader cannot currently tell how much of a row's cost — and therefore its rank — rests on AA's cache protocol rather than the exact configuration. **Recommendation:** a per-row cache-share stress (design in §2, R1). It is implementable from public data because E157 already reconciles AA's published per-category costs against the price list (p·d = C), so the input/cached/output cost split is public per configuration. This is distinct from the rejected "category-price revaluation branch," which failed on missing per-component category *telemetry*; this stress varies the assumed share against published prices and needs no telemetry. [platform.claude](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)

2. **No clean-evidence companion view for the official Plan ranking.** Part 17's own Known limitations state that 21 of 30 (max-effort) and 60 of 74 (AI Coding Daily) plan rows rest on possibly-superseded or expired routes, and 3 and 12 on measurements inside acknowledged accounting-fault windows. The E212 lifecycle table types every row and the official table carries flags, but the O3 full-utilisation Plan order — the headline Plan output — is computed over all of these together, and per-row flags do not let a reader see the *comparative* order among the cleanly evidenced subset. The O4/O7 pattern already established the analogous fix for route kinds (a measured-only view). **Recommendation:** publish a labelled companion view of the Plan ranking restricted to Current-stage, non-fault-window routes, with its row count printed and a "comparison vacuous below two" guard in the E198 style. It is a pure filter over already-published rows and satisfies the routine-refresh constraint.

**Low**

1. **The E265 figure lint is scoped to sentences, leaving table cells and list items uncovered.** The check fails on an unmarked figure "in any sentence not dated before the release day or tied to a decision," and the current figures in these parts all sit in prose — but the E212 lifecycle table directly below shows the pattern of rule text living in tables, and a stale "N of M" or dollar amount placed in a table cell or bullet would pass the check today. **Recommendation:** extend the lint to Markdown table cells and list items, with a fixture that plants a stale figure in a cell and fails the build.

2. **Structural capacity bounds carry no discretization-versus-semantics decomposition.** Part 15 (E39) publishes feasible_lower_bound, relaxed_upper_bound and grid_resolution, and routes whose bounds do not coincide carry [lower, upper] into Θ as a structural bound on M_W — but the gap between the bounds is never decomposed into the part that is a grid artifact versus the part that is genuine semantic ambiguity (E115's S_r). **Recommendation:** when bounds do not coincide, re-run the LP at a finer grid (e.g. 5 minutes vs the 15-minute default) and publish the gap change; if the gap collapses, label the bound "discretization-limited." Low priority today since no route has an identified M_W, but it is cheap and prevents misreading a solver artifact as semantic uncertainty.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised; all implementable from public data refreshable each release.

1. **Cache-share stress on C_W (addresses Medium 1).** For each ranked row, take AA's published per-category costs reconciled under E157: fresh-input cost I, cached-input cost K, output cost O (I + K + O = C_i,W). Derive the implied cache-read token share s = K / (K + I·r) where r = cached-read price ÷ base input price from the price record in force. Recompute C′ with s shifted by ±10 percentage points (clipped to ) at the same prices, publish per-row ΔC/C and any adjacent-rank movement in `scores.json` as a diagnostic. Keep it scenario-layer only, never entering Θ, consistent with O25's protection of the published record. If AA publishes the typical hit rate per configuration, stress around that published rate rather than the derived share (see Q1).[0][1]

2. **Clean-evidence Plan companion view (addresses Medium 2).** Filter the official Plan table to rows whose selected route is in the E212 "Current" stage and carries no fault-window flag; print the row count, keep the same scoring, and label it the way the measured-only view is labelled. Publish beside the official Plan ranking, not in place of it.

3. **E265 lint extension to tables and lists (addresses Low 1).** Parse rendered Markdown table cells and list items with the same "today / N-of-M / cohort-count / dollar" triggers, plus a build fixture with a stale figure in a cell.

4. **Grid-resolution stress (addresses Low 2).** For any route where the capacity bounds do not coincide, publish the bound pair at 5-minute and 15-minute grids and the gap ratio; a gap that shrinks materially at the finer grid is labelled discretization-limited rather than semantics-unresolved.

5. **Automated transcript stage for video evidence (within O197, not a challenge to it).** The Known limitations record that the staged search could not read YouTube video pages. Since O197 requires only that reach stays automated, add an automated transcript-extraction step for video pages the staged search surfaces, recorded in the search log like every other reached source. Plan-allowance reports (O71) are exactly the evidence class often published as videos, so this widens concordance coverage without any manual pass.
