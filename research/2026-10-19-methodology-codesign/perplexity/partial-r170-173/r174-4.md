1. Findings by severity

Critical

None. The section's rules cited from elsewhere (identification cases, expiry clocks, comparator keys) are internally consistent across the E106 case order, and the current data posture is conservative: today zero end-user report routes are admitted, so none of the admission machinery is silently shaping a score.

High

Arithmetic midpoints inside a multiplicative system. A passing cell's value is "the geometric mean of its non-provisional reports' midpoints," and each observer's pooled midpoint is likewise taken from its tight bounds ((largest lower + smallest upper)/2, E350), with O115's band [LL ÷ 1.20, 1.20 × SU] clipping those midpoints. Everything else in this layer treats multiples multiplicatively: concordance is ratio-based (1.20×), costs are log-scaled, pooling is geometric. An arithmetic midpoint is not the multiplicative centre of a range: for it is 55 versus √(10·100) ≈ 31.6. Because wide ranges arise precisely from coarse meter readings — the very reads O86 was created to excuse — the bias falls hardest on the least precise observers, and it flows through bandMoved, the observers the clipping names, and E348's re-checked provisional contestation. Why it hurts the objective: the first admitted cell will carry a systematically upward-biased point value whose size depends on report granularity, which is precisely the arbitrariness O86/O115 were designed to remove. Recommended change: take the per-report and per-observer midpoint as the geometric midpoint √(a·b), keep the band exactly as is (it is already log-symmetric: log-band = log-midpoint ± log 1.2), and clip in log space. Where a lower bound is zero, the report is a one-sided bound and should enter the range test but never the pooled value (consistent with E61's "zero-inclusive bounds are unidentified"). This is not in the ledger's rejected list; E350 froze the arithmetic form, so this is a new argument against that specific arithmetic.

The weekly→monthly 52/12 conversion applies an extrapolation class O82 forbids, without a stated charter. O82 holds that a five-hour figure "stays in its own unit and never admits a route," and E270 holds that a meter fraction "scales to its own window, never across windows"; yet the eligibility profile converts a weekly exhaustion to a month at 52/12 weeks while calling it "an annualised display convention that does not establish capacity over any one billing period." The two treatments are both defensible — five-hour windows are session-anchored burst caps, while weekly caps apply on top and are the long-run binding constraint — but the draft never states the distinguishing criterion, so the boundary between O82's prohibition and the weekly conversion is reviewer-decreed rather than derivable. Why it hurts: a public methodology whose admission boundary rests on an unstated distinction is not re-derivable by an outside checker, and "does not establish capacity" sits awkwardly next to that same figure feeding case (3) scoring. 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from public, refreshable data:

Log-space pooling for report cells (fixes the High finding). For each report with range [a, b], define m = √(a·b) (reports with a = 0 contribute to the range test only). Pool maternally in the same space: cell value = exp(mean(log m_i)) over non-provisional reports, clipped to [LL ÷ 1.20, 1.20 × SU] — identical in form to today's rule, with the midpoint definition fixed. Publish bandMoved on the log-moved observers as now. Zero regression risk today because no report route is admitted; the change must land before the first admission.

Window-conversion charter (fixes the second Medium). One registry table, keyed per plan and window: {5-hour: display only, case (5)-adjacent, never converts (O82); weekly sustained-reset: monthly factor 365.25/84 = 4.348, case (3) maximum, flagged "annualised, reset phase outside evidence" (E39); billing-aligned: direct}. The charter turns O82 and reviewer 77's ad-hoc constant into one derivable rule and gives O178's five-hour-change non-event a home.

Meter-change re-certification hook (fixes the third Medium). Extend the E18/E18-triggered record for the December shared-allowance change to require two determinations before any GPT route is re-admitted: the O126 meter denomination of the new shared pool, and a dollar-flatness test — if OpenAI publishes model-specific token-to-allowance rates on the pool, transfers on it take O144-style brackets with r from the published rates; if it publishes a model-flat rate, the no-bracket exemption stands with that citation.
