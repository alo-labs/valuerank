The weekly SemiAnalysis watch is the only ungated evidence channel in these parts. Part 5 states the selected-article watch ("a weekly scheduled search of the web, SemiAnalysis's newsletter and its X accounts (report-only)") has no build gate, while every comparable refresh channel fails the build when stale: the monthly and release-time research clocks (E167/E218, part 6), the article re-read (O130, build fails when the latest read is more than 30 days old), and the staged-search stage coverage (O75). Why it hurts the objective: the Plan basis for the two providers that dominate the ranking rests entirely on this one selection, and the design's claim that "recency never needs to be a comparator key" holds only if a successor analysis or figure revision is detected promptly; a silently lapsed watch delays re-selection indefinitely without any release-visible signal. 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from public data refreshable routinely:

Plan-evidence horizon with an age-60 checkpoint (findings Medium 2). Formula: planEvidenceHorizon = date(selectedAnalysis.expiry) − date(V), minimum over selected routes, printed in the release summary beside planResearchClocks; at measurement age ≥ 60 days the release requires either a fresh selection, an additional three-stage search, or a recorded owner acceptance memo before it can ship.

Build-gate the weekly watch (findings Medium 1). Registry: watchLog[] entries of (date, queries, sources reached/unreachable); gate: date(V) − max(watchLog.date) ≤ 14 days, build fails otherwise. This closes the last ungated channel at near-zero cost, since the watch already runs.

A cache-price scenario bracket for M_W transferability. Every currently selected route is "mix unidentified" because AA publishes no cache split, so E210 is inactive and the multiple is applied to the basket workload with no bound at all. A partial, purely diagnostic activation is possible from data already in the registry: providers' published cache-read token prices. Compute the E210 ratio set ρ_k = x_k / u_k under two mix assumptions — the measured cache-heavy mix re-priced at cache rates, and a cache-free mix at list rates — and publish the resulting factor bracket on M_W/M_obs as a sensitivity beside the selected route, never as a score input. This gives readers the first quantified sense of how far a cache-heavy measured multiple can fail to transfer to the basket's demand region.

Per-route stage timeline for flagged routes. Part 6's flagged-measurements list gives per-route "possibly superseded from / blocking from" dates that a reader cannot project forward without diffing releases. Publish a stageTimeline per route (supersededFrom, blockingFrom, expiry, demotion, each with the governing decision ID), so blocker and hull transitions under O196/E276 are visible before they happen.

Batch the Low-severity flags (observation-date basis, OpenCode Go pricing/tier basis, partialScope rename) into one registry-field pass; all are schema additions with no ranking effect.
