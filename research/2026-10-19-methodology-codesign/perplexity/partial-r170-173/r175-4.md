## 1. Findings by severity

**Critical**

None.

**High**

- **Search reach vs. the coverage gate (new argument against the O197 interplay).** Part 7's search log records that reddit.com (only via archive mirrors), X, Zhihu, LINUX DO search, GitHub search and YouTube were unreachable from the build environment — these are the dominant venues for end-user plan-allowance reports. Meanwhile E278 stops the build on any uncovered cohort model × first-party plan cell, and O124 requires a passing cell's reports to be ones the staged search found. Why it hurts: the Plan objective needs routinely refreshable measured multiples; with the primary venues structurally unreachable, every new model × plan cell will systematically fail coverage, holding releases (O12) or leaving routes on fallbacks, and the measured-only Plan view cannot grow — the deadlock is by design, since O197 fixes the search as automated. Recommended change: (a) publish per-venue reachability per release in scores.json (`searchReach`); (b) **Owner decision challenge (O197/O124)**: allow a hashed archive-mirror or manually captured record to satisfy the "staged search found" requirement when the venue is logged unreachable at the search date, labelled "archive-sourced", with E194 dates taken from the original post where the mirror shows it (the O124/O165/O168 hashed-artifact machinery already exists); (c) failing that, make persistent venue unreachability a standing disclosure on the Plan headline rather than a search-log line.

**Medium**

- **O76 speed-mode conversion puts a provider figure in the numerator (Owner decision challenge).** A speed-mode report is converted at the provider's published multiplier (OpenAI Fast 2.5×, Ultrafast 8×) before pooling. The multiplier is a provider marketing number that scales the numerator directly — the exact class the fixed Value Multiple evidence rule excludes — and two converted reports can "concord" at the provider's figure, manufacturing the agreement O86 tests for. OpenAI's rate cards show the Standard-speed credit rates per 1M tokens are entirely provider-published, so nothing independent anchors the conversion. Recommended change: a converted report may not be one of the two admitting reports unless the multiplier is corroborated by one independent standard-mode report on the same model and plan, or by the observer's own metered token counts; otherwise value converted usage at min(provider multiplier, meter-implied multiplier) and flag. [help.openai](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12642688-using-credits-for-flexible-usage-in-chatgpt-personal-plans)

- **The ChatGPT bracket exemption's premise is undocumented and the registered December 2026 change may invalidate it.** Part 8 exempts ChatGPT transfers from the O144/O146 price-ratio bracket with the one-line reason that "Codex usage is priced per model at API token usage". The property that actually justifies exemption is that per-model pricing at API rates makes the allowance effectively **dollar-denominated**, so the full-utilisation multiple is model-invariant — confirmed by OpenAI pricing Work/Codex usage in credits per 1M input/cached/output tokens per model  and by an end-user measurement describing the usage bar as a dollar meter at API list rates. Why it hurts: as written, the exemption looks arbitrary against O144's own premise (ChatGPT plans meter tokens, O126), and the registered announced change — one shared token allowance across ChatGPT and Codex from December 2026, measured in tokens, with advanced models consuming more  — would replace dollar-denomination with a raw token pool, giving GPT transfers exactly the price-ratio exposure the Claude bracket covers. Recommended change: (a) record the dollar-denomination property in `evidencePolicy.meterDenominations` as the exemption's stated premise; (b) add a conditional rule that GPT lower-of transfers take the O144/O146 bracket whenever the recorded meter denomination ceases to be credits/dollars at API rates — the O126 records already detect this. [help.openai](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12642688-using-credits-for-flexible-usage-in-chatgpt-personal-plans)

- **The bracket uses a point "shared token mix" while demand is set-identified.** The O144/O146 bracket computes r over "the registry's shared token mix", but E124's D_iW is a set-identified feasible region with unpublished per-component category splits, and the draft's own `categoryMultipleRange` exists precisely because per-category ratios depart from the mix. Why it hurts: the headline bracket understates transfer uncertainty whenever the target workload's true mix sits at the region's edge — the same principle E208 enforces on the API side (the basket's own demand region, never Index-wide shares). Recommended change: compute the bracket endpoints over the extreme admissible mixes of D_iW (a linear-fractional program over the feasible region; cheap to solve), keep the point-mix bracket as the displayed convenience, and publish both.

- **The weekly→monthly conversion's admission status is stated inconsistently.** The eligibility profile converts a weekly allowance to a month at 52/12 weeks for admission, while the Value Multiple section calls the same conversion "an annualised display convention that does not establish capacity over any one billing period". A reader cannot tell whether a weekly-derived multiple is admitted at 4.333× or is display-only. In reality weekly windows are session-anchored — they start at the first request and reset 7 days later  — so a calendar month holds 4 or 5 windows and 52/12 is only an annual average (±1 window ≈ ±20% monthly). Recommended change: one sentence stating that weekly-derived multiples are admitted at the annualised figure under the full-utilisation headline (O3), with a conservative 4-window variant published beside the route brackets as a sensitivity. [felloai](https://felloai.com/chatgpt-limits/)

**Low**

- **Pooling scale inconsistency.** The pool is the geometric mean of reports' *arithmetic* midpoints; for wide meter ranges the arithmetic midpoint is upward-biased on a multiplicative scale. Publish a sqrt(L·U) geometric-midpoint pool as a sensitivity beside the official O115 figure (no change to the official value).
- **O84 over-splitting (Owner decision challenge).** A change detected on any Kimi plan splits every Kimi plan, discarding concordant in-window evidence on untouched plans; O113's meter-family records already provide narrower scoping. Split only the plans a record names or its meter family reaches.
- **E270 build-stop on duplicate observer records.** A mirror or repost of the same observer's content would hold a release. Dedup by content hash (observer + readings) before the gate; stop only on genuinely divergent duplicates.
- **Invisible concordant 5-hour evidence.** The three concordant GPT-6.1 Terra 5-hour reports (2026-10-31) admit nothing under O82 and appear in no output. Publish 5-hour-window figures as a labelled per-window diagnostic stream (never converted, never a route) so real evidence stays visible to readers on 5-hour budgets.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised; all implementable from existing registry records and routinely refreshable:

1. **Search-reach disclosure and archive-sourced admission** (High finding): per-venue `searchReach` in scores.json each release; hashed archive-sourced reports admitted (labelled) when the venue is logged unreachable at the search date; standing headline disclosure otherwise. Largest single lever on the Plan ranking's measured-evidence coverage.
2. **Conditional bracket activation for GPT transfers** (Medium): bracket switches on when `evidencePolicy.meterDenominations` shows the ChatGPT/Codex meter is no longer dollar-denominated at API rates — directly operational for the December 2026 shared-token change already registered in `announcedChanges`.
3. **Extreme-mix brackets** (Medium): headline bracket endpoints as min/max over D_iW of (Σ_k p_target,k · d_k)/(Σ_k p_donor,k · d_k), solved as a linear-fractional program over the set-identified demand region; publish beside the point-mix bracket.
4. **Speed-multiplier corroboration rule** (Medium): converted reports admit a cell only with an independent standard-mode corroboration, else capped at the meter-implied multiplier.
5. **Cheap sensitivities** (Medium/Low): 4-window conservative variant for weekly-derived multiples; geometric-midpoint pooling sensitivity; Kimi split scoped to named plans or meter family.
