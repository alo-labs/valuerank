**Medium**

1. **Label gate reads a statistic that contains its own comparator (Owner decision challenge, O182/O183/O187).** The like-for-like gate compares basket cost (which includes the AA Index-run Terminal-Bench component) against Terminal-Bench cost per attempted task. The correlation between a sum and one of its addends rises mechanically with that addend's expenditure share, so the gate's τ_b ≥ 0.5 pass becomes easier as TB's share of basket cost grows, independent of the broader basket's agreement with execution cost. This hurts the objective because the gate is the sole mechanism standing between a low broader-workload reading and an issued "cheapest" label — today the AI Coding Daily ranking reads 0.35 on cost-per-solved while its labels stand on a 0.91 like-for-like pass. Concrete change: in `attemptCheck`, publish (a) Terminal-Bench's median expenditure share over the panel (E22 already computes per-row shares) and (b) the leave-one-TB-out τ_b (the attribution loop already computes τ_b with each basket component left out), and treat a pass paired with leave-one-TB-out τ_b < 0.5 as "uncertain agreement" (labels issued with warning) rather than a clean pass.

2. **The LiveBench 2.0 Overall S1T kernel is the only leaf whose SD is not written out.** Part 11 specifies explicit formulas for the binomial, Jeffreys, Omniscience, Bug Hunt, GDPval-AA and AI Coding Daily kernels, but LiveBench 2.0 Overall gets only "the weighted variance bound over its seven categories' published task counts." The weights are not named, and they matter: LiveBench 2.0's Overall is a weighted mean of its seven suites with Long Agentic ×2, Coding ×1.5, Design ×1.5 and the rest ×1, and LiveBench publishes its category weights with each release  — so the "weighted" in the bound must be pinned to those release weights, not question counts or equal weights. This hurts reproducibility of the published noise bands, separability figures and O44/O128 noise margin, which the draft elsewhere holds to exact-formula standard. Concrete change: state SD²_Overall = Σ_c (ω_c/Ω)² · p̃_c(1−p̃_c)/n_c with ω_c = LiveBench's published release weights, and publish the narrow reading for the part-counted categories (Reasoning 4, Data Analysis 3) beside separability figures, mirroring the E263 narrow/wide pair already published for AI Coding Daily. (Note LiveBench 2.0 suites are single-run, so task sampling is the right — and only — noise target here; the issue is specification, not target choice.) [blog.abacus](https://blog.abacus.ai/blog/2026/11/06/livebench-2-0)

3. **The single noise margin is not decomposed by leaf.** O44/O128's quality noise margin (4.5 points) is derived from S1T, a stream the draft itself labels a mixed-target dispersion stress (E330): binomial task-sampling for most leaves, a single-run dispersion approximation for Bug Hunt (E326), an n = 6 wide reading for AI Coding Daily, and an unspecified weighted bound for LiveBench. Without a decomposition, a reader cannot tell whether the margin that gates the cheapest-clearing-bar-margin companion (O44) and the bar noise companion (O171) is dominated by a defensible kernel or by the widest assumption, nor which leaf's evidence would most improve separability. Concrete change: publish a per-leaf variance-contribution table beside the noise margin — contribution_b = (w_b/100)² · Var_b(z) computed from the existing draws — naming the top three contributors and the total variance share of leaves whose kernels are approximations (Bug Hunt, AI Coding Daily wide reading, LiveBench weighted bound, GDPval assumed 95% normal). Entirely post-processing of existing scenario draws; routinely refreshable.

**Low**

1. **E77 uses equal-tailed two-sided intervals for one-sided decisions.** "Met" reads only the lower bound and "not met" only the upper bound, so each decision spends δ/2 on an unused tail. A one-sided bound with the full per-look δ in the relevant tail preserves the Bonferroni family-wise guarantee over the 16 looks at materially higher power (fewer expansions to 160,000). Pure efficiency; no correctness issue.

2. **E72's Monte Carlo SE √(p̂(1−p̂)/N) is a Wald quantity.** It is labelled descriptive and separate from the Clopper–Pearson machinery, but for shares near 0 or 1 it understates uncertainty; reporting the already-computed CP half-width (or a Wilson SE) alongside would cost nothing.

3. **The printed uncertainty descriptions (E155) don't say cost precision is currently a point mass.** Costs are captured at source precision unrounded, so S0/S1 cost draws have zero width today; "published-record reporting-precision scenarios" could be read as including cost noise. The actually-varied-targets object covers this, but the human-readable description should state "cost: point-mass (source-precision capture)" so frontier frequencies are not over-read as jointly cost-and-quality robust.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised, all implementable from public data on the existing build:

1. **Strengthen the O182/O183 gate output (from Medium 1).** Add to `diagnostics.workloadCrossCheck.attemptCheck`: TB's median expenditure share (E22 data), the leave-one-TB-out τ_b (reuse the attribution loop's leave-one-component-out computation), and a two-bit status — clean pass, or pass-with-warning when the leave-one-TB-out reading falls below 0.5. This converts an acknowledged-but-invisible circularity into a reader-visible check at zero new data cost.

2. **Pin the LiveBench 2.0 Overall kernel (from Medium 2).** Write the formula with LiveBench's published release weights, and add the E263-style narrow reading (per-question counts for Reasoning and Data Analysis) beside each separability figure for the max-effort ranking, exactly as AI Coding Daily's narrow/wide pair is published today. [blog.abacus](https://blog.abacus.ai/blog/2026/11/06/livebench-2-0)

3. **Per-leaf variance decomposition of the noise margin (from Medium 3).** One `scores.json` object computed from existing S1T draws: per-leaf contribution (w_b/100)²·Var_b(z), top-three leaves named, approximation-kernel variance share. Feed the same object to the O171 noise companion so each bar answer shows what its margin is made of.

4. **One-sided per-look allocation in E77 (from Low 1).** Keep the Bonferroni-over-16-looks structure and the Clopper–Pearson inversion; allocate the full δ = 0.05/16 to the tail each decision direction reads. Also optionally add the CP half-width beside the Wald SE (Low 2). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binomial_proportion_confidence_interval)

5. **Cost point-mass statement in `scenarios.streamDescriptions` (from Low 3).** One sentence per stream naming whether cost varies at all in that stream, so "cost primitives included" in E161's frontier event is not over-read.
