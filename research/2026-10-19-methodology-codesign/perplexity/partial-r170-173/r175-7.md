**Low**

1. **Vintage mixing in the O171 pointwise screen's denominator.** The screen computes Δ_i = (Q̄_i − H)/√(σ_i² + σ_H²) with σ_H frozen from the evidence that set the bar (E352) and σ_i from the current capture . The draft handles the setter-as-answer case, the record-changed case, and the no-frozen-SD fallback, but not the case where the setter is still ranked and its *current* evidence has been re-captured with a different task count since the freeze: the numerator then pairs a current Q̄_i with a frozen H while the denominator mixes a current σ_i with a frozen σ_H. Why it hurts: the screen is the instance-level stability reading a reader is told to prefer over the O44 companion, and a vintage mismatch in σ_H silently mis-scales Δ in one direction for every row compared against that bar. Concrete change: store the capture id beside `levelSd`, and when the setter's current capture hash differs from the frozen one, print a `levelSdVintage` flag with the current-SD alternative Δ beside the frozen one (both are one line in `displayedCheapest.<basis>.bars[].noise`).

2. **E219 observed-range bar sensitivity is computed for API bar results only.** Part 13 says "the API bar results are recomputed and published beside the claim" ; Plan bar results get no such companion, and no reason is stated. Under O10 the quality vector is shared between bases, so the quality-side sensitivity (missing leaves bounded by the cohort's observed range) applies identically to Plan qualification; only the cost side differs. Why it hurts: an asymmetry with no stated cause invites the reading that Plan bar answers are less robust than they are, or that the sensitivity was forgotten rather than excluded. Currently dormant (O36 holds, the sensitivity equals the claim), so impact today is nil. Concrete change: either extend E219 to Plan bar results (same quality recomputation, cost machinery untouched, E273-unresolved rows stay unresolved) or record the exclusion reason in the registry.

3. **The O44 noise margin's median pools rows across workloads within a ranking.** The margin uses "the median, over the ranking's configurations (one value each, the same on both bases), of the analytic quality SD" . If a ranking carries subset-workload rows (permitted by O117/E184), their per-row SDs are computed on fewer leaves and pool with main-workload SDs in one median — a mild tension with E277's principle that noise quantities stay within one workload. Dormant today, but the pooling is invisible when it activates. Concrete change: compute the median over main-workload rows only, or publish both medians when a subset-workload row first appears.

4. **The E228 fallback window start is unlabelled as to conservatism direction.** Where a measurement's window start is unknown, E92/E228 takes "else 29 days before publication" as the earliest day a superseding change can be dated . A measurement actually taken earlier but published recently thus has its window mis-dated forward, over-flagging routes as superseded blockers. That direction is conservative for label integrity (blockers deny labels, never earn them) but weakens label coverage, and the draft states the rule without stating the direction. Concrete change: one sentence in the registry entry noting the fallback over-flags (blocker-leaning), so a reader who sees a Plan route blocked on a dated change can tell whether the block rests on a stated window or the fallback.

## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised; all implementable from the existing build artifacts.

1. **Freeze-paired screen variant (fixes Low 1).** In `barCurrency`/bar records, store `{levelSd, levelSdCaptureId, levelSdSetter}`. In the O171 screen, when `levelSdCaptureId ≠ currentCaptureId(setter)`, print `Δ_frozen` and `Δ_current` side by side with a `vintage-mixed` flag; the label semantics (rounding-stable / contested / passes) follow `Δ_frozen` as the conservative reading. No label logic changes; one additional field in `scores.json`.

2. **Extend E219 to Plan bars (fixes Low 2).** Reuse the API recomputation path on the shared quality vector (O10): for each Plan bar result, recompute qualification with missing leaves bounded by the cohort's observed range, publish beside the claim as `observedRangeBars.plan`, labelled "sensitivity, not evidence". Rows whose Plan intervals are unresolved under E273 publish "unresolved" there too — no new solver class is needed.

3. **Workload-scoped O44 median (fixes Low 3).** Change the median's population from "the ranking's configurations" to "the ranking's main-workload configurations", with a `subsetRowsPresent` boolean; publish the subset median separately when non-empty. This keeps the companion consistent with E277's within-one-workload principle at zero cost while O36/O117 hold.

4. **Direction label on the E228 fallback (fixes Low 4).** A one-line registry note: "fallback window (publication − 29 d) is assumed; it can only over-flag supersession, never under-flag it", plus a `windowBasis: stated | fallback` field on each E228-blocked route so the published blocker list distinguishes the two.
