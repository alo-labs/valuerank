## Normative definitions (executable specification)

- **Coverage and scores.** Every ranking-specific definition below (scores, anchors, bars, intervals, scenario labels) is evaluated within one ranking, on that ranking's leaf table, anchors, bars and cohort rows (E253); the cohort rule and the leaf formulas are common to both. Leaf weights w_b are percent points summing to 100, and z and u are on 0–100. p_i = Σ_{b∈O_i} w_b / 100. Displayed observed-only quality Q̄_i = Σ_{b∈O_i} w_b z_ib / (100·p_i). Full-composite score Q_i(θ) = (Σ_{b∈O_i} w_b z_ib(θ) + Σ_{b∉O_i} w_b u_ib) / 100, u_ib ∈ [0,100] (fixture: leaves worth 30 points missing, every other leaf at 60 → p_i = 0.70, Q̄_i = 60, Q_i ∈ [42, 72]). **Formal bounds** (E35): Q_i^L = min_{θ∈Θ} Q_i(θ) and Q_i^U = max_{θ∈Θ} Q_i(θ) over the joint admissible set Θ below (measurement intervals of observed leaves and u_ib ∈ [0,100] for missing leaves); "missing at 0/100 at the point estimate" is not the bound. S0 uses Q̄_i; S1/S2 use the full-composite equation. Any sampling law for wholly missing leaves is a declared scenario convention (with one published alternative), never evidence; formal bounds are distribution-free.
- **Joint admissible set Θ (for interval outputs: frontier and bar).** Fixed: nominal weights, primary price rule, frozen route, linear GDPval map (E1). **Target (owner decision O25)**: interval outputs protect the **published record** — the benchmark results and costs as published for each exact configuration (for cost: the published deployment-equivalent cost of the fixed workload). They are robust to the record's reporting precision and to missing evidence, not to run-to-run noise. Benchmark repeatability and fitted-parameter uncertainty (published confidence intervals, run-to-run ranges) are outside the S0–S2 shares and are reported only by separately named streams whose documented estimand supports them (E145, E155; in v1, S1M as a conventional range sensitivity inside Terminal-Bench's published interval, dormant today, E162, E165, and S1T for task sampling, O39); token-use and price-basis variation enter only named S2 specification branches. **Uncertainty descriptions (E155)** are generated from the stream manifests and printed beside every uncertainty output (v1: S0 "published-record reporting-precision scenarios"; S1 "S0 plus conventional missing-evidence scenarios"; S2 core "S1 plus hierarchical weight stress"; S1M "S1 plus conventional range sensitivity inside Terminal-Bench's published 95% confidence interval" (or "dormant: equals S1", E236); S1T "published-record leaf values redrawn with task-sampling noise (independent-task approximation)"; other benchmark repeatability and fitted-parameter uncertainty "not quantified in v1 scenario shares"); a release-text lint rejects any statement that a source interval or run range enters scenario shares unless an active stream's allowlist admits that target. Variable in Θ: rounding intervals of published values at their published precision, missing-leaf and missing-component domains (identification), identified M_W ranges and other structural bounds (e.g. deployment-policy bias bounds). **Constraint basis (O19 under O25)**: each uncertainty object records constraint_basis (rounding of the record / published interval / public repeated runs / structural bound / analyst assumption / score domain), estimand, randomness_unit, constraint_target, coverage_claim and supporting_public_evidence (E46), and separates observed_statistic from target_parameter. **Target-compatibility gate (E49)**: an interval narrows a Θ variable only when its documented constraint_target is that published-record variable (or a public, justified mapping connects them); published confidence intervals and run ranges target other estimands and therefore never enter Θ; a full score domain expresses the absence of a narrower admissible constraint on a missing value. Analyst-assumed allowances never enter Θ. **Completeness (E11)**: every input, whatever its weight, enters Θ through a documented constraint or its full domain; an input with neither makes the affected output "unevaluable" for that row. Missing repeatability evidence never becomes zero uncertainty. Published benchmark intervals are consumed with their stated interpretation. Shared constraints (prices, donor measurements, task/run observations, quota pools) move jointly. With owner assumptions off, a Plan route that depends entirely on an owner assumption (an owner-named transfer or an O46 provider quota ceiling) is "unevaluable" in interval outputs (never silently replaced by API cost). Tests: widening Θ cannot move a row from "possible" to "definite" or create a confirmed result; evidence loss is tested in two stages (E38) — the result within R_W may change when a competitor leaves R_W, but the selected-cohort extension can never strengthen.
- **Plan measurement vs transport.** M_observed = eligible API-valued measured consumption ÷ gross cash paid (the measured workload). **M_W** ∈ [L, U] = the multiple for the scored workload W (O15 basket), bound to W's hash, the release price-list version and the quota-schema version; transport evidence for another workload qualifies only if its capacity model or conservative bound also covers W, with its identification mechanism recorded: direct compatible-workload measurement / publicly evidenced additive capacity model / evidenced transport bound / unidentified. The Plan headline score uses M_W where identified, else M_observed, the O88 same-model figure (O90) or an owner assumption labelled by kind (O16 flag). **Scalar convention (E74)**: a set-identified M_W ∈ [L, U] enters the displayed score at its lower bound L (the "lower-capacity-bound scoring convention", not an expected capacity), also when building the Plan anchor object; the joint Plan cost interval, the value-score interval and common-panel rank sensitivity under the upper-bound convention are published beside it. **Numerator eligibility (E75)**: eligible API-priced consumption is delivered model execution under the declared deployment policy, including policy-permitted retries; failed or rejected requests, platform overhead and evaluation-only repetitions are excluded. **Delivery is not outcome (E97)**: "failed request" means no delivered model execution (transport error, rejection before execution); delivered, policy-permitted execution stays eligible whether the task passes or fails, and a model-generated refusal is delivered execution — the same support as the API basket, whose cost covers every attempt regardless of success, so task failure is never penalised twice. Each record stores request_delivery_status, task_outcome, deployment_policy_role, token_observability, api_basket_treatment, plan_numerator_treatment and eligibility_reason, and U = Σ_r e_r p·t_r with e_r set by delivery and policy, never by outcome (fixtures: delivered pass and delivered fail both included; a permitted retry after a failed attempt — both included; rejected before execution — excluded; inseparable overhead — bounded or unidentified). Each measured consumption record carries execution status, deployment-policy role, token observability, eligibility (included / excluded / bounded) and evidence; where receipts cannot separate eligible from ineligible consumption, a supported bound is carried or the transport is unidentified (never a deduction from cohort averages). **Workload-demand evidence (E44)**: additive-capacity transport needs, per configuration and workload version, demand objects with machine-checkable units (E94): t_ie = category-k tokens per deployment-equivalent task of component e (input, cache read, cache write, reasoning, output); d_ie = n_e × t_ie per component pass, or a directly evidenced component-pass vector with its aggregation operator where the published aggregation is not an ordinary per-task mean (e.g. the Coding Agent Index's 290 × pooled category-token means); and d_iW = Σ_{e∈W} d_ie per complete workload pass, the only demand object the capacity solver accepts (fixture: a per-task vector supplied where a workload-pass vector is required is rejected by its units even when its entries satisfy another identity); each with execution stack, task support, category definitions, source precision and cache-policy compatibility, plus evidence that the capacity mechanism covers the relevant context lengths, tool use and effort (or conservative bounds). Where only partial token information is public, d_iW ranges over the feasible set D_iW and capacity is optimised over it: M_W^L = min_{d∈D, (a,B)∈A} M(d, a, B), M_W^U = max over the same set. A dollar total alone never identifies d. **Demand–cost reconciliation (E84)**: the demand object records aggregation_operator, task_attempt_support, category_telemetry_support, deployment_equivalent_multiplier, cost_reconciliation_residual and compatibility_status, and must reconcile to the scored cost — p_release · d_ie = C_i,e for every component and p_release · d_iW = C_i,W under compatible category definitions and the same aggregation and telemetry-support convention (for the Coding Agent Index, 290 × compatible pooled category-token means); a vector is never rescaled to force agreement; incompatible supports leave this transport mechanism unidentified without changing the displayed O27 cost or its completeness; without a demand object this transport mechanism is unidentified (the displayed score stays under O16). A feasible schedule certifies attainability conditional on its demand and quota inputs, not those inputs. **Plan interval outputs use only an identified M_W**; otherwise the row's Plan cost is "unidentified" for those outputs (it stays scored and ranked). Cohort spread or arbitrary bands never convert "unidentified" into a bound.
