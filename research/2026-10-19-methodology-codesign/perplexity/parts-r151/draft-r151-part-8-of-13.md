## Attempts reconciliation

The manifest records separately, per leaf: independent evaluation repetitions (e.g. Bug Hunt's mean over N published runs — affects precision only) and deployment attempt policy (attempts permitted per task, retry/best-of-k/selection/verifier policy) with the cost coverage of those attempts. Only a genuine deployment-policy mismatch between the quality figure and the priced attempts is reconciled (as a bias bound in the identification layer); repetitions are not a bias.

## Uncertainty (three separate layers, one scenario engine)

A joint scenario simulation (N = 10,000) is the single engine. **Primitive sampling (E83)**: scenarios draw the primitive variables of the constrained evidence model (E73) under the selected branch and then derive costs, capacities, multiples, quality and value; derived quantities (e.g. Plan cost = C_W / M_W = F / λ under compatible identified transport) are never drawn independently; every scenario must pass the applicable identities and feasibility checks; infeasible combinations are never silently rejected and redrawn unless that conditional sampling law is itself declared; where public evidence supports no joint sampling mechanism, the branch publishes bounds or is unevaluable (fixtures: C/M = F/λ; gross cash changing with consumption under an evidenced payment rule; shared donor and price primitives propagating to every affected row; individually admissible draws that violate a joint constraint). **Cost is drawn too**: published rounding supports reporting-precision draws, and public paired per-task or per-run cost observations for the exact configuration support resampling at their evidenced level; an analyst-selected token-use range belongs only in a named S2 stress branch, and without supporting observations repeatability is reported as "not quantified", never zero and never an invented S0 distribution (E134; today S0 cost draws are reporting precision only, and the build rejects an S0 primitive whose parameter_derivation cites only an analyst convention). **Joint rounding law (E154)**: rounding is a conventional treatment, not an empirical error distribution. The build compiles each connected constraint component of rounded published records (records linked by an accounting identity, e.g. rounded component costs and a rounded aggregate) and gives it a registered joint-law object (constraint_component_id, published field IDs, free source coordinates, joint_law_id, sampler_version, sampling_status). A singleton component keeps its independent uniform kernel (arcsine alternative); a derived quantity is the identity applied to its drawn primitives — a basket cost is its exact components plus each rounded component's own draw, never one uniform on the summed interval; an affine-linked component uses the registered v1 convention, uniform volume on the feasible polytope in its declared free coordinates, sampled only by an explicitly registered exact algorithm within its resource limit; without a registered law, or beyond that limit, the component keeps deterministic bounds and the affected frequencies are withheld as "joint rounding law not evaluated" — feasibility alone never activates sampling (fixture: two rounded components and a rounded aggregate, every draw inside all three reporting intervals and satisfying the identity; equivalent accounting representations give the same derived-cost distribution). In v1 the admitted basket's costs are captured at source precision (AA's per-evaluation cost per task and the Coding Agent Index mean are unrounded) and the quality leaves are separately published records, so every compiled component is a singleton; aggregates (AA's Index total, the Coding Agent Index published sum) are deterministic validation identities, never sampling constraints, and the build fails if a multi-record component lacks a registered law. AA's published cost already embeds AA's cache-pricing protocol (registry: protocol and date), so cache-hit variation is a **named S2 specification-stress branch** around that protocol, not an S0 redraw (E34). **Layer membership (E122)**: S0 and S1 use the primary price rule only; the first-party-list versus cross-provider-median comparison is a named S2 specification branch with shared price primitives and all demand–cost identities kept within the branch; each stochastic primitive carries uncertainty_layer (S0 reporting precision or supported measurement variation under the primary rule / S1 missing evidence or identified transport / S2 alternative price rule, cache policy, quality mapping or weights / bounds-only), validated at build time so a methodological alternative never enters a measurement-labelled stratum. Cost and AA-run leaf values are coupled only as the Dependence rule allows (paired public observations; otherwise dependence stress branches). **Stream allowlist (E145)**: each stream's registered target profile is an explicit allowlist and takes precedence over the generic sampling instructions in this section — a primitive enters a stream only when the profile names its target. For v1: S0 varies only the published records' reporting precision (cost and leaf rounding); S1 adds the declared conventional identification draws (missing-leaf domains; Plan transport and transfer bounds only where identified — none today);
