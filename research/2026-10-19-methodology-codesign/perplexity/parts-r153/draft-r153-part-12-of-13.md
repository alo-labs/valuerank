
## Governance

Evidence classes (measured / claim / estimate / assumption); provider claims never overwrite independent measurements. a model-availability change flags the plan's routes and splits nothing, owner decision O105, 2026-10-28, and its flag only prints the label: it blocks no label under E228 and gives no report pair precedence under O78, owner decision O107, 2026-10-28; every controlled measurement on a plan a record covers carries the earliest such record dated on or after the earliest day it can have been taken as its label date and, when they differ, the earliest limit, price or credit record from there on as its separate blocking date (none when only an availability change flags it), both counting only records dated by the release cutoff, so an earlier availability change never cancels a later limit or price change; a bundled credit grant, such as API credits added to a plan, flags and blocks like a limit change but splits no report partition, as the meter users read did not change (owner decision O111, 2026-10-29); and a measurement's span starts no earlier than its model's launch on the plan or the plan schedule it says it ran under (owner decision O110, 2026-10-29), a change dated on that start day being part of the measured regime (an editorial ruling, E18); E18) and a 90-day hard expiry; after a further 30-day grace an expired route is **demoted to diagnostic-only** and the model falls back to API cost (flagged) in the official Plan table (O13), labelled "cross-estimand ordering not identified" and excluded from Plan interval outputs (E36). **Release-time route order (E142)**: every measurement object is classified for eligibility and expiry first; Each row stores its AA price-list date; score changes are decomposed into price change vs token-use change vs **AA cache-rate update** (AA's Index category costs show a changed cache-read share with unchanged token totals and prices; E176) vs **benchmark-record update** (refit, re-anchoring or regrading of a published value with no new task execution; E90) vs method vs cohort, by a declared sequential bridge where effects interact; a component public evidence cannot separate is labelled "benchmark-record update — decomposition unavailable". **Swing attribution (E177)**: the bridge replays the build from the two releases' evidence bundles (E159), one input class at a time in the declared order, and publishes per row each step's change, the order-dependence term (forward minus reverse ordering) and the replay residual (the replayed final score minus the published score); a score change is attributed only when the replay residual is within 10⁻⁶ points and every step carrying more than 5 points names the changed bundle fields (artifact and field locator) or the versioned method or cohort change behind it. A benchmark-record update whose changed published field is named is attributed even when its decomposition is unavailable (E90). The gate compares rows ranked in both releases; a row ranked only in the new release is published as "cohort admission (no prior score)" and one ranked only in the old release as "cohort exit", and neither is held by this gate. The scenario engine uses a published RNG seed and draw order per release, and per-stratum outputs are released as data files. Plan rows state that the multiple values the measured workload's API-equivalent consumption only (other plan features are not valued) and publish the fee period, the cash paid (subscription fee plus declared additional cash, F_gross) and the selected multiple's API-priced amount M × F_gross so light users can judge fit, typed by the selected measurement object (E166): amount_kind measured-workload capacity / observed eligible consumption, capacity not established / assumed API-equivalent consumption (owner-named transfer, donor named) / API-equivalent consumption measured on the same model at another effort (O88, O90, donor named) / identified target-workload capacity (bound to W) / provider quota ceiling, the full quota at API rates (O46, full use assumed), with the capacity status (E139), valuation status (E138) and an interpretation label generated from those fields; API-fallback rows print no Plan amount, and the amount is never called capacity unless the measurement establishes it. **Amount lineage (E168)**: every amount records amount_target (measured-workload consumption / measured-workload capacity / assumed consumption / target-workload capacity / full-quota consumption for an O46 ceiling; an O88 same-model route, graded measured under O90, records measured-workload consumption, its donor's target), workload_id, measurement_object_id, capacity_solution_id, cash_object_id, purchase_policy_id and derivation (direct observation / solver projection / authorised assumption, a donor's measured multiple / same-model measurement, the multiple measured at another effort (O88, O90) / quota ceiling, the tier's credits at the provider's API rate / valid multiplication). M × F reproduces an amount only within one object: the measurement record U_obs = M_obs × F_obs, or fixed positive cash U_W* = M_W × F_0. A target-workload capacity is the capacity solver's U_W* (bounds are the projections of the joint solution set, never products of separately projected multiple and cash endpoints; the displayed lower-bound multiple is a scoring convention), and is never the displayed multiple times cash from another object; when the target solution's cash lineage is not established, the measured amount stays publishable and no target capacity is manufactured (fixtures: fixed cash reproduces target capacity; consumption-dependent cash rejects cross-object multiplication; set identification by projection; missing target cash lineage). A compatibility join of each observation's basis with the price records in force at V (E141) returns exactly one of four outcomes: (1) compatible as captured; (2) compatible after evidenced revaluation, which is exact only when every priced category and band with positive demand in the component moves by one common factor k (then C' = k·C for any demand mix; a category has zero demand only where a published category total shows it) or when E157's valuation sufficiency supplies the component's demand by category and band; (3) AA's applied price not in force at V (O29): the row keeps AA's cost at the price AA applied, flagged "AA cost at superseded price" (or "AA cost at standard price during a promotion", E141) with that price's effective dates, and stays in the value ranking; it earns no interval label of its own and competes in other rows' interval outputs over the hull of its published record and its current-tariff bound below, so it can block but never enable another row's label; its scenario outputs use its published record and carry the flag; it is never a reference in anchor derivation (E160) or in the measured-evidence calibration (E164); and its Plan cost divides only by a multiple valued on the same basis (E138's statuses are evaluated against the row's basis: an exact measurement valued at the new tariff is revalued to the row's basis under outcome 2 or is diagnostic-only; an owner-named transfer or an O88 same-model route needs a donor measurement valued on price records in force during the applied price's effective dates; when no route survives, the row falls back to API cost, flagged, O13).
