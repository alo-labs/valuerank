## Known limitations

Full-utilisation Plan cost overstates value for light users (workday diagnostic shown); Plan rows of providers with no authorised or admitted route are priced at API cost (O72; today 2 of the max-effort ranking's 27 plan rows, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (max) and Gemini 4 Argon (high), and 3 of the AI Coding Daily ranking's 51, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (max), Gemini 4 Argon (high) and Kimi K3.1 Pro), so the Plan order mixes route availability with value; and most plan rows rest on a route flagged possibly superseded or expired (21 of 27 and 44 of 51), and some on a measurement taken inside an acknowledged accounting-fault window that no user report pair has yet replaced (3 and 8; O91, O92); the Value Multiple values only the API-equivalent consumption of the measured workload, so plan features outside it (chat, image generation, other surfaces, pools shared with them) are not valued; AA cost uses AA's token mix and cache protocol; Terminal-Bench work is priced in two executions; Bug Hunt covers two repositories; Bug Hunt, AI Coding Daily and a tbench.ai gap fill measure model+harness stacks, and AI Coding Daily's harness differs by model; suite is primarily English text; ITBench-AA remains pending until complete exact-variant cost evidence covers two-thirds of a ranking's cohort rows jointly with the admitted components (E95, E101), and the Coding Agent Index v2.0 is pending in the AI Coding Daily ranking (today 3 of 16, E254), so that ranking's cost basket holds no coding-agent component unless FrontierCode's own chart total, which stands in for the index while it is pending (owner decision O116, 2026-10-29), is admitted (today it covers 5 of 16, so it is not); Terminal-Bench 4.0's Index run is its one agentic coding evaluation, and its coding share is far lower than the max-effort ranking's. Independent software-engineering benchmarks run few efforts per model (LiveBench 2.0 and Vals once per model at its top effort, AI Coding Daily mostly at high and medium), so neither ranking covers the cohort: today the max-effort ranking ranks 9 of the 30 cohort configurations, the AI Coding Daily ranking 16, and 9 are in neither. The two rankings use different leaves, weights, anchors and bars, so their values, ranks, bands and bar results are not comparable, and a configuration ranked in both has two values. Each leaf's spread over the ranked rows is published with the non-discriminating test (scores.json `diagnostics.qualityDispersion`). MiMo-V2.6-Pro's added leaves rest on the owner's statement that its single reasoning mode is AA's high configuration (O54, E252), and Kimi K3.1 Pro's on the owner's decision that its effort-less AA configuration is max, its documented maximum and default (O66). Instruction following has no weight while Surge's Complex Constraints is excluded (O53). On the Plan basis the MiMo multiples are provider-derived quota ceilings, not measurements (O46).
