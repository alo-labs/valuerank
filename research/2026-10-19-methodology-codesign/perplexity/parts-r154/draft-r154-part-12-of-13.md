Each row stores its AA price-list date; score changes are decomposed into price change vs token-use change vs **AA cache-rate update** (AA's Index category costs show a changed cache-read share with unchanged token totals and prices; E176) vs **benchmark-record update** (refit, re-anchoring or regrading of a published value with no new task execution; E90) vs method vs cohort, by a declared sequential bridge where effects interact; a component public evidence cannot separate is labelled "benchmark-record update — decomposition unavailable". **Swing attribution (E177)**: the bridge replays the build from the two releases' evidence bundles (E159), one input class at a time in the declared order, and publishes per row each step's change, the order-dependence term (forward minus reverse ordering) and the replay residual (the replayed final score minus the published score); a score change is attributed only when the replay residual is within 10⁻⁶ points and every step carrying more than 5 points names the changed bundle fields (artifact and field locator) or the versioned method or cohort change behind it; for a swing over that gate, so must the largest steps that together carry 80% of it, so a swing built from several smaller steps is never attributed without a named cause (E275).
