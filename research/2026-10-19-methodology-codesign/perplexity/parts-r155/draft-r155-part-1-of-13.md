# ValueRank methodology — draft r155

*Where prose and the Normative definitions differ, the Normative definitions govern. Every rule tagged with an owner (O) or editor (E) number is normative wherever it appears; between two tagged rules an owner decision governs an editor decision, and a rule that amends another governs it (E191); an editor record never amends or overrides an owner decision; between two editor rules where neither amends the other, the later-numbered record governs, and finding such a conflict is a defect corrected in the next revision. Each E number names one decision record; a record that holds several rules labels them, and an amendment cites the record it amends. The valuation outcome (4) once tagged E198 is E202.*

## Objective and claim scope

AA's Pareto frontier of Intelligence Index vs. total USD cost to run its Intelligence Index does not answer the question ValueRank exists for: **which frontier AI model is actually lowest cost for the value it delivers in practice**, once (a) subscription-plan economics are considered and (b) benchmarks geared to real software-engineering work are weighed alongside general capability. ValueRank serves two buyers, ranked separately: **API buyers** (pay per token, incl. third-party agents) and **Plan buyers** (provider subscription apps/CLIs: Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT, SuperGrok…).

Claim scope: "value among the configurations ranked in one ranking (configurations AA plots on, or within the near gap of, its rendered frontier that have that ranking's full benchmark set; see Cohort), under that ranking's published benchmark mix and evidence". On the Plan basis a frontier or bar claim also needs identified routes (E61, E67); without them the Plan ranking is exploratory arithmetic, not a claim. ValueRank publishes two rankings (owner decision O53): the **max-effort ranking**, which leads (site and README default, O54), and the **AI Coding Daily ranking**. Each has its own leaf set and weights (O55), anchors, bars, scenario run, basket admission and diagnostics (E253, E254); values, ranks and claims compare within one ranking only, never across the two. No configuration is ranked for its evidence alone outside the frontier and near-frontier cohort (O51). Every ranked row is a **configuration** (model version, effort, fallback, route), with a compact configuration ID and an expandable per-leaf execution manifest; tables and claims say "configuration", not "model" (E78). Not a market-wide optimum across all configurations and workloads.

   - **Noise band (owner decision O42)**: beside each ordinal rank the ranking prints the row's band by a fixed rule (every row is inseparable from its band's first row; adjacent rows in different bands may also be inseparable): rows in value order, a row joins the current band while its value is not separable from the band's first row at correlation 0, otherwise it starts a new band; on the Plan basis a band that starts at one of a configuration's plan rows extends forward over its other plan rows (never back over rows already placed in an earlier band), a convention that keeps a configuration's plan rows together in the band order (E248); should one configuration's plan rows ever span more than the separation threshold, a row of another configuration that the first row can be told apart from starts a new band as usual, so the invariant that every row is inseparable from its band's first row holds for every row of another configuration; configurations whose plan rows span that far today: none (max-effort ranking), none (AI Coding Daily ranking).    - **Dispersion convention (E180)**: every cohort SD and variance in this methodology (within-cohort influence, the non-discriminating threshold, drift spreads, covariance allocation) is the population statistic over the declared panel (divisor n), because the panel is the population being described, not a sample from a larger one; each statistic has one minimum panel and one status string below it: an SD or SD ratio for cost influence needs two rows and otherwise reads "undefined (panel too small)"; the non-discriminating test needs two observed rows for a leaf and otherwise leaves the leaf unmarked, with no status; the drift flag, the normalised weighted spread and the group covariance allocation need three rows and otherwise read "not estimable (insufficient panel)"; the value-variance allocation (E189) needs three rows and is otherwise withheld (null), and a τ_b agreement needs three rows (eight for the cost cross-check) and otherwise reads "undefined (panel too small)"; scenario dispersion (E87) is likewise the population variance over the stream's scenarios. Subset-workload strata get their own statistic (undefined below two rows); a statistic over the whole displayed table mixes anchor objects and may be shown only as "displayed-table dispersion"; a zero denominator returns "undefined — no composite dispersion"; a Plan panel of several plan rows from one configuration reads "undefined (one configuration)", because its rows share one quality and the split would be fixed by construction. A release is flagged (not held) when cost's share of the common-workload influence, 0.25·SD(S_C) / (0.25·SD(S_C) + 0.75·SD(S_Q)), is under 10% ("cost not discriminating") or above 90% ("quality not discriminating", E126); the exchange rate of every anchor version is published as a series, and a trimmed-anchor diagnostic (10th and 90th percentile reference costs with the same ln 2 padding) reports rank shifts only, never replacing the primary anchors; the flag is informational and β never changes (owner decision O2). Because cost and quality covary across the cohort, the SD ratio describes dispersion, not contribution, so the same panel also publishes the **value-variance allocation (E189)** Cov(0.25·S_C, V)/Var(V) and Cov(0.75·Q, V)/Var(V), which sum to 1 (withheld below three rows, and for a Plan stratum of one configuration's plan rows, where in both cases the split is fixed by construction; today the max-effort ranking's 1-row API subset stratum reads "undefined (panel too small)" and the max-effort ranking's 4-row Plan subset stratum reads "undefined (one configuration)"), and the τ_b of the cost-only and quality-only orderings against the value ordering (informational; the flag is unchanged).
   - **Nominal weight beside influence (E196)**: the headline calls the 25% cost weight nominal and prints cost's value-variance allocation for each basis beside it (generated each release); a negative allocation (or one above 100%, which makes the other term negative) is printed as it is, fires the informational manifest rule "realised influence (E272)" naming the basis and the offsetting term (the E126 SD ratio lies strictly between its extremes and cannot show it; β never changes, O2), with the sentence that dearer configurations score higher on quality, so the term with the smaller spread offsets the other instead of adding to it (today the max-effort ranking's cost allocation is 106% API and 21% Plan, and the AI Coding Daily ranking's -96% API and -25% Plan); the anchors are not recalibrated to equalise influence, because the weight is owner policy (2026-10-19) and the anchors are frozen evidence ranges, and the disclosure never changes a score. ## Source contract (versioned registry)

**Evidence bundle (E159)**: each release is (method version, registry, evidence bundle, extractor versions, outputs). The bundle lists every captured artifact the build reads — source payloads or permitted captures, committed with the release — with its content hash, capture time and extractor, plus a bundle hash; every scored observation records its artifact, field locator and published value. Rebuilding from the bundle at the pinned publication clock must reproduce the admitted components, route selection, anchors, scores and deterministic interval statuses without consulting live pages (and the scenario outputs under the recorded RNG, seed and draw order); where redistribution is restricted, the permitted capture is retained and its hash and locator published. Machine-readable registry per release: AA chart identity, filters and snapshot timestamp (current: "Intelligence Index vs. Cost to Run Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index", x-axis "Cost to Run Intelligence Index (USD, Log Scale)", unit "USD to run all Intelligence Index evaluations", captured 2026-10-25T09:53:28Z for the release cutoff O65 set, the previous capture (2026-10-23) before it); **frontier membership extraction (E195)**: membership source = the rendered Pareto SVG path's vertices matched to the plotted circle centres (tolerance 0.5 px; today all 19 vertices matched at 0 px, as they did at the 2026-10-17 capture), the capture procedure (public chart and tooltips only) and its version; the build fails unless every captured frontier point sits on a path vertex, one point per vertex, and the path rises strictly in chart coordinates (cost and Index both increasing). Two cases are kept apart. Overlapping circles off the line are separated at capture by hovering each and recording the resolution, as the 2026-10-17 capture did for GLM-5.2 and GPT-5.5 (at the current capture every circle returned a distinct tooltip at a 1600 px viewport, so no pair needed resolving). Two circles at one vertex (an exact cost-and-Index tie) are separated the same way; if hovering cannot separate them, the vertex check fails and the build stops until the owner records a decision. Rounded tooltip values tie (today Claude Haiku 5.5 (high), GPT-6 Luna (max) and MiMo-V2.6-Flash all display 38; Claude Opus 5.5 (xhigh with fallback) and Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max with fallback) both display 56) and are never used to recompute or check membership; the near rule's rounded-Index gaps are measured against these points; cost field, unit and accounting (as AA defines it, including AA's cache-pricing protocol and its date); per model: version, effort, fallback, serving route and per-benchmark stack; per benchmark: version, raw metric, **pinned source field**, population, attempts, aggregation, source revision/commit, observation and retrieval dates; explicit missingness reason (not evaluated / incompatible variant / no public evidence / withdrawn). **Withdrawn benchmark (E250)**: AA withdrew DeepSWE v1.1 from its Coding Agent Index (O49); its chart and per-configuration results stay in the raw data as "withdrawn by AA, not scored", and no DeepSWE result enters quality, sampling noise, cohort membership or any diagnostic. Pinned fields today (E31): AutomationBench-AA = AA field `automationBenchPartialScore` (the value AA's Index uses); Omniscience = AA's Omniscience Index field `omniscience` (O28), with `omniscienceBreakdown.accuracy` and `omniscienceBreakdown.hallucinationRate` kept as diagnostics and reconciliation checks; Coding Agent Index cost = AA's per-task cost-to-run `mean.costUsd` × the 290 required tasks of v2.0 (O27, O50), with "Total Cost Across the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index" (`sums.costUsd`) kept as a reconciliation diagnostic (E104); Bug Hunt = run results and means published by the benchmark's maintainer (Bug Hunt Bench, github.com/phuryn/bug-hunt-bench, Product Compass), which ValueRank does not run; the leaves the two rankings add (O53) = the records of `data/swe_tracks.json` (E257): Vals AI's per-model value and standard error for Vibe Code Bench v1.1 and Code Migration v1.1, LiveBench 2.0 Overall, LiveBench's own weighted mean of its seven category scores from its release table (release 2026-10-23, O61, O67), and AI Coding Daily's Laravel-quality, React-TS-quality and edge-case columns (60 points; the 10-point bug-finding column excluded). A change of AA chart or field is a versioned methodology migration, never a silent refresh.

## Cohort and configuration (fixed policy)

- **Cohort (owner decision O51, 2026-10-23)**: the cohort is every configuration AA plots on its rendered frontier or within the near gap of it (rounded Index within 3 points of the frontier's best at equal or lower cost; the gap stays on AA's plotted Index, the one public composite plotted against cost for every configuration, owner decision O129, 2026-10-30), each plotted effort its own row: today 30 configurations at the cutoff (19 on the rendered frontier, 11 near it) from 13 families (8 with points on the rendered frontier, 5 with near points only). Every other catalog configuration carries the exclusion reason "not plotted on or within the near gap of AA's rendered frontier" (E251). Asked why configurations outside the frontier and near-frontier families were ranked, the owner asked "Why is that at all required?" and chose "Frontier + near only". The retention rules are withdrawn: E163, the retention half of E187, O31 and O33's evidenced-effort rows. A configuration off the frontier and the near gap is not ranked whatever evidence it has, so the retention route, its roster check against the Bug Hunt scoreboard and its leaving rule no longer exist; Claude Fable 5.1, Muse Spark 1.3, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, which entered through O31, left the cohort with O51.
- **Two rankings (owner decisions O52, O53, O54)**: ValueRank picks the benchmarks and ranks the configurations that have all of them, balancing the number of benchmarks against the number of configurations ranked; the cohort does not set the spread of benchmarks (O52). No benchmark outside AA covers the cohort at exact effort (`research/2026-10-23-swe-benchmarks/reports/SWE benchmark candidates.md`), so ValueRank publishes two rankings, each over the cohort configurations that have its complete leaf set (Quality): the **max-effort ranking** (no rule limits it to one effort per family; it holds one configuration per family at the vendor's top effort only because LiveBench 2.0 and Vals run each model once, at its top effort, with MiMo-V2.6-Pro at high, O54; today 9 configurations from 9 of the 13 families), which leads on the site and in the README (O54), and the **AI Coding Daily ranking** (mostly high and medium efforts; today 16). Today 4 configurations are in both (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (max), GPT-6 Luna (max), Gemini 4 Argon (high) and MiMo-V2.6-Pro), so 21 of the 30 are ranked at least once. Each ranking has its own leaf table and weights (O55), frozen anchors, quality bars, scenario run, basket admission and diagnostics, scored by the same engine (E253, E254); values compare within a ranking only. **Common-leaf diagnostic ranking (owner decision O121, 2026-10-29)**: because the two leading rankings cannot rank the cheaper efforts that LiveBench, Vals and AI Coding Daily never run, a third ranking scores every cohort configuration with AA's leaves and Bug Hunt, at any effort, on the ten leaves the leading rankings share, at the O55 weights (FrontierCode 25, Terminal-Bench 20, Bug Hunt 15 and the seven leaves outside software engineering). Today it ranks 28 configurations, 7 of them ranked in neither leading ranking: Claude Haiku 5.5 (low), Claude Haiku 5.5 (xhigh), Claude Opus 5.5 (xhigh), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (xhigh), GPT-6 Astra (xhigh), GPT-6 Luna (low) and GPT-6.1 Sol (xhigh). It has its own frozen anchors, quality bars, basket admission and scenario run, but it is diagnostic: it earns no interval or scenario label, its gates are disclosed but never hold the release, a configuration ranked only there stays listed (not ranked) in ValueRank, and a model page never makes it its home. For each bar position and basis it publishes its displayed-record cheapest row beside each leading ranking's answer at the same position (each ranking's bar levels are its own), with whether they differ (scores.json `v3Tracks.common.leadingComparison`). **Admission per ranking (owner decision O57, implementing E254)**: O15 and E140 admit a component when its exact cost covers two-thirds of "the cohort"; with two rankings this is two-thirds of the ranking's own cohort rows, because each ranking is scored on its own rows and anchors. The choice changes the result: today the Coding Agent Index v2.0 is priced for 8 of the max-effort ranking's 9 rows, so it is admitted there, while under a whole-cohort reading it would have stayed pending (the counts at the decision are in the ledger, O57). **Per-ranking full set (E255)**: a cohort configuration is ranked in every ranking whose full set it has, and it is excluded from ValueRank, listed under O38 with its searches, only when it lacks every ranking's set (today 9: Claude Haiku 5.5 (low), Claude Haiku 5.5 (xhigh), Claude Opus 5.5 (xhigh), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (xhigh), GPT-6 Astra (xhigh), GPT-6 Luna (low), GPT-6.1 Sol (xhigh), MiMo-V2.6-Flash and MiniMax-M3.1-Flash). Each ranking also lists the cohort configurations without its own set, with their missing leaves (today 21 beside the max-effort ranking and 14 beside the AI Coding Daily ranking). Every missing (configuration, added leaf) pair carries a logged search whose open-web step is the coverage research (`research/2026-10-23-swe-benchmarks/add_track_searches.py`).
