

## Known limitations

Full-utilisation Plan cost overstates value for light users (workday diagnostic shown); Plan rows of providers with no authorised or admitted route are priced at API cost (O72; today 0 of the max-effort ranking's 19 plan rows, none, and 0 of the AI Coding Daily ranking's 63, none), so the Plan order mixes route availability with value; and most plan rows rest on a route flagged possibly superseded (or, past the lifecycle's hard expiry, expired; 16 of 19 and 54 of 63), and some on a measurement taken inside an acknowledged accounting-fault window that no user report pair has yet replaced (3 and 12; O91, O92); the Value Multiple values only the API-equivalent consumption of the measured workload, so plan features outside it (chat, image generation, other surfaces, pools shared with them) are not valued; AA cost uses AA's token mix and cache protocol; Terminal-Bench work is priced in two executions; Bug Hunt covers two repositories; Bug Hunt, AI Coding Daily and a tbench.ai gap fill measure model+harness stacks, and AI Coding Daily's harness differs by model; suite is primarily English text; ITBench-AA remains pending until complete exact-variant cost evidence covers two-thirds of a ranking's cohort rows jointly with the admitted components (E95, E101), and the Coding Agent Index v2.1 is pending in the AI Coding Daily ranking (today 2 of 15, E254), so that ranking's cost basket holds no coding-agent unit (O137 retired FrontierCode's O116 stand-in); the Index's Terminal-Bench 4.1 and FrontierCode 2.0 runs are its agentic coding evaluations, and its coding share is far lower than the max-effort ranking's. Pending basket components do not themselves remove label eligibility (owner decision O133, 2026-10-30), and its summary discloses the admitted basket and both pending units with their coverage; separately, while the AI Coding Daily ranking's like-for-like cost check (basket cost against Terminal-Bench 4.1 cost per attempted task, O183) is below 0.5 or cannot be computed (O187), O182 withholds its confirmed-cheapest interval labels and scenario-supported cheapest labels only; its frontier and qualification outputs, evaluated answers, displayed-record answers and diagnostics remain published. Independent software-engineering benchmarks run few efforts per model (LiveBench 2.0 and Vals once per model at its top effort, AI Coding Daily mostly at high and medium), so neither ranking covers the cohort: today the max-effort ranking ranks 5 of the 45 cohort configurations, the AI Coding Daily ranking 15, and 26 are in neither. Each ranking publishes the share of its cohort it ranks beside the previous release's share and flags a drop, informational and never a hold (owner decision O166, 2026-11-01, on reviewer 83; scores.json `rankingCoverage`), so a benchmark withdrawal cannot shrink a ranking without a rule noticing; the first v3 release has no previous share to compare. The two rankings use different leaves, weights, anchors and bars, so their values, ranks, bands and bar results are not comparable, and a configuration ranked in both has two values; as a descriptive check only, the τ_b of the two rankings' API value orders over the configurations ranked in both is published (scores.json `v3.rankingAgreement`, E274; on a handful of shared configurations τ_b takes only a few values, so it is published from eight shared configurations, E41's floor, and otherwise reads "undefined (panel too small)" with the panel; today the check reads undefined (panel too small) on a panel of 1, GPT-6 Luna (max)). Each leaf's spread over the ranked rows is published with the non-discriminating test (scores.json `diagnostics.qualityDispersion`). MiMo-V2.6-Flash's match to AI Coding Daily rests on Xiaomi's documentation that thinking enabled, its default, is its one reasoning mode (O135, O140), not on an effort AA publishes; it is listed, not ranked, while its Bug Hunt run stays unmatched. Instruction following has no weight while Surge's Complex Constraints is excluded (O53). On the Plan basis the MiMo multiples are provider-derived quota ceilings, not measurements (O46). The staged report search could not reach Reddit directly (it read an indexed mirror), X (HTTP 402) or YouTube video pages, and it read LINUX DO, where many end-user reports for Kimi, Xiaomi and MiniMax plans are posted, only through a mirror; each search's reached and unreachable sources are recorded in the registry's search log, so a "no concordant pair" outcome for those providers partly reflects where the search could look; the owner decided that search reach stays fully automated, so no owner-run manual pass counts as a stage and the unreachable sources stay disclosed as a limit (owner decision O197). OpenAI's global meter resets came about every two days while they lasted, so a weekly reading over that period almost always spans one (the registered resets, each named by its provider: OpenAI at 2026-10-07, OpenAI at 2026-10-10, OpenAI at 2026-10-12, OpenAI at 2026-10-14, OpenAI at 2026-10-17, OpenAI at 2026-10-19, OpenAI at 2026-10-21, OpenAI at 2026-10-22, OpenAI at 2026-10-24, OpenAI at 2026-10-27 and Google at 2026-11-17; the OpenAI series ends with its last entry); under O156 such a reading counts when every reset it spans is a registered record, and carries the flag "measured across documented meter resets", so a flagged OpenAI route can again be replaced by a report pair (O78, O93) before demotion; a reset the registry does not record cannot be detected (a reset an account buys or banks for itself pulls its allowance forward and adds none, OpenAI's help article on paid Work and Codex resets, so it is an account event and not a provider-wide registry record, and a reading that spans one carries the same limit), which is why the reset records are re-read with the rest of the change registry each release.
