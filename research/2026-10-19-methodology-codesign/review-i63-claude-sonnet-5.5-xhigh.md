## 2. Methodology design recommendations

Prioritised. All use public data refreshed routinely.

1. **Single-source the Plan evidence vocabulary (L1, L2).** Generate the E212 table's trigger and comparison cells, the E85 grade list and line 53's schedule from `plan_routes` (`GRADE`, `POST_CHANGE_SUPERSEDED`, `SOFT_EXPIRY_DAYS`, `HARD_EXPIRY_DAYS`, `GRACE_DAYS`) and from the registry's `changeDetection`, as E265 does for figures; add the O77 trigger to the E18 text once. Make `route_selection` take a plan scope and return per-plan choices; define the configuration-level choice as the best of them.
2. **Put the report-eligibility profile in the registry (L3).** A versioned `reportEligibilityProfile` with the per-report fields the rules use: `observer`, `modelBasis` (stated / inferred) and `tierBasis` (stated / inferred), `meterTie` (fraction / exhaustion / none), `window` and `bindingWindow`, `values[]` with `lo` and `hi`, `speedMode`, and the E194 date. Hash it into the evidence bundle. `concordant_pool` then pools the union of values, so the whole-range rule is the ordinary max / min ≤ 1.20; the build rejects `meterTie: none`; a figure from a non-binding shorter window is stored unconverted and never pooled with a monthly figure. Add registry lists of acknowledged accounting-fault days and of detected or documented change dates, and let the partition builder split at them. Fixtures: an observer with two values, a range-valued report, an inferred model version, a pair straddling a registered change, a 5-hour figure on a plan with a binding weekly cap.
3. **Show the exposure of flagged routes (L4, L5).** I62's recommendation 4 is unrecorded in the ledger; what is new is that the flagged set now holds every ChatGPT route and both Claude Max plans (18 of 27 and 40 of 51 plan rows). At the max-effort Plan anchors (ratio 1,219; 2.44 value points per cost doubling; median threshold 2.76; tolerance 2.19×) I scaled every flagged multiple together and re-ranked: ×0.8 (SemiAnalysis's reported −20% A/B arm) and ×0.5 each change the position of 3 of 27 rows (the largest move is two places) and leave rank 1 (Claude Opus 5.5 (max) on Claude Pro) in place; ×0.2 (the roughly 5× per-token step in claude-code #102667) moves 20 of 27 and lifts Claude Haiku 5.5 (max) on Pro to third. Print, for each flagged row, the multiple factor at which its value moves by the median threshold (the E242 arithmetic, applied to flagged controlled routes as well as to †-marked assumptions) and mark such rows ‡ beside †. This is a presentation of E242, outside Θ (O19) and never a label.
4. **Make the manifest true by construction (L5).** Register each gate and flag with a decorator (`@gate("O64", …)`), derive the manifest from the registry of gates, and build the lifecycle entry from plan rows.
5. **Record Terminal-Bench's attempt structure (S1T).** All 61 `terminalBench40` values in the captured snapshot are multiples of 1/198 (66 tasks × 3 attempts; 22 are also multiples of 1/99, 19 of 1/66), so n = 66 is the wide reading, as 6 is for AI Coding Daily. If AA documents three attempts per task, publish a narrow reading at n = 198 beside the separability figures, as E263 does for AI Coding Daily, so the 11–17% Terminal-Bench leaf has a bracket like its neighbours. This is my inference from the granularity, to be confirmed against AA's methodology page.
6. **Date the change detections by their evidence.** Keep the post-id decode for X sources (the Day 7 post is 2026-10-11T23:45:59Z) in the registry beside each `changeDetectedOn`, and state the same-day rule once (a report dated on a change's day counts as before it unless its recorded instant is later, as O69 does for cutoff-day rows).
