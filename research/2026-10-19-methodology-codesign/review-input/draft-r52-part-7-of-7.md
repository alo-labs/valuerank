- **Plan capacity under multiple pools.** Where public controlled measurements establish additive consumption per budget w (a_wk per token of category k, spendable capacity B_w over the fee period) and d_k = tokens of category k in one complete workload pass (d_iW, E94): λ_w = B_w / Σ_k a_wk d_k, λ = min_w λ_w (workload passes per fee period), M = λ × C_i,W / F_gross, where B_w must be attainable over the fee period: billing boundaries, reset phase (e.g. account-specific weekly reset times), carryover and evidenced throughput limits are recorded, and where windows and billing periods misalign capacity is computed by a small time-indexed linear program (maximise Σ_t y_t subject to the window constraints), or a conservative bound when scheduling feasibility is not established. **Window semantics (E30)**: each window is encoded as recorded in the quota schema — session-anchored windows start at first use; fixed-reset windows at their recorded reset times; sliding windows as ∀t: Σ_{τ∈(t, t+h]} a_wτ y_τ ≤ B_w; unknown semantics use the conservative sliding form for the achievable-capacity schedule only. **Semantics identification (E115)**: where the reset mechanism is not publicly established, the route records a set S_r of publicly admissible mechanisms (semantics_status established / unresolved, admissible_semantics with evidence, lower_bound_scope, upper_bound_scope, capacity_identified_across_semantics, initial-balance and reset-phase domains); the certified bounds must enclose the consumption-optimal, minimum-cash multiple (E105) under every s ∈ S_r — L_r = min over s of the verified lower bounds, U_r = max over s of the relaxed upper bounds — and a point is identified only when they coincide; if a covering upper bound cannot be established, the route reads "semantics unresolved; conservative achievable capacity only", the bound is a published one-sided diagnostic and M_W stays unidentified, so under E106 the displayed multiple remains M_observed (fixtures: sliding and fixed-reset both admissible with different capacities → no point identification; both give the same certified capacity because an independently established bottleneck binds → point identification permitted). Model-specific sub-caps inside a shared pool and model-specific burn coefficients are separate constraints, never borrowed from another model. **Schedule contract (E39)**: time is discretised on a published grid (default 15 minutes) with stated boundary treatment; fixed-phase windows are ordinary time-indexed constraints; session-anchored windows use explicit session-start variables (a session starts at the first consuming step after the previous session ends) or, equivalently, enumeration of admissible session schedules on the grid; where public semantics are insufficient, a documented feasible conservative schedule is used instead of an unconstrained optimum. Every identified capacity result publishes **feasible_lower_bound** (from a verified feasible schedule), **relaxed_upper_bound** (from a valid relaxation of the same public quota semantics), grid_resolution and optimality_scope (E56); an event-aligned grid is used where public reset times permit it; a point capacity is certified only when the two bounds coincide under the recorded semantics, otherwise the interval [lower, upper] is carried into Θ as a structural bound on M_W. A grid optimum is never reported as the target capacity optimum. This λ / time-indexed LP is the **only governing Plan formula** (release tests: loosening a non-binding 5-hour cap cannot change a plan whose weekly cap binds; two bursts one hour apart, each 60% of a 5-hour cap, are infeasible; the same bursts six hours apart are feasible). Harmonic-style category combination is used only where a common additive budget is established; otherwise the observed-mix multiple is kept with its mix-unmatched flag and target-mix capacity is reported as unidentified. Provider quota descriptions may document structure but never supply the numerator. **Capacity target (E69)**: the scored Plan target is **token-equivalent capacity** — API-priced consumption, which is how value is defined on the Plan basis — so a divisible aggregate demand vector is the estimand, not a relaxation of it. Completed-work feasibility is a separate diagnostic: each route records a workload-execution mode (divisible / task-level resumable / task-level non-preemptible); where public task-level records exist, a verified feasible task schedule gives the completed-pass capacity; **same units (E76)** — token-equivalent capacity is first expressed in workload-equivalent passes (API-valued capacity ÷ cost of one deployment-equivalent pass of W) and the completion ratio completed ÷ token-equivalent is shown only when both cover the same complete workload, deployment policy, horizon, funding policy and valuation basis; partial task-level coverage is a named restricted-support diagnostic; otherwise completed-pass capacity is "not identified" and never substituted for the scored target.
- **Dependence.** Every uncertain input records interval meaning (rounding / published CI / observed range / identification bound / analyst stress range), sampling law with justification, and dependency identifier with supporting paired evidence. Costs and outcomes are resampled jointly only when paired task/run observations are public; otherwise shared factors (same price list) are kept and independent / positive / negative dependence stress branches are published; interval outputs use conservative joint bounds.
- **Bug Hunt estimand (E29).** The measurement is the mean of the published runs for the exact model, effort and harness (pooled fixed/105 per complete run; run count recorded; owner-published means and run-level results where public). Run-to-run spread from public repeated runs is measurement evidence; repository differences are a repository sensitivity, not repeats. Under O25 the record is the published mean (a single-run row's record is its single run), so run-to-run spread never enters Θ and, in v1, no scenario stream (E145, E155): it is published beside the leaf as measurement evidence and may enter only a separately named repeatability stream; any analyst repeatability band is sensitivity-only; cohort spread is never used as exact-variant evidence.
- **Provenance and exposure (E57).** Per leaf: dataset origin, execution organisation, harness, grader. Four exposure vectors (share of admitted quality weight by each of these attributes) are computed per release from the admitted evidence, not a static list; "AA-run" and leave-AA-out are defined by **execution organisation**. Task-overlap exposure is reported separately (e.g. Terminal-Bench: independently executed quality leaf, two further AA executions priced in the basket).
- **Versioning.** Methodology version, evidence/benchmark version and release snapshot are separate. A new observed price under an unchanged price rule is a data refresh; changing a rule is a methodology migration; a benchmark-version change triggers a comparability review with dual-run where feasible.

## Governance

Evidence classes (measured / claim / estimate / assumption); provider claims never overwrite independent measurements. Plan evidence carries a 30-day soft expiry with change detection (a plan is flagged "possibly superseded" when scoped change detection — pricing tables, rate-limit documentation sections and model-availability matrices, not full-page diffs — shows a change since measurement, or when ≥ 2 independent end-user reports (O11 independence) describe an undocumented limit change; E18) and a 90-day hard expiry; after a further 30-day grace an expired route is **demoted to diagnostic-only** and the model falls back to API cost (flagged) in the official Plan table (O13), labelled "cross-estimand ordering not identified" and excluded from Plan interval outputs (E36). **Release-time route order (E142)**: every measurement object is classified for eligibility and expiry first; diagnostic-only candidates (including demoted ones) leave the official selection; the E88 dominance filter and the E85/E111/E116 comparator then run over the surviving official-display-eligible route–measurement pairs, and the API-cost fallback of O13 applies only when none survives; the selection is frozen within the release's scenarios, and an eligibility-transition reason ("the evidence expired") is published separately from the comparator's selection reason (fixtures: the selected measurement expires while another measurement of the same route survives; every measurement of the selected route expires while another route survives; only an owner-named transfer whose donor measurement is current survives (E144); nothing survives → API fallback; a past release rebuilt with its original cutoff reproduces its selection). Each row stores its AA price-list date; score changes are decomposed into price change vs token-use change vs **benchmark-record update** (refit, re-anchoring or regrading of a published value with no new task execution; E90) vs method vs cohort, by a declared sequential bridge where effects interact; a component public evidence cannot separate is labelled "benchmark-record update — decomposition unavailable". The scenario engine uses a published RNG seed and draw order per release, and per-stratum outputs are released as data files. Plan rows publish the fee period, the cash paid (subscription fee plus declared additional cash, F_gross) and the selected multiple's API-priced amount M × F_gross so light users can judge fit, typed by the selected measurement object (E166): amount_kind measured-workload capacity / observed eligible consumption, capacity not established / assumed API-equivalent consumption (owner-named transfer, donor named) / identified target-workload capacity (bound to W), with the capacity status (E139), valuation status (E138) and an interpretation label generated from those fields; API-fallback rows publish no Plan amount, and the amount is never called capacity unless the measurement establishes it. **Amount lineage (E168)**: every amount records amount_target (measured-workload consumption / measured-workload capacity / assumed consumption / target-workload capacity), workload_id, measurement_object_id, capacity_solution_id, cash_object_id, purchase_policy_id and derivation (direct observation / solver projection / authorised assumption / valid multiplication). M × F reproduces an amount only within one object: the measurement record U_obs = M_obs × F_obs, or fixed positive cash U_W* = M_W × F_0. A target-workload capacity is the capacity solver's U_W* (bounds are the projections of the joint solution set, never products of separately projected multiple and cash endpoints; the displayed lower-bound multiple is a scoring convention), and is never the displayed multiple times cash from another object; when the target solution's cash lineage is not established, the measured amount stays publishable and no target capacity is manufactured (fixtures: fixed cash reproduces target capacity; consumption-dependent cash rejects cross-object multiplication; set identification by projection; missing target cash lineage). Freshness: AA cost snapshot and price list max age 45 days, with change-triggered refresh; monthly cadence. **Tariff applicability (E141)**: each price record carries observed_at, effective_from, effective_until (only where publicly established), tariff_status (standard / promotional / grandfathered), applicability_conditions, scheduled_refresh_at (the next known effective boundary, refreshed on schedule independently of page diffs) and a source evidence ID; a release uses the prices applicable at its declared valuation timestamp; a promotion with an unknown end keeps the applicable price in the primary ranking, flagged "promotional tariff", with a named S2 standard-tariff branch only where the standard tariff is publicly documented and the consumption ledger supports repricing; an AA note that its cost omits a tariff component (on 2026-10-17: Claude Haiku 5.5's higher long-context pricing is not reflected in AA cost figures) is shown on the row as "AA cost caveat". Unknown boundaries stay unknown (no assumed dates). Each row publishes, per stratum and branch, its **row-level scenario dispersion** (E87; the former per-row "own normalised weighted spread" is dropped as undefined without a panel, E136 — the common-panel weighted spread and this decomposition answer the two questions): Var_s(V_i) = β²Var_s(S_C,i) + (1−β)²Var_s(Q_i) + 2β(1−β)Cov_s(S_C,i, Q_i), with the covariance term published separately and labelled scenario dispersion under the declared kernels (bounds-only rows: "no scenario SD defined"); cohort SD notation is reserved for the within-cohort influence diagnostic. Release gates (on failure the release is **held** — fix the data or re-anchor as a versioned migration with a dual-run comparison; O12): the clip gate defined under Cost, an unversioned AA chart/field change, or any score swing > 5 points without an attributed cause. The registry records, per row: chart field name; total cost and cost per task side by side; repeat inclusion; AA cache protocol and date; price rule (first-party list vs cross-provider median for open weights); fallback-token treatment; Elo anchor identity (e.g. GDPval-AA scale pinned to a named reference model), rating snapshot ID and integration-freeze status and judge-panel/grader versions for rubric-graded leaves. A change of rule in any of these is a methodology migration; a new value under an unchanged rule is a data refresh (see Versioning). Leave-AA-out is reported as "not identifiable under the coverage rule" when the remaining identified quality weight is under 40% (the case today, with ≈20% independent weight), and is excluded from any denominator. **Independent-source checks (E62, split by E66)**: on the same matched panel of rows with both independently executed leaves (Bug Hunt, Terminal-Bench), two named statistics with model-row bootstrap intervals (labelled matched-panel sensitivity): (a) **quality agreement** — Kendall τ_b between the observed-quality ranking and the independent-only quality ranking Q_ind = (z_BugHunt + z_TB)/2; (b) **quality-substitution value agreement** — τ_b between the displayed value ranking and value recomputed with Q_ind in place of quality, cost, anchors, basis and route held fixed (this substitutes quality evidence only; it does not validate costs or Plan multiples). A panel with insufficient rank variation returns "undefined". (c) **Disjoint-evidence agreement (E71)** — τ_b between AA-executed-only quality and independently-executed-only quality on the same panel, with retained leaves and weights published, labelled a restricted evidence comparison (not an eligible ranking); (a) and (b) share inputs with the composite and are described as such. Descriptive only, no scenario share. **Active-rules manifest (E63)**: each release lists every gate, flag and rule with its status (fired / passed / vacuous / unresolved) so readers can see which rules bound.

## Known limitations

Full-utilisation Plan cost overstates value for light users (workday diagnostic shown); AA cost uses AA's token mix and cache protocol; Terminal-Bench work is priced in two executions; Bug Hunt covers two repositories; DeepSWE/Bug Hunt/TB measure model+harness stacks; suite is primarily English text; FrontierCode and ITBench-AA remain pending until complete exact-variant cost evidence covers two-thirds of ranked rows jointly with the admitted components (E95, E101).
