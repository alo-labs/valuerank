## Quality (75% of the value score)

Normalisation on raw magnitudes with frozen, versioned anchors: z = 100 × clip((x − a_low)/(a_high − a_low), 0, 1); a_low = chance/baseline level, a_high = documented ceiling where one exists, else 100%; rounded inputs treated as rounding ranges; unclipped z kept in the data release. **Clipping audit (E110)**: per leaf, the release publishes raw-domain validity (the source metric's documented domain), anchor position (below / inside / above the frozen anchors), the clipping amount, the nominal weight affected and the action — valid saturation beyond an anchor is accepted and disclosed with the clipped rows' identities and raw values; a value outside the metric's documented domain (e.g. a percentage above 100) is a data-validation error that blocks the release; both outcomes appear in the active-rules manifest. Each release publishes the **normalised weighted spread** (w_i × SD(z_i), normalised; formerly "effective weights") and, on a declared common complete panel with fixed coefficients, a signed **covariance allocation** of composite variance, Cov(w_i z_i, Q) / Var(Q) (sums to one; individual terms can be negative; "not estimable on this panel" without the needed variation; no imputation, no rows with different renormalised coefficients; E81), the leaf rank-correlation matrix and the **share of quality weight by data source** (today ≈80% AA-run: DeepSWE, FrontierCode, GDPval, AutomationBench, Omniscience, LCR, HLE, SciCode, CritPt; ≈20% independent: Bug Hunt, Terminal-Bench). LiveBench instruction following is retired (owner decision O26): LiveBench 2.0 (livebench.ai shows "Release 2026-10-14" and, rendered on 2026-10-20, "Last updated 18 October 2026"; the registry pins the source's displayed release and update dates with the capture date, E129 as amended by E175) has no instruction-following suite, the frozen 1.0 release lacks the newest ranked models and AA's IFBench covers 1 of 19; AA-Omniscience takes the reliability group's full 10%. **Drift flag (E114)**: on one declared common complete panel I and the eligible leaf set B* (leaves not marked non-discriminating), d_b = w_b × SD_{i∈I}(z_ib), D_g = Σ_{b∈g∩B*} d_b / Σ_{b∈B*} d_b, compared with the nominal group share renormalised over the same B*; a group is flagged when D_g < 0.5 W_g or D_g > 1.5 W_g; an insufficient panel returns "not estimable (insufficient panel)"; an empty eligible set B* or a zero denominator returns "undefined (no eligible quality dispersion)" with the excluded leaves published and no group flag (E153; the normalised weighted spread follows the same governing rule, Share_b = d_b / Σ_h d_h when Σ_h d_h > 0 and otherwise undefined, and the covariance allocation without composite variance is "not estimable on this panel" — insufficient panel, no eligible leaves and zero dispersion are distinct published statuses; fixtures: an empty eligible set, a constant-score panel); row-specific renormalised coefficients are never combined; the signed covariance allocation (E81) stays a separately named diagnostic; a leaf with cohort SD(z) < 3 points is marked **non-discriminating** (still scored and reported, excluded from drift-flag counting).
