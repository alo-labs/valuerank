- **Matched-cost diagnostic (diagnostic only).** C_i^match = Σ_{g∈G} ω_g P_ig over canonical **execution families** g (E55; e.g. DeepSWE v1.1, FrontierCode, Terminal-Bench as executed by AA): quality-weighted **complete benchmark passes** (E24), with P_ig = cost of one complete deployment-equivalent pass of family g for configuration i (Σ of its accounting subcomponents; published task count × mean cost per attempted task) and frozen diagnostic weights ω_g (Σ ω_g = 1, from the quality weights of the leaves that reference g, fixed with the panel before ranks are examined); a quality leaf references exactly one execution family; model API token charges only (input, cache read/write, reasoning, output × the primary price list; sandbox, environment-setup and verifier compute excluded; E17); where a source cannot separate retry or harness tokens the leaf is not priced for that row; tbench.ai cost columns are never joined. B may include any leaf with exact-variant per-task cost (AA per-evaluation costs, DeepSWE published mean tokens × the same price list, FrontierCode, Terminal-Bench). Published warnings (reference cost C_i,W, never the Index total): (a) pairwise reversal — C^match orders two rows opposite to C_W with both intervals separated (overlap or tie = no reversal); (b) R_i = (C_i^match / C_i,W) / median_{j∈I}(C_j^match / C_j,W) > 1.5 or < 1/1.5 **and** a rank difference on C^match vs C_W of more than 3 places within the common model panel I. Below 40% priced quality weight, or when a row lacks the panel, the cross-check is "unidentified". Cost per solved task is shown alongside. **Diagnostic object (E103)**: the matched-cost diagnostic is its own versioned object D = (family set G, weights ω_g, leaf-to-family map, price rule, panel rule) with an identity hash; a new cost observation under an unchanged D is a data refresh, while a changed family set, mapping or weight vector creates a new D, and reversal warnings are compared across releases only through an old/new bridge on the common evidenced panel that separates cost-data changes from diagnostic-composition changes. These warnings test the API cost shape only; on the Plan basis the workload question is the M_W identification status.
- **Plan cost input (full-utilisation effective cost, labelled)** = API cost input ÷ Value Multiple of the selected route. Computed per model–plan **tier**–surface–configuration route (tier and fee are part of route identity and published). **Measurement objects (E111)**: each controlled measurement and each complete concordant report partition (E107) is a measurement object m = (route ID, partition ID, report IDs, measurement workload, valuation basis, measurement dates, identification object); **pooled-evidence clocks (E151)**: before a partition is pooled, each report is classified by its own clock (its measurement window's end, else its report date; an undated report ages with the route) and by scoped change detection at the release cutoff; reports past expiry and grace leave the partition as an eligibility transition recorded with its reason; the complete surviving partition is then pooled and re-tested for independent-source count and concordance — never a searched-for passing subset among still-eligible reports — and the object publishes its contributing report IDs, the excluded report IDs with reasons, each contributor's observation end and stage, and the partition stage (its most advanced contributor stage, conservative), which governs the object's expiry (fixtures: a demoted report leaves and the remaining pair pools at the partition stage of its older member; expiry that leaves fewer than two reports turns the route's pooled evidence diagnostic); the comparator below selects among route–measurement pairs, so one route with several incomparable measurement objects contributes one candidate per object. The official route is then chosen **lexicographically** among the remaining routes by a machine-readable comparator (E85), published with every route's comparison tuple and a selection-reason field (e.g. "unidentified routes tied on grade; selected by the larger observed multiple") and described as "evidence-priority selected", not as the lowest-cost route: (1) identification class — point-identified > set-identified > unidentified; (2) evidence grade — controlled measurement of the exact model > ≥ 2 concordant end-user reports > owner-named transfer (assumption); (3) the comparison multiple — the point M_W, or the lower bound L of a set-identified M_W; for an unidentified route, its observed multiple M_observed (lower bound where a range is recorded), because the owner's rule divides the Plan basis by the highest eligible Value Multiple (AGENTS.md), labelled "observed-workload multiple — target-workload ordering not identified" (E93); each route records its measurement workload, valuation basis (API price list and date) and fee period, and a measurement priced on another basis enters only after its observed consumption is revalued to the row's valuation basis (E170) from a public token ledger — otherwise it is a price-basis mismatch, unmatched under O7 and diagnostic-only (E123); (4) lower **gross cash** for the fee period under the declared purchase policy; (5) route ID; (6) measurement-object ID (E116) — a canonical identity built from the route ID, partition and report IDs, measurement dates, workload, valuation basis and identification-object version — so the order is total over route–measurement pairs; the selected pair ID is published (fixture: shuffling the admissible-candidate file changes neither the selected pair nor its uncertainty object). The selection is reproducible from the admissible-route file alone; the lower-bound-only choice is published as sensitivity (fixtures: equal grades, point vs set identification, different additional-cash policies, complete ties, the E88 dominance cases, unidentified vs unidentified routes at equal grade — larger observed multiple, then lower gross cash, then route ID — including routes on different valuation bases). Route key = plan × limit-schema version × purchase cohort (e.g. grandfathered pricing) × surface × model × effort; page diffs flag but do not clear a route — a new public measurement does; press round-numbers are never multiples. Plan scores without an identified M_W are kept (O16), flagged "workload-unmatched"; the selected route is **frozen** for the primary ranking (no tier switching inside scenarios). All admissible routes are kept in the release data. **Route-alternative table (E171)**: for every admissible route–measurement pair the release publishes its identification class and evidence grade, measurement workload and valuation status (E138, E170), displayed multiple and the displayed Plan value score the row would receive with it, comparison tuple, dominance or exclusion reason, and whether its comparison with the selected pair is identified on W or is observed-workload arithmetic only; the route-selection sensitivity is the lower-bound-only choice above. **Selection summary (E179)**: each row's table states how many admissible pairs it has (and how many are unidentified) and its leave-selected-out choice — the pair the comparator selects when the selected pair is removed, with its displayed multiple and displayed Plan value score, or "none: the row would fall back to API cost (O13)" when the selected pair is the only admissible one — so the gain from selecting the largest of several unidentified observed multiples is visible beside the selected value. No separate "best" route ranking exists: the owner's highest-eligible Value Multiple rule is comparator key 3, and a larger displayed score in the table is displayed arithmetic, never a claim that the route is cheaper on W where M_W is unidentified. **Full utilisation** means the maximum eligible API-equivalent consumption attainable during the measured fee period under all evidenced quota, funding, throughput and execution constraints and the recorded purchase policy, as computed by the governing Plan capacity formula; the binding constraints and unused non-binding capacity are published (E70). The fee is the amount paid for the measured period (no annual-discount conversion). **Token mix**: where category-specific consumption is evidenced, the multiple comes from the governing Plan capacity formula; otherwise the measurement's category mix is compared with AA's: L1 distance on category shares > 0.30 flags the route "mix-unmatched" (shown, not silently trusted). - **SWE cost companion (diagnostic only, O14)**: c_SWE from published SWE per-task costs; flagged when its ratio to basket cost deviates > 50% from the cohort median. Priced-mix coverage (share of quality weight whose leaves are priced in the basket) is published each release.
- **Admission rule for the official Plan ranking (O11)**: a measured route enters only via a controlled saturation measurement with receipts (e.g. SemiAnalysis) or ≥ 2 independent end-user reports concordant within ±20% (operationally, E98: the largest report is at most 20% above the smallest; reports of 100 and 120 pass, 80 and 120 fail). **Measurement capacity status (E139)**: every measurement object records measurement_capacity_status — capacity established on its measurement workload (a controlled saturation measurement, or a report that documents the binding limits reached) / eligible delivered consumption observed, capacity not established / saturation unverified — with saturation evidence IDs, binding-constraint evidence, fee-period coverage and initial and terminal balance evidence; O11 still admits concordant reports without a saturation requirement, but a report whose capacity is not established is labelled "observed-usage multiple; full utilisation not established" (concordance never upgrades the status), and two questions are published separately per route: was capacity established on the measurement workload, and is it identified on W. With fixed positive gross cash, verified attainable consumption U_att gives the lower bound M* ≥ U_att / F_0; with consumption-dependent payment, E105 governs and U_att / F_att is never promoted to a capacity-multiple bound. Single-source routes are diagnostics. Owner-named transfers (O4/O7) remain labelled assumptions. Each multiple records source count and sampling basis (power users who saturate plans are over-represented). Independence = distinct observers, measurement periods at least 7 days apart and no shared upstream source. **Report comparability (E102)** precedes concordance: reports are pooled only when a public comparability record shows the same fee-period definition and gross-cash accounting, route and limit-schema identity, eligible-consumption treatment (E75/E97), valuation basis (repriced to a common price list where receipts permit: x_k* = eligible usage at the common prices ÷ gross cash for the recorded period) and a compatible measurement workload and saturation target; reports that cannot be reconciled stay separate measurement objects (each may still be a workload-unmatched diagnostic), and contributing report IDs and comparability failures are published. **Report set (E107)**: for each route r and release t, a versioned evidence-selection object E_r,t is built before any concordance test — a pre-registered discovery procedure (sources searched, cutoff date), inclusion rules (O11 independence, E75/E97 eligibility, end-user plan tier and report date within the window) and exclusion reason codes; E102 partitions E_r,t by comparability, and a partition by limit schema is made only where public evidence establishes the schema change. Concordance is tested on every eligible report of a partition together — never on a searched-for passing subset (fixture: comparable reports 100, 110 and 150 fail as a set, so the route is diagnostic-only although the pair 100/110 would pass); a failing partition is diagnostic-only with the disagreement recorded. Source count counts distinct independent measurement objects, not posts or citations. Published: discovery procedure and cutoff, included and excluded report IDs with reasons, schema-partition evidence, the concordance outcome for each complete partition, and which reports determine the displayed measurement. **Concordance is checked on the unpooled comparable reports before pooling** (E33): max_k x_k / min_k x_k ≤ 1.20 (boundary fixture: 100 and 120 pass, 100 and 120.001 fail); concordant reports are then pooled by geometric mean; step-based meter readings are preferred over self-declared totals. Where SemiAnalysis publishes a per-(plan, model) measurement for a model, it replaces any owner-named transfer for that model.
- **Quota schema**: the registry stores each plan's limit-schema version: windows (e.g. 5-hour and weekly) with their **semantics** — session-anchored (starts at first use), fixed reset (calendar or account reset time) or sliding — shared pools across surfaces (e.g. one pool for chat and CLI), pool scope per window (all-models vs per-model), model-specific sub-caps within a shared pool and model-specific consumption (burn) coefficients where measured; how reset balances are treated (counted only if a measurement shows them spendable on the measured workload); and fee decomposition: the **primary multiple uses gross cash**: M = eligible API-priced usage delivered ÷ (subscription cash fee + any cash required for that usage); usage funded by credits bundled into the fee is counted once in the numerator; separately purchased usage is excluded unless its payment is in the denominator; the denominator must be positive and reconcile to receipts. Net-of-credit accounting is a diagnostic only. Each route publishes cash fee, additional cash, included credits and whether consumed, eligible API-valued consumption, and excluded consumption with its funding source. **Funding-aware capacity (E54)**: the route object states a purchase policy — no additional purchases / fixed measured additional cash (the default: full utilisation is conditional on the measured additional cash) / a publicly evidenced consumption-dependent payment rule — and the capacity program uses non-overlapping funding flows (subscription entitlement, bundled credits, purchased usage): each consumed unit uses exactly one funding source, funding-specific balances bound their own flows, and every applicable shared rate limit binds the combined flows. Where public evidence does not establish how payments create feasible capacity, the observed multiple is kept for display (O16) and target-workload transport is unidentified. **Consumption-dependent payments (E80)**: consumption, funding flows and payments are represented jointly; purchases are fixed by the route's declared purchase policy (the optimiser never chooses purchases beyond the declared budget); the program maximises eligible consumption U(y) over the feasible set, evaluates gross cash at the solution, and breaks ties by minimum gross cash; capacity_boundedness, payment_rule, purchase_domain, gross_cash_at_solution and the tie-break are published; unbounded capacity or an unestablished payment relationship leaves target-workload capacity unidentified (display under O16). Maximising the multiple instead of consumption is never substituted. **Multiple certification (E105)**: capacity bounds bound M_W directly only with fixed positive gross cash F_0 (M_W ∈ [U^L/F_0, U^U/F_0]); otherwise the published M_W interval must enclose M*(v) = U*(v) / F*(v) — the multiple at the consumption-optimal, minimum-cash solution — over every admissible evidence state v. With a public deterministic payment function F = f(U), U/f(U) is bounded over a certified domain containing U* (monotonicity used only where established); with schedule- or funding-dependent payment, the consumption optimum and the minimum-cash tie-break are certified, or conservative joint bounds cover all admissible optimal solutions; otherwise M_W stays unidentified (the observed multiple is displayed under O16). Published fields: capacity_optimum_bounds, cash_at_capacity_optimum_bounds, multiple_target, multiple_bounds_derivation, payment_monotonicity_certificate, lexicographic_optimality_status. Fixture: a feasible schedule with U = 10 and F = 1 never certifies M_W ≥ 10 when the consumption optimum is U = 20 at F = 10 (M* = 2). An owner-named transfer between models in different pool scopes or with different burn coefficients carries a directional bias bound in the identification layer; a sub-cap or burn coefficient is never borrowed from another model to identify M_W. A schema change triggers the 30-day soft expiry.
- **Value Multiple** = API-priced usage a plan delivers over a stated fee period ÷ fee for that period. Each multiple records fee period, workload, model/effort, token categories (input, cache read/write, reasoning, output), valuation price list and date, shared usage pools, whether capacity was exhausted and which rate-limit windows bound it. **Valuation compatibility (E138, applying E106 and E123)**: each measurement records its valuation basis — already on the row's valuation basis (E170: the tariff in force at the release valuation timestamp V, or, for a row whose AA cost applied a price not in force at V, that applied price under O29): it continues to the workload-identification checks, and an otherwise eligible workload-unmatched measurement stays scored under O16; revalued from a compatible public consumption ledger: it continues with the revalued multiple; on a different basis that cannot be revalued: diagnostic-only (E106 case 4), supplying neither an official score nor an anchor observation; basis not recorded: displayed with the flag "valuation basis unverified" until the registry records it, never interval-eligible (O7 excludes established mismatches; a missing record is not treated as one). The absence of a token ledger alone never decides price-basis compatibility (fixture: two routes with the same measured multiple, one on the release basis and one on an unreconcilable older basis, receive different official-admission outcomes). Where M_W is unidentified (E32) the row is excluded from Plan interval outputs. No extrapolation of short bursts to a billing period unless the controlled measurement establishes it.
