2. Methodology design recommendations

None beyond the designs already specified in r49.

Against the stated objective—not a broader claim of market-wide optimality or universally applicable production economics—the draft now provides the necessary distinctions:

Separate API and Plan buyer rankings, with an explicit cost–quality exchange rate rather than an undisclosed interpretation of “value.”

Exact-configuration evidence, immutable workload identities and compatible-subset comparisons without filling missing costs.

Deterministic published-record frontier and bar outputs, distinct from convention-dependent scenario frequencies.

Separate tests for Plan measurement eligibility, valuation compatibility, measurement-workload saturation and target-workload identification.

Reproducible evidence bundles, versioned migrations, release gates and explicit unresolved or unevaluable outcomes.

I would retain these contracts rather than add another normalization, uncertainty layer or eligibility rule. The remaining limitations identified above are substantive, but the draft already either bounds their consequences or restricts its claims accordingly. Recommending their removal would reopen adopted decisions without a new argument.
