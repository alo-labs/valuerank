The executable safeguards address the consequential failure modes without manufacturing certainty. In particular, the original constrained evidence model governs over enclosing boxes; witnesses and infeasibility claims have separate proof obligations; unresolved decisions do not become certified results; and omitted competitors prevent an evaluable-panel result from silently extending to the whole selected cohort.

These are reasons not to reopen adopted decisions, rather than requests for more diagnostics or another layer of certification. Adding further machinery without a newly identified decision error would not materially improve the stated objective.

This is a methodology-specification assessment. It does not establish that an implementation passes the registered fixtures, that the complete release evidence bundle reproduces its outputs, or that every captured observation is correct.
