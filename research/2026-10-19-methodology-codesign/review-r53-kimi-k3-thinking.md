2. Methodology design recommendations

None beyond the designs already adopted in r49. I do not find a remaining material change that both improves the stated objective and avoids reopening a settled decision without a new argument.

The reasons for that assessment are:

The API ranking now distinguishes its actual measurement from the broader practical question. Its cost is explicitly contestant-model API token expenditure on a named SDLC basket—not total workflow operating cost. Workload hashes, component admission units, accounting-tree exclusivity, subset-specific calibration and common-panel comparisons prevent materially different cost objects from being presented as interchangeable evidence. The limitations of combining this basket with the quality vector remain visible rather than being resolved through unsupported imputation.
