2. Methodology design recommendations

None beyond the designs already adopted.

The following distinctions are central to that assessment:

The headline score and the frontier answer different questions. The headline implements the owner’s specified cost–quality exchange; the frontier and quality-bar outputs preserve a separate cost-minimisation interpretation. The draft discloses the exchange rate rather than presenting the weighted score as an objective dollar-per-unit-of-quality ratio. These contracts appear in “Outputs,” “Scoring with frozen, per-basis anchors,” and the normative frontier and bar definitions.

Its measured-only view does not confuse measured consumption with identified target-workload capacity. This is a substantive limitation, but already an adopted and prominently specified one—not a new recommendation.

The treatment of superseded tariffs is conservative in the appropriate direction. E170, E183 and E185 retain the required displayed ranking while preventing a superseded-price row from earning an interval label or disappearing as a potentially adverse competitor. The newly priced-category and zero-current-price cases withhold affected claims rather than manufacture finite bounds.

E184 resolves the anchor-migration boundary cases without silently changing the scored workload. Frozen anchors persist; a migration lacking eligible references is deferred; a new methodology version cannot publish its first release without main-workload anchors. API and Plan workload migrations remain atomic.

The uncertainty machinery distinguishes reporting precision, missing-evidence conventions, specification stress and source-interval sensitivity. In particular, S1M’s conventional sampling inside a confidence interval is not represented as a source-derived probability distribution or allowed to authorise the core scenario label.

These are assessments of the specification as written, not verification that an implementation or release bundle satisfies it. An implementation audit would require the generated registry, evidence bundle, active-rules manifest and executable test results; their absence from this review is not itself a defect in the methodology draft.
