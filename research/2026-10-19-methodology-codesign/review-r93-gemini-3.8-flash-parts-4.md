Circular or undefined route inheritance when all measurements of a donor model are demoted or missing

Issue: In Part 5 (Report eligibility profile) and Part 6 (Transfer provenance, GPT routes), transfers and O88 same-model routes inherit their effective figure from the donor configuration's route selected by the comparator. Part 5 specifies: "a lower-of transfer re-takes the lower of its plan's eligible effective figures, a measurement the comparator excludes that no pair replaced dropping out, and with no eligible figure the transfer keeping its stored donor and being diagnostic with it (O95, owner decision O104)". However, in Part 4, comparator key 2 establishes that owner assumptions (transfers) are evaluated in the same comparator tuple. If every measured donor route for a model on a given plan is demoted to diagnostic (e.g. past the 120-day age limit or failing valuation compatibility), Part 6 states that an O88 same-model route "selects the pair itself... follower is dropped" or "takes the pair's figure". If no report pair exists and the donor measurement drops out of official competition, the wording in Part 6 (Transfer provenance) states that "unknown or circular donor references fail the build". Yet under O104, it states it "keeps its stored donor and being diagnostic with it". The build failure gate directly contradicts the diagnostic-fallback rule of O104 when a donor measurement lapses.

Why it hurts the objective: ValueRank releases must build deterministically without unhandled pipeline panics when donor measurements age past the 90+30-day lifecycle window (O13). An unhandled build failure halts release automation instead of cleanly demoting dependent rows to diagnostic status.

Concrete change: Amend the transfer provenance validation in Part 6 to explicitly state: if a donor measurement object transitions to diagnostic status (via O13 expiry, valuation mismatch, or fault demotion without replacement), dependent transfer routes and O88 same-model routes are automatically tagged diagnostic with transition reason donor_diagnostic_lapsed and excluded from the official comparator; the "build failure on unknown or circular donor references" must apply strictly to syntactically unresolved route IDs, missing schema references, or cyclic transfer graphs, not to expired/demoted donor objects.

Ambiguity in multi-token denomination verification for cross-effort O88 routes
