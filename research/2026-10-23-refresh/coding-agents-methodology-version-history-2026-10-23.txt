Version History

Version 2.0

October 2026 - current

Replaced DeepSWE v1.1 with FrontierCode v1.1 from Cognition: 100 software engineering tasks on real open-source repositories, graded on whether a maintainer would merge the change
Renamed SWE-Atlas-QnA to SWE-Atlas-QnA-AA with adjustments to Scale AI's grading: GPT-6 Luna (xhigh) as the judge with five votes per criterion, and 127 rubric criteria reworded; published on Hugging Face

Version 1.5

September 2026 - October 2026

Replaced Terminal-Bench 2.1 with Terminal-Bench 4.0: 66 harder terminal tasks, updated environments and verifiers, and revised compute and time budgets
Upgraded DeepSWE v1.0 to v1.1, retaining the same 113 tasks with updated execution environments and isolated verification of committed patches
Aligned SWE-Atlas-QnA grading with Scale AI's published methodology, using Claude Opus 4.5 as the judge across 124 repository Q&A tasks

Version 1.4

August 2026 - September 2026

Upgraded Terminal-Bench 2.0 to Terminal-Bench 2.1, covering the full 89-task set
Added reward hacking detection aligned with Terminal-Bench's integrity methodology, scoring reward-hacked trials 0
Revised token counting methodology for agents that report reasoning within output tokens

Version 1.3
