# Stage 2 notes: Gemini 4 Carbon on Google consumer plans (2026-10-31)

Extends `../../stage1-google-carbon.md`. Question: was Gemini 4 Carbon available on any Google consumer plan (Google
AI Plus, AI Pro, AI Ultra; Gemini app, Antigravity, Gemini CLI) by 2026-10-31T04:10Z?

## Verdict: not available on any consumer plan (confirmed)

  "Includes Gemini 4 Argon" label). Pro lists "Access to Gemini 4 Argon …", Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini 3 Pro. Ultra
  lists "Higher access to Gemini 4 Argon …". Carbon is not mentioned.
- **Antigravity models page** (antigravity.google/docs/models): Argon (Pro, Ultra, Enterprise), Gemini 3.8 / 3.7 /
  3.6 Flash, 3.1 Pro, Claude Sonnet / Opus 5.5 and 4.6, GPT-OSS-120b. No Carbon. The only date on the page is
  "Will be removed on November 2, 2026" (for three legacy models).
- **Gemini app release notes** (gemini.google/release-notes): the newest entry is 2026.10.13 ("Gemini 4 Argon is
  rolling out globally for Google AI Pro & Ultra subscribers"). There is no Carbon entry.

  and consumers … as soon as possible".
