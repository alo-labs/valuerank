Hello Antigravity team and community,

We subscribe to Google AI Ultra and use the Antigravity CLI (`agy`), including headless mode (`agy -p`) for scripting and CLI workflows.

We ran the exact same three short prompts on a single account and model (`gemini-4-argon-high`), comparing interactive mode against `agy -p`, and recorded `/usage` before and after each run:
- On `gemini-4-argon-high`, interactive mode consumed **26.5% less** of the 5-hour quota than `agy -p` resuming the same conversation (`307 ppm` vs `418 ppm`).
- On a cold start (`agy -p` without a warm session cache), the same three turns consumed **2.5x to 7.2x more** quota than on a warm interactive cache (`1,294 ppm`).

The `/usage` description states: *"Quota is consumed proportionally to the cost of the tokens."* However, there is no disclosure in `/usage` or the CLI documentation that headless (`agy -p`) invocations can drain the 5-hour subscription quota significantly faster for the same work (we have not established a weekly-quota difference).

Could the team clarify:
1. How headless (`agy -p`) runs are counted against the Google AI Ultra 5-hour and weekly subscription quotas compared to interactive sessions?
2. Whether quota credits or billing adjustments are available for subscribers whose weekly quota was exhausted days before reset due to this undisclosed difference?
