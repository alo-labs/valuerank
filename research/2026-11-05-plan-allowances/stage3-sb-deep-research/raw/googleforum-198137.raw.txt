Additional finding: I compared this with my earlier /btw side conversations (about 60 of them, Oct 14 – Nov 3).

Until Nov 3, every /btw prompt was wrapped by the app with:

Since the update (first seen Nov 4, 07:32 UTC), this wrapper is gone: the question is sent as-is and tools are enabled. In the broken conversation, the single-turn guard no longer applied, and the side conversation kept getting resumed by a "Continue" signal: 347 turns from one question.

So the regression seems to be: tool-enabled /btw removed the single-turn limit, but the side conversation still gets auto-continued and has no stop condition.
