Claude Fable 5.2: Launch Offers Include Fable up to 50% of Weekly Limit Until November 16 for Pro and Team Standard Seats, 1–3 "Fable Resets" for Max, Ultra, and Others (Usage and Requirements)

In celebration of the Claude Fable 5.2 release, Pro plans and Team standard seats include Fable up to 50% of their weekly usage limit until November 16, 2026, while Max, Ultra, and Team premium seats receive 1–3 Fable resets depending on subscription tenure. This guide details ineligible plans, how resets work, how to use them, and expiration deadlines based on the Claude Help Center.

Bottom line

Pro and Team Plan Standard Seats Include All Fable Models up to 50% of Weekly Usage Limits at No Extra Charge for One Week Until November 16, 2026; Max, Ultra, Team Premium Seats, and Others Receive 1–3 "Fable Resets" Based on Subscription Tenure

According to the Claude Help Center article "Claude Fable 5.2 launch offers," subscribers to eligible paid plans receive additional Fable usage for a limited time to celebrate the release of Claude Fable 5.2. Plans and seats that include Fable models receive 1–3 Fable resets depending on subscription tenure. (Source: Claude Help Center "Claude Fable 5.2 launch offers")

Offer Details by Plan

Plan Details
Pro plan and Team plan standard seats All Fable models are included for one week up to 50% of the plan's weekly usage limit, plus one Fable limit reset if billing start date requirements are met
Max plan, Ultra plan, Team plan premium seats, and legacy seat-based Enterprise plan premium seats 1–3 Fable limit resets depending on subscription tenure
Plans and seats that use Fable models via usage credits Fable models are included for one week, plus one Fable reset if billing start date requirements are met
  • Both offers begin on November 9, 2026, when Fable 5.2 is released. All Fable models—Fable 5.2, Fable 5.1, and Fable 5—are eligible.
  • Ineligible: Free plan, standard seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans, self-service Enterprise plans, and usage-based Enterprise plans. In addition, Fable resets cannot be used on HIPAA-compliant Enterprise plans.

Number of Resets by Subscription Tenure (Max, Ultra, etc.)

Subscription Tenure Fable Resets
Less than 6 months 1 reset
6–12 months 2 resets
Over 12 months 3 resets
  • Subscription tenure is calculated from the date of the initial payment (or the date of the first App Store payment if subscribed through an app store). If a subscriber canceled and later returned, tenure is still calculated from the initial payment.
  • For Team and Enterprise, tenure is measured from the organization's initial payment, meaning all premium seats within the organization receive the same number of resets.
  • Resets are available to eligible accounts whose paid billing began before 10:00 a.m. Pacific Time on November 9, 2026. For Team and Enterprise seats, the organization's billing must have started and the seat user must have joined the organization by that time. If a subscription that started prior to this date is in a free trial, resets will be granted once it transitions to a paid subscription.
  • The deadline for tenure-based resets provided to Max, Ultra, Team premium seats, and others is 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on March 8, 2027. Unused resets will expire.

How "Fable Resets" Work

  • Weekly limit: Restores the amount consumed by Fable models. Usage consumed by other Claude models remains unchanged.
  • 5-hour limit (if present on the account): Completely refills the 5-hour window, including usage consumed by other models.
  • Example: If 80% of the weekly limit has been used—50% by Fable and 30% by other models—usage returns to 30% of the weekly limit after the reset.
  • Resets do not raise limits. They restore used capacity, allowing users to continue working within their existing limits.
  • Resets cannot be undone once used. If an account is canceled or downgraded before using them, the resets will no longer be available.
  • Resets can be used at any time before the expiration deadline, even before reaching the limit.
  • Because verified Ultra accounts do not have a 5-hour limit, the reset affects only the weekly limit.

One-Week Fable Included Period (Pro and Team Standard Seats)

  • From November 9 to 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on November 16, 2026, all Fable models are available at no extra charge up to 50% of the plan's weekly usage limit.
  • Once that 50% is exhausted or the one week ends, Fable usage will be billed to usage credits if usage credits are turned on. Any unused portion of the 50% does not roll over.
  • The single reset provided in this scenario expires at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on November 16.
  • The default model does not change. To use Fable, select it from the model selector menu.
  • Note: Fable models consume usage limits faster than other Claude models. Users are advised to check their status under "Usage" in "Settings" and use the Fable reset if their Fable allowance runs out before the end of the week.

How to Use a Reset

  1. Open "Usage" in "Settings."
  2. Select "Reset for free" under "Limit resets."
  3. Fable usage is restored immediately.
