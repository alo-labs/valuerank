Frequently asked questions

There are three Google AI plans that users can subscribe to: Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra.

Google AI Plus gives you more access to Google AI, including:

  • Gemini app with access to Gemini 4 Hydrogen and limited access to Gemini Omni Flash5

  • Google Flow with more access to Gemini Omni Flash5

  • Gemini Notebook with more access

  • Gemini in Gmail and more

  • More access to Tasks in Gemini2

  • 400 GB of storage for Google Photos, Drive, and Gmail

  • Other member benefits

Google AI Pro gives you everything in Plus, with expanded access to Google AI, and:

  • Gemini app with access to Gemini 4 Neon and access to Gemini Omni Flash5

  • Google Flow with expanded limits to Gemini Omni Flash5

  • AI Studio with expanded limits to prototype, experiment, and build with our Gemini 4 Neon model and Nano Banana Pro.6

  • YouTube Premium Lite individual7 plan

  • Gemini Notebook with expanded access

  • Google Search:4 Get access to the Gemini 4 Carbon model in AI Mode

  • Google Search: Early access to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode (US only)

  • Google Antigravity: Expanded rate limits and access to Gemini 4 Neon in Google Antigravity, our agentic development platform

  • Google Colab: Get expanded access to powerful accelerators and longer runtimes with 200 compute units per month

  • More Remix generations and Veo 35 photo-to-video generations in Google Photos (US only)

  • Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Vids, and more

  • Google Home Premium Standard: 30 days of event history and Gemini features. Included in all markets where Google Home Premium is available. Learn more

  • Expanded access to Tasks in Gemini2

  • 5 TB of storage for Google Photos, Drive, and Gmail

  • Other premium member benefits

Google AI Ultra gives you a higher level of access to everything in Google AI Pro, as well as:

  • Gemini app with higher access to Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Neon for frontier performance in complex workflows, plus access to Gemini Omni Flash5

  • Google Flow with higher limits to Gemini Omni Flash5

  • AI Studio with higher limits to prototype, experiment, and build with our Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Neon models and Nano Banana Pro.6

  • Project Genie10 is available in more than 140 countries - see full list of countries. Only available with a Google AI Ultra plan.

  • YouTube Premium Family11 plan

  • Google Search:4 Higher access to the Gemini 4 Carbon model in AI Mode

  • Google Antigravity: Higher rate limits and access to Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Neon in Google Antigravity, our agentic development platform

  • Google Colab: Get higher access to powerful accelerators and longer runtimes with 2,000 compute units per month

  • Highest Remix generations and Veo 35 photo-to-video generations in Google Photos (US only)

  • Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Vids, and more

  • Google Home Premium Advanced: 24/7 video history, event descriptions, and more. Included in all markets where Google Home Premium is available. Learn more

  • Higher access to Tasks in Gemini2

  • Cloud storage starting at 20 TB for Google Photos, Drive, and Gmail

  • Other premium member benefits
