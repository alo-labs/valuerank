Antigravity: Gemini 4 Carbon Now Usable on Paid Google AI Pro, Ultra and Enterprise: Plan Table and Selection Guide

Official Google Antigravity documentation, re-read on November 25, 2026, states that Gemini 4 Carbon is available on paid Google AI Pro, Google AI Ultra and Enterprise. The table read on November 13 listed Google AI Pro as not available; the current table differs. It is not available on Free or Google AI Plus. Gemini 4 Argon is noted as removed on November 30, 2026. Based strictly on official documentation, this guide covers the plan table, how to choose, setup, and what is not stated.

Conclusion: Carbon Is Available on Paid AI Pro, AI Ultra, and Enterprise

[Update of November 25] This article was originally written from the official table read on November 13, 2026, which listed Google AI Pro as not available. Re-reading it on November 25, 2026, the table has changed: it now lists Carbon as available on paid Google AI Pro as well. The text below has been corrected to match what was read on November 25.

Google Antigravity's official documentation "Choose a model" includes a compatibility table by plan. The row for Gemini 4 Carbon is as follows:

  • Free & Google AI Plus: Not available (❌)
  • Google AI Pro: Available (✅; †: a paid Google AI Pro plan, or a trial with eligible premium devices)
  • Google AI Ultra: Available (✅)
  • Enterprise: Available (✅)

In other words, Carbon can be selected on a paid Google AI Pro plan. The official sentence reads: "Gemini 4 Carbon is included on paid Google AI Pro plans (and trials with eligible premium devices), Google AI Ultra, and Gemini Enterprise within the shared Gemini quota." The official "Plans" page states that Google AI Pro trials get a lower quota and a more limited set of models (including Gemini 4 Hydrogen) than paid Google AI Pro.

Gemini 4 Neon is also marked ✅ in the same column. Gemini 4 Hydrogen is available on all plans. Gemini 4 Argon is noted as being removed on November 30, 2026.

(Sources: Google Antigravity Docs "Choose a model", Google Antigravity Docs "Plans") There is no update date displayed on this documentation page. This article reflects content re-read on November 25, 2026 (the table read on November 13 listed Google AI Pro as not available).

Plan Compatibility Table (Official Google Documentation)

Model Free / Google AI Plus Google AI Pro Google AI Ultra Enterprise
Gemini 4 Neon / Carbon ❌ ✅ (†) ✅ ✅
Gemini 4 Argon (removed on November 30) ❌ ✅ (†) ✅ ✅
Gemini 4 Hydrogen ✅ ✅ ✅ ✅
Orchestrator (planner selectable) ❌ ✅ (†) ✅ ✅ (‡)
  • †: Available on the paid Google AI Pro plan or through a trial included with eligible premium devices (official note). It states that an application is required to switch to paid Google AI Pro.
  • ‡: In Gemini Enterprise, Orchestrator only runs Carbon (High) as the planner paired with Hydrogen (High) as the executor. Choosing the planner and executor yourself is available only on Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra (official note).
  • "Orchestrator" is a model option where a planner plans and Gemini 4 Hydrogen implements. The planner can be Gemini 4 Neon (default), Gemini 4 Carbon, Gemini 4 Argon, or Claude Opus 5.5 (official table). The table read on November 13 listed Orchestrator (Carbon) as "Google AI Ultra only"; the current table differs.

The same table also lists other models. Claude Sonnet 5.5 (thinking) and Claude Opus 5.5 (thinking) are noted as available on Google AI Pro only with non-trial subscriptions. Gemini 4 Argon, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.6, and GPT-OSS-120b carry a note stating that they will be removed on November 30, 2026 (the table read on November 13 gave "November 18" for Claude 4.6 and GPT-OSS-120b).

Model Selection Guidelines (Google Documentation Explanation)

The documentation designates Orchestrator as the "recommended default" and provides the following guidelines for model usage:

Model to Use When to Use
Orchestrator Most daily development tasks, including feature development, bug fixes, refactoring, and multi-file debugging. Carbon or Argon plans and reviews, while Hydrogen implements faster and with fewer tokens.
Gemini 4 Carbon or Gemini 4 Argon Work requiring maximum reasoning depth at every intermediate step (such as designing complex distributed systems from scratch, untangling subtle concurrency race conditions, or cross-repository migrations). Selecting the model directly also allows choosing the thinking level.
Gemini 4 Hydrogen Fast daily tasks that do not require a planning phase (package updates, cleaning up duplicate code, scaffolding boilerplate code, unit tests for well-defined functions, documentation updates, and simple scripts). Selecting the model directly also allows choosing the thinking level.

During the planning phase, Orchestrator only reads the codebase and makes no modifications. It states that when Hydrogen encounters unexpected issues (such as two failed validations), control returns to the planner. Orchestrator has a fixed configuration, and the thinking level cannot be selected.

How to Choose and Configure (Official Documentation)

  • Antigravity App: Open the model selection menu located below the conversation prompt and select an available model. Orchestrator appears in the same menu; hovering over Orchestrator in the menu lets you override the planner for that conversation only (official description).
  • Antigravity CLI: Select using /model. Passing the model name enclosed in quotation marks at startup is also documented (e.g., agy --model "Orchestrator (Carbon)"). Running agy models lists the models available for your account.
  • Thinking levels for most adjustable Gemini models are Low, Medium, and High. Only Gemini 4 Hydrogen defaults to High and adds a fourth level, "Extra" (xhigh in the CLI).

How to Check Availability on Your Plan

  1. Check your Google AI plan (Free, Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, Google AI Ultra, or Enterprise).
  2. In the table above, locate your plan's column and verify whether the row for the model you want to use has a ✅.
  3. You can verify actual availability by checking whether the model appears in Antigravity's model selection menu. In the CLI, you can display the list using agy models (the method described in the official documentation).
  4. If you are on paid Google AI Pro but Carbon cannot be selected, check whether you are on a trial. The official "Plans" page says trials get a lower quota and a more limited set of models than paid Google AI Pro, and that to get the paid quota and models right away you submit a request to cancel the trial and switch to a paid plan.

Program for Researchers

(Content read on November 13; not re-read on November 25.) Google's November 10, 2026 announcement states that there is an application quota for researchers to use Argon and Carbon free for 6 months at Google Antigravity's highest usage tier. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis and are subject to eligibility criteria, capacity limits, and screening.
