Claude Fable 5.2 on Max and Ultra: how much do you get? We measured it

On Claude Ultra, the weekly Fable 5.2 limit held about $630 of API-equivalent usage: around 17 hours of Claude Code. A week on Opus 5.5 holds $2,494.

November 14, 2026 / Merijn

  • claude-fable

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 on November 9, 2026. On the Max and Ultra plans it doesn’t share the weekly limit freely with the other models. It has a weekly limit of its own, and Anthropic doesn’t say how big that limit is in usage.

So we measured it on our own Claude Ultra account. We read the Fable limit every 30 minutes and matched the readings to Claude Code’s token logs.

The short answer: a full Fable 5.2 limit on Ultra held about $630 of usage at API list prices per week. At this account’s pace that’s about 17 hours of Claude Code. A week on Opus 5.5 holds about $2,494.

How does the Fable limit work?

Anthropic’s help page says you can use “up to 50% of your weekly usage limits on Fable models at no extra cost”. Fable counts toward the normal weekly limit too, and it uses it “faster than other Claude models”.

In practice that gives you two meters. The usage page and /usage in Claude Code show the normal weekly limit plus a separate Fable limit. Only Fable usage moves the Fable meter. When it reaches 100%, Claude Code says “You’re out of usage credits” and offers two ways forward: buy usage credits, which are “billed at standard API rates”, or switch to another model.

One detail is easy to miss. The 50% is a ceiling on the Fable share, not an addition: you “can never use more than your weekly limit”. A full Fable limit uses half your week. Opus 5.5 and the other models get the other half.

How much Fable fits in a week?

On Ultra, about $630 of Fable 5.2 usage at Anthropic’s API list prices ($8 per million input tokens, $40 output, $0.20 cache reads). That’s the measured value. Because the meter shows whole percentages, the range is $560 to $720.

A second method gives almost the same answer. In the days when this account used Fable and Opus side by side, each dollar of Fable moved the normal weekly meter about 2.2 times as far as a dollar of Opus 5.5 (95% interval: 1.2x to 3.0x). Half a week on Ultra is $1,247 of Opus usage. Divided by 2.2, that is about $570 of Fable.

“API-equivalent” means what the same tokens would cost on the Anthropic API at list price. It measures usage, not money you pay. It is also the price you’d pay for Fable after the limit, because usage credits are billed at API rates.

And on Max?

We measured Ultra only. Anthropic says Ultra gets “10 times the Pro plan’s usage every week” and Max five times, and in our earlier measurement the Ultra week was 2.00x the Max week. If the 50% rule works the same way on both plans, a Max Fable limit holds about $315 a week. That’s a calculation, not a measurement.

Fable fills the week about 4.5 times faster per hour

Per dollar, Fable moves the weekly meter about 2.2 times as fast as Opus 5.5. Per hour of work the gap is bigger, because a Fable hour also costs more at list price. At this account’s pace, a typical active hour in Claude Code cost $38 on Fable 5.2 and $16.50 on Opus 5.5.

  • Opus 5.5: a typical hour uses 0.7% of the Ultra week, a heavy hour 2.0%. A full week holds about 150 typical hours.
  • Fable 5.2: a typical hour uses 3.0%, a heavy hour 8.6%. The Fable limit (50% of the week) is gone after about 17 typical hours, or 6 heavy ones.

On Max every number doubles: about 6% of the week per typical Fable hour, and a Fable limit of about 8 hours.

What the launch resets are worth

Anthropic gives existing subscribers free Fable limit resets. On Max and Ultra you get one, two or three, depending on how long you’ve paid: under six months, six to twelve months, or longer. They expire on March 8, 2027, and only accounts with paid billing before November 9, 2026 get them. You use one under Settings > Usage > Limit resets.

A reset gives back the Fable usage you have spent, and leaves the usage of other models where it is. On Ultra that’s one more Fable limit: about $630 of usage per reset. This account had two, and used both in the first week.

On this account the weekly reset day stayed the same after both resets. Use a reset when the Fable meter is full, not before: a reset at 30% gives back only 30%.

Is Fable on a subscription worth it?

At full use, the Fable limit holds about $14 of usage per $1 of plan price per month, on Max and on Ultra. For Opus 5.5 that figure is $54. Per dollar, a subscription gives you much less Fable than Opus. It still beats the API: the same Fable usage costs about $2,700 a month on the API, and on Ultra it fits inside $200, with half of every week left for other models.

What else changed on Max and Ultra

On October 30, 2026, Anthropic renamed its plans. Max 20x is now Ultra, at the same $200 a month. Max 5x is now Max, at $100. Ultra has “no five-hour limit for verified accounts”; Anthropic verifies accounts automatically. Our account lost its 5-hour limit with the rename.

Anthropic now also publishes the weekly ratio: Ultra is 10x Pro per week, Max 5x. That’s the 2.00x we measured in October.

How we measured

  • The Fable limit: Anthropic’s usage endpoint, the source of the usage page and /usage, reports the Fable limit as its own percentage. We read it every 30 minutes for this account. On November 14 it went from 0% to 8% while this account used $50.28 of Fable, so 100% is about $630.
  • Usage: every Fable 5.2 request in Claude Code’s local logs, priced at $8 per million input tokens, $40 output, $0.20 cache reads, and 1.25x or 2x the input price for 5-minute or 1-hour cache writes.
  • Cross-check: a regression of the normal weekly meter on Fable and non-Fable usage, per meter interval, October 23 to November 13 (370 intervals, $368 of them Fable), using CodexBar’s hourly readings. The Opus 5.5 weekly limit of $2,494 comes from our September and October measurement.
  • Pace: API-equivalent usage per clock hour with more than $5 of usage, October 19 to November 14: 37 Fable hours and 155 hours on other models. Scripted runs without a transcript are left out of the pace.

Limits of this measurement:

  • It’s early. The direct reading spans one day and eight percentage points, so the range is wide.
  • One account, one person’s coding work. Your hours depend on your pace.
  • Max isn’t measured. Its numbers assume the 50% rule and the 2x weekly ratio hold.
  • On November 13, after a login error, some Fable sessions on this machine billed to a second account. That day is left out.
  • Anthropic can change limits at any time. These numbers describe November 2026.

FAQ

How much Fable 5.2 do you get on Claude Ultra?

About $630 of usage at API list prices per week, measured in November 2026, with a range of $560 to $720. That’s half the weekly limit, and about 17 hours of typical Claude Code use.

How much Fable 5.2 do you get on Claude Max?

We estimate about $315 a week, half of Ultra, or about 8 hours of Claude Code. We didn’t measure Max directly.

Does Fable 5.2 use the limit faster than Opus 5.5?

Yes. Per dollar at list price, it moves the weekly meter about 2.2 times as far. Per hour of work it’s about 4.5 times: 3.0% of the Ultra week in a typical Fable hour against 0.7% on Opus 5.5.

What happens when you hit the Fable limit?

Claude Code stops Fable requests. You can buy usage credits, billed at API rates, or switch to another model. The rest of your weekly limit stays available for the other models.

What is a Fable limit reset?

A launch offer that gives back the Fable usage you’ve spent. Max and Ultra subscribers get one to three, depending on how long they’ve paid, to use before March 8, 2027. On Ultra one reset is worth about $630 of usage.

Does Claude Ultra still have a 5-hour limit?

Not on verified accounts. Since October 30, 2026, Ultra has only weekly limits once Anthropic has verified the account. Max still has a 5-hour limit.
