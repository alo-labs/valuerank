Team Subscription

Token Plan Team Edition is an AI programming subscription solution tailored for development teams and enterprises. In addition to inheriting all the benefits of the individual edition, it provides capabilities such as member management, seat allocation, team usage statistics, and centralized billing management. The team owner purchases packages and allocates seats in a unified manner, and members can call the MiMo model in mainstream AI programming and agent tools via their exclusive API Keys.

Core Strengths

  • First access to the latest flagship models and features: All plans support six models: mimo-v2.6-pro, mimo-v2.6-flash, mimo-v2.5-asr, mimo-v2.5-tts-voiceclone, mimo-v2.5-tts-voicedesign, and mimo-v2.5-tts. Pro and Max also support mimo-v2.6-pro-ultraspeed. Token-based credit conversion keeps usage transparent and manageable.

  • Unified Space Seat Management: Centralize the management of team members, and allocate, reclaim and purchase additional seats as needed to flexibly adapt to team changes.

  • Team usage is transparent and controllable: It is metered independently per seat, supports viewing usage by model and member, so you can clearly grasp the consumption of team quota.

  • Centralized Billing and Invoice Management: View records of plan purchases, renewals and additional seat purchases in a unified manner, apply for invoices centrally to streamline financial management.

  • Flexible Subscription Plans: Three tiered packages including Standard, Pro and Max, supporting monthly auto-renewal, annual auto-renewal and one-time purchase, to meet the needs of teams of different sizes and usage intensities.

  • Inherit all the benefits of the personal version: Multi-ecosystem Out Of The Box, enjoy night-time discounts, no 5-hour and weekly usage restrictions.

Quota Consumption Rules

Language models deduct Credits quota based on the number of tokens; ASR models deduct Credits quota based on the duration of the input audio (the duration is counted to the nearest second and converted to hourly billing); TTS series models are free for a limited time and do not consume package Credits. The Team version is measured independently per seat. When an already consumed seat is assigned, the new member will inherit the remaining quota of this seat for the current cycle, and the already consumed portion will not be reset.

  • Quota Exhaustion: Once the monthly quota of a seat is used up, the seat will be suspended, without affecting other seats that still have remaining quota. A team-shared usage package is coming soon, stay tuned.

  • To continue using the service: you may contact the team owner to purchase additional seats as needed and reassign them, or switch to the pay-as-you-go standard API service to keep using it.

Package Purchase

  • Discounts: Enjoy an approximate 12% discount for annual auto-renewal plans or one-time purchases valid for 1 full year, plus 20% lower resource consumption during nighttime hours

    • Annual Discount: Annual recurring subscription offers an approximate 12% discount compared to the monthly rate, and one-time purchases of 12 months also qualify for the annual package price. No first-purchase discount is available for the Team Plan.

    • Off-peak Night Rate: A consumption coefficient of 0.8x applies during off-peak hours (00:00–08:00 Beijing Time, 16:00–24:00 UTC).

  • Flexible purchasing options: We support continuous monthly subscription, continuous annual subscription and one-time purchase, with a minimum order of 2 seats. One-time purchases can be valid for 1 to 12 months, catering to the usage needs of teams across different cycles.

  • Support for adding seats mid-subscription: The owner may add seats of the same tier during the validity period of the current plan, with a minimum of 1 seat per addition. The fee and current quota for the newly added seats will be calculated based on the remaining validity period, and their expiration date will align with that of the current plan.

  • Auto-renewal supported: Auto-renewal is available for monthly and annual subscriptions, and the subscriber can turn it off at any time.

  • Invoice Support: Domestic users can apply for invoices based on the transaction orders in their recharge details, and the actual invoiceable amount is the actual payment amount. Overseas users can directly download the invoice after purchase, or download it on the recharge details page.

Precautions

  • Multiple members are not supported to share the same seat

  • Upgrades and mixed purchase of package tiers are temporarily not supported during the validity period. If you need to increase the available resources, you can purchase additional seats for the current tier as needed.

  • Unsubscription and refund for individual or partial seats are not supported. No refund will be provided for unused quotas within the package. Please carefully select the appropriate subscription plan and number of seats based on your own usage needs.

Package Usage

The quota of the Token Plan package can only be used in programming tools (such as OpenClaw, OpenCode, etc.), and it is prohibited to use it in the form of API calls for request behaviors in obvious non-coding scenarios such as automated scripts and custom application backends.

If calls beyond the permitted scope are made using the API Key corresponding to the package, such act will be deemed as a violation or abuse, and the platform reserves the right to take measures including suspending services for the relevant subscription and banning the API Key.

Quick Guide

Get started quickly with Token Plan, from subscribing to a package to using the MiMo model in coding tools.

Subscribe to the Token Plan

Visit Token Plan, then select and purchase the appropriate Team Edition subscription package as needed.

Invite members and assign seats

After successful subscription, you can invite members in the Team Edition TokenPlan Console and assign available seats before you can use the package. Of course, you can also assign a seat to yourself.

Obtain the package-specific Base URL and API Key

After members successfully access the team workspace and obtain the seats assigned by the owner, they can acquire the package-specific Base URL and API Key within the team workspace (the owner should enter "My Seats").

  • API Key: In the team workspace, obtain your dedicated API Key (in the format of ttp-xxxxx).

  • Base URL: You need to configure one of the following Base URLs in the AI programming tool subsequently (The protocol varies by tool, and the Base URL shall be subject to what is displayed on the Team Edition TokenPlan page). For specific operations, please refer to the corresponding user guide document of the AI programming tool.

    • OpenAI Compatible Protocol

      • China Cluster: https://token-plan-cn.xiaomimimo.com /v1

      • Singapore Cluster: https://token-plan-sgp.xiaomimimo.com /v1

      • European Cluster: https://token-plan-ams.xiaomimimo.com /v1

    • Anthropic Compatibility Protocol

      • China Cluster: https://token-plan-cn.xiaomimimo.com /anthropic

      • Singapore Cluster: https://token-plan-sgp.xiaomimimo.com /anthropic

      • European Cluster: https://token-plan-ams.xiaomimimo.com /anthropic

Precautions

  • Please keep your API Key safe and do not disclose it to others.

  • The API Key is only valid during the validity period of the Token Plan subscription you have purchased.

Used in AI Agents and programming tools

The Token Plan is supported for use in multiple mainstream AI programming tools, and all tools share the usage quota of the subscribed plan.

Go to AI Tools Overview to view the configuration guide corresponding to the tools you are using (such as OpenCode, OpenClaw, etc.).
