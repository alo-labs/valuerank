Coverage and interpretation

The TB4 snapshot overlaps 11 of the 21 current ValueRank models. The missing cohort rows are: Kimi K3, GPT-5.5, GLM-5.3 Flash, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Qwen3.8 Max, Muse Spark 1.2, DeepSeek V4 Flash, Gemini 3.6 Flash, GLM-5.2, Gemini 3.5 Flash.
